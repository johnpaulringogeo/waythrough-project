About These Checklists

Every checklist is free to download and print, with no registration. They're formatted to print cleanly on US Letter paper.

Give them to clients, tenants, or anyone looking for housing help. They're informational only, not legal advice; for your specific situation, talk to an attorney.

Using these checklists with clients? Print, copy, and hand them out as much as you want. No attribution needed, but we'd like to hear how they're working for your clients.
