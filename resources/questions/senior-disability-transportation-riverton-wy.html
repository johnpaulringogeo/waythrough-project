Reader Question

Is there transportation for seniors and people with disabilities in Riverton, Wyoming?

Asked September 2026 · Answered by Matt · Updated November 4, 2026

The question

“Is there transportation for seniors and people with disabilities in Riverton, Wyoming?”

Sent through our Ask a Question form. We removed names, places and other details so no one can be identified.

The short answer

Yes. The Wind River Transportation Authority (WRTA) runs buses and curb-to-curb on-demand rides in Riverton, and it has said it will keep giving free rides to and from the Riverton Senior Center. If you have Wyoming Medicaid, it can also help with trips to medical appointments.

Wind River Transportation Authority (WRTA)

WRTA is Fremont County's public transit, run by the Fremont County Association of Governments. It offers fixed bus routes, WRTA On-Demand rides and a SafeRide service. In 2025, WRTA, the Riverton Senior Citizens Center and the Wyoming Department of Transportation announced they were combining transit in Riverton. The senior center's rides had mainly served older adults and people with disabilities. WRTA said it would keep curb-to-curb demand-response service in Riverton and keep giving free rides to and from the Riverton Senior Center.

  • Phone: (307) 856-WRTA, which is (307) 856-9782. Monday to Friday, 6 a.m. to 7:30 p.m.; closed weekends.
  • Website: fcagwy.gov/wrta. On-Demand fares can be paid in the Token Transit app.

WRTA warned in 2026 that funding cuts could reduce some routes and hours. Call to confirm what's running on the days you need, and ask about any senior, veteran or disability fares.

Riverton Senior Citizens Center

Wyoming's Aging Division lists the senior center at 303 East Lincoln, (307) 856-6332, as a provider of support services and meals for older adults. Ask about rides to its programs and help booking WRTA trips. For help finding senior services anywhere in Wyoming, call the Aging Division's Community Living Section at 1-800-442-2766.

Wyoming Medicaid travel help

If you have Wyoming Medicaid, its Travel Assistance benefit can help you get to medically necessary appointments with Medicaid providers. Under the state's member guide, trips in your own town or county may be covered with a public transit punch card. For appointments in another town, Medicaid may pay mileage or arrange a bus, taxi or shuttle. Call the Medicaid Customer Service Center at 1-855-294-2127, Monday to Friday, 7 a.m. to 6 p.m., and choose Travel Assistance. Ask before your appointment when you can, and keep proof that you went.

Help for people with disabilities

Wyoming Services for Independent Living, based in Lander, serves Fremont County. Ask whether it has a ride or travel help program you can use.

What to do now

  1. Call WRTA and ask what rides you can use, how to book and what they cost.
  2. Call the senior center about rides to meals and activities.
  3. If you have Medicaid, call 1-855-294-2127 before your next medical appointment.
  4. If you still can't find a ride, call 211 for other local options.

What to say

"I'm a senior in Riverton and I don't drive. What rides can I use for errands and doctor visits, how far ahead do I need to book, and is there a senior or disability fare?"

Riverton doesn't have its own housing authority; Section 8 there is run by the Cheyenne Housing Authority's Lander/Riverton office. See our Riverton housing page.
