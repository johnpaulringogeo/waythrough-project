The Buffalo Municipal Housing Authority (BMHA) runs public housing in the City of Buffalo and a Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8) program. HUD's PHA contact report lists it as NY002 (Combined) and its voucher program as NY449, both at 300 Perry Street, Buffalo, NY 14204, (716) 855-6711. BMHA calls itself "the City of Buffalo's largest landlord and only public housing authority." The short version today: BMHA says its voucher waitlist is closed and it expects to reopen it "sometime in 2026," while its public housing application is posted and taken by email, mail or drop-off. BMHA is not the only voucher door in Buffalo: the Rental Assistance Corporation of Buffalo runs the City of Buffalo's Section 8 program, and Belmont Housing Resources runs New York State's vouchers in Erie County. This page covers BMHA's exact words, how to apply, the phone numbers, the other two voucher agencies, and where to turn if you need help with rent now.

Waiting-List Status

Waiting lists open and close often. The status below is what we found the last time we checked; confirm it on the official site before you count on it.

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8): Closed. Checked November 12, 2026: BMHA's voucher page says "The Waitlist for the Housing Choice Voucher Program is currently closed. We expect to reopen applications sometime in 2026." It also says "Future wait list openings will be advertised through local media and our social media accounts." No opening date is posted. Some listing sites claim BMHA opened its voucher list earlier in 2026; we found nothing on BMHA's own site to back that up.
  • Public housing: Taking applications (no open or closed wording posted). BMHA's Public Housing Program page posts a fillable application and says completed applications "can be emailed directly to the BMHA Housing Assistance Office at 245elmwood@bmhahousing.com" or mailed or dropped off at 245 Elmwood Avenue, Buffalo, NY 14222, Monday to Friday, 8:30 am to 4:30 pm. Paper applications in English and Spanish are at that office and at 300 Perry Street. BMHA does not say whether individual development lists are open or closed. Its FAQ says "The wait can be from 30 days to several months or years" and that there are wait lists at many developments, "especially for larger family units and non-elderly single apartments." 2026 income limits: $56,850 for one person, $81,200 for four (checked November 12, 2026).

Programs BMHA Runs

  • Public housing: family, senior and disabled developments across the city, from studios to 5 bedrooms, applied for with one BMHA housing application
  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8); the waiting list is closed. BMHA says its vouchers "can be used to lease apartments in the City of Buffalo and throughout Erie County."
  • Housing Choice Voucher office for current voucher holders and landlords: 476 Louisiana Street, Monday to Friday 8:30 am to 4:30 pm, 716-855-6711 (press 8)
  • Housing Assistance Office (public housing applications): 245 Elmwood Avenue, Monday to Friday 8:30 am to 4:30 pm, 716-855-6711, ext. 500 or 501

How to Apply

Apply on the authority's own application page: www.bmhahousing.com/219/Public-Housing-Program. If you have already applied for public housing, check your status with the "APPLICANT LOGIN" on the BMHA portal; for portal problems, email portalhelp@bmhahousing.com. Voucher holders and voucher applicants can call the Housing Choice Voucher office at 716-855-6711 (press 8). Keep your mailing address, phone and email current with BMHA. If BMHA cannot reach you when your name comes up, you can lose your place.

If this is your first time applying, our step-by-step guide to applying for Section 8 covers each stage, and how to find your PHA shows which authority covers where you live.

Other Authorities Serving This Area

  • Rental Assistance Corporation of Buffalo (RACB): HUD's PHA contact report lists RACB as NY409, "Section 8 City of Buffalo," 470 Franklin Street, Buffalo, NY 14202, (716) 882-0063. RACB says its list takes online applications from 8:00 am Monday, November 30, 2026 until 4:00 pm Friday, December 18, 2026, with spots assigned by lottery after it closes (checked November 12, 2026 at racbny.org/waiting-list). If that window has passed, check racbny.org for the next one.
  • Belmont Housing Resources for WNY, 2393 Main Street, Buffalo, NY 14214, (716) 884-7791, runs New York State's (HCR) Housing Choice Vouchers for the Erie County Consortium. On November 12, 2026 its page said: "Currently the waitlist for Erie County Consortium and the Niagara County program are closed and not accepting application." A September 16 to 18, 2026 opening took Family Unification Program referrals only. Its City of North Tonawanda program is taking applications, with a preference for people who live or work in North Tonawanda. Status line: 716-884-2358, ext. 307.
  • BMHA does not run shelter. Its FAQ answers "Does the BMHA provide emergency housing?" with "No." If you are homeless or about to be, call Erie County DSS at (716) 858-8000 during business hours, or 211 WNY at 1-888-696-9211 after 5 pm.
  • BMHA is not an emergency rent fund. If you are behind on rent, see our Buffalo emergency rent help guide: Erie County DSS Emergency Services, Gerard Place, the WNY Eviction Prevention Project, 211 WNY, HEAP and New York's eviction deadlines. If you already live in BMHA housing or have a BMHA voucher and fall behind, call BMHA at 716-855-6711 right away.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Buffalo Section 8 waiting list open?

BMHA's is not. As of November 12, 2026, BMHA says its Housing Choice Voucher waitlist is closed and that it expects to reopen applications sometime in 2026; openings will be advertised in local media and on its social media. Belmont's Erie County Consortium list is closed too. The Rental Assistance Corporation of Buffalo's list takes online applications from November 30 to December 18, 2026 (8:00 am to 4:00 pm); if that window has passed, check racbny.org or call (716) 882-0063.

How do I apply for BMHA public housing?

Download the fillable BMHA housing application from bmhahousing.com and email it to 245elmwood@bmhahousing.com, or mail or drop it off at the Housing Assistance Office, 245 Elmwood Avenue, Buffalo, NY 14222, Monday to Friday, 8:30 am to 4:30 pm. Paper applications in English and Spanish are also at 300 Perry Street. Questions: 716-855-6711, ext. 500 or 501.

How long is the wait for BMHA public housing?

BMHA says the wait can be from 30 days to several months or years, and that lists are longest for larger family units and non-elderly single apartments. Applying to more developments can shorten your wait.

How do I check my BMHA application status?

Use the APPLICANT LOGIN on the BMHA portal at bmhaportal.bmhahousing.com. If you have trouble with the portal, email portalhelp@bmhahousing.com. Voucher holders can call the Housing Choice Voucher office at 716-855-6711 and press 8.

Who else gives out Section 8 vouchers in Buffalo?

The Rental Assistance Corporation of Buffalo runs the City of Buffalo's Section 8 program (470 Franklin Street, (716) 882-0063), and Belmont Housing Resources for WNY runs New York State's vouchers in Erie County (2393 Main Street, (716) 884-7791). Each has its own list, so apply to every one that is open.

What does a Section 8 tenant pay in Buffalo?

Under HUD rules, a family with a voucher or in public housing usually pays about 30% of its adjusted monthly income toward rent and utilities, and the housing authority pays the rest of an approved rent. BMHA's voucher page says rent for voucher holders cannot exceed 30% of their monthly income.
