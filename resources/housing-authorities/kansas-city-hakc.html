Housing Authority of Kansas City, Missouri (HAKC)

Serves Kansas City, Missouri and Jackson County, with public housing in Jackson, Clay and Platte counties · Last checked November 3, 2026

Waythrough is an independent resource, not HAKC. We summarize public information to help you get oriented. For applications and the most current waiting-list status, always use the official links below.

Quick Facts

Serves: Kansas City, Missouri and Jackson County, with public housing in Jackson, Clay and Platte counties
Phone: (816) 968-4100
Official site: www.hakc.org/
Apply: HAKC application page
Status last checked: November 3, 2026

The Housing Authority of Kansas City, Missouri (HAKC), established in 1941, is the public housing agency for Kansas City on the Missouri side of the state line. HUD lists it as PHA MO002 with a program type of Combined, so it runs both Housing Choice Vouchers and public housing, and HAKC says it manages 10,000 vouchers and "serves Jackson County along with the Kansas City, Missouri Metropolitan Area." Its office is at 3822 Summit St., Kansas City, Missouri 64111, open Monday to Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. It is a different agency from the Kansas City, Kansas Housing Authority across the state line. The short version today: HAKC's website does not say whether any of its waiting lists is open or closed. What it does publish is one online application, on the Bob.ai platform, that adds you to all of its voucher lists at once, and a second one that adds you to all of its public housing lists, with weekly emails about your place on each. This page covers HAKC's exact words, how its lists and preferences work, the phone numbers, and the other housing authorities in the metro.

Waiting-List Status

Waiting lists open and close frequently, so treat the status below as a starting point and confirm on the official site before you rely on it.

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8) and Project-Based Vouchers: No open-or-closed status posted. Checked November 3, 2026: no page on hakc.org says the voucher or project-based lists are open or closed, and we found no opening or closing notice among HAKC's 2026 news posts. HAKC's Apply Online page says: "We have one common application for all our voucher programs, including Project Based Vouchers. We maintain a separate waitlist for programs and our 18 Project-Based Voucher Properties. You submit one application and will be automatically added to 19 waitlists." The application link opens a Bob.ai sign-up page that shows no list status before you make an account. HAKC's Administrative Plan says HAKC will close the list when the estimated wait for the newest applicants "reaches 24 months" and will announce any reopening "at least 10 business days prior" in local media. Its How to Apply page says "The average wait for voucher assistance is 3 years."
  • Public Housing: No open-or-closed status posted. HAKC's Apply Online page says: "We have one common application for our 11 properties. You apply once and are automatically added to 11 waitlists." Its Tenant Selection FAQ says the public housing wait "depends on the bedroom size needed. The average waiting time is 6 to 18 months." Paper applications can be picked up at the office and returned to the drop box in the lobby. HAKC says: "Every Sunday night, we run our waitlists and send an email with your current position on all the waitlists you're a part of." When HAKC invites you to send a full application, it expects it within two weeks; after a reminder letter, another two weeks without it removes you "from all of our waitlists" (checked November 3, 2026).

Programs HAKC Runs

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8), about 10,000 vouchers
  • Project-Based Vouchers at 18 properties, all on HAKC's one voucher application
  • Public housing at 11 properties in Jackson, Clay and Platte counties; HAKC's homepage counts 1,722 units and its Public Housing page says "over 1,900"
  • Section 8 Homeownership Program for voucher holders
  • Family Self-Sufficiency, youth, adult and senior programs through Resident Services

How to Apply

Apply directly through the authority's official application page: www.hakc.org/apply-online. If you have already applied, call (816) 968-4100 to verify your position and keep your contact information current. If the authority cannot reach you when your name comes up, you can be dropped from the list.

New to the process? Our step-by-step guide to applying for Section 8 walks through the whole thing, and how to find your PHA helps you locate the right authority.

Other Authorities Serving This Area

  • Kansas City, Kansas Housing Authority (KCKHA) is a separate agency across the state line, HUD code KS001, program type Combined, at 1124 N. 9th Street, Kansas City, KS 66101, (913) 281-3300 (KCKHA's homepage and HUD's Kansas list). Its voucher page said on November 3, 2026: "Currently KCKHA is not accepting applications for the HCV/Section 8 Program. When the wait list opens, announcements will appear on our website." Its public housing page takes wait-list applications online, with a separate form for its Bonner Springs properties. KCKHA's own pages give (913) 279-3473 for its voucher office and (913) 279-3441 for Resident Selection.
  • Johnson County Housing Authority, HUD code KS162, serves Johnson County, Kansas, from 12425 W. 87th St. Pkwy., Lenexa, (913) 715-6600 (HUD's Kansas list).
  • On the Missouri side, the Housing Authority of the City of Independence (HUD code MO017, (816) 836-9200) and the Lee's Summit Housing Authority (HUD code MO030, (816) 524-1100) run their own lists; both numbers are from HUD's Missouri list. HUD's Missouri list has no separate housing authority for Jackson, Clay or Platte County.
  • HAKC is not an emergency rent fund. If you are behind on rent, see our Kansas City emergency rent help guide: free eviction lawyers through the City's Right to Counsel program, United Way 211, the City's rent-help providers and Missouri's eviction deadlines. If you already have HAKC housing or a voucher and fall behind, call HAKC at (816) 968-4100.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Kansas City, Missouri Section 8 waiting list open?

HAKC does not say. As of November 3, 2026, no page on hakc.org states that its voucher, project-based or public housing lists are open or closed, so treat any other website that says one way or the other as unconfirmed. What HAKC does show is an Apply Online page with one common application for its voucher programs and one for public housing, on the Bob.ai platform. HAKC's Administrative Plan says a reopening is announced in local media at least 10 business days ahead. If you are not sure your application will be taken, call HAKC at (816) 968-4100.

How do I apply to HAKC?

Online, from HAKC's Apply Online page, which sends you to Bob.ai to make an account, or on paper at the office at 3822 Summit St., returned to the drop box in the lobby. You need an email address, and HAKC strongly suggests a cell phone number that can receive texts. HAKC says to apply you will need a birth certificate, a Social Security card and a valid form of ID. You can apply for both programs: HAKC says once you apply for one, you will be prompted to apply for the other, and each program keeps its own list, so you can stay on one even after the other houses you.

How long is the wait for HAKC?

HAKC gives more than one answer. Its How to Apply page says "The average wait for voucher assistance is 3 years." Its Tenant Selection FAQ says the voucher wait "averages 1-3 years" and that public housing averages 6 to 18 months depending on bedroom size. Its Administrative Plan says HAKC will close the voucher list once the estimated wait for the newest applicants reaches 24 months. None of these is a promise, and the Section 8 program is not built for emergencies.

Who gets priority on HAKC's lists?

For vouchers, HAKC's Administrative Plan gives Preference 1 to veterans with an honorable discharge and to homeless households; everyone else is ranked by date and time of application. It also adds a preference for families moving from Emergency Housing Vouchers into regular vouchers as that program winds down. For public housing, HAKC's Public Housing page lists, in order: working, work training program, elderly and disabled; then homeless, job training program, housing factor and rent burden; then non-preference, with applicants ranked by date and time within each group. Bring proof of the preference you claim to your interview.

I live in Kansas City, Kansas. Which housing authority do I apply to?

The Kansas side has its own agency, the Kansas City, Kansas Housing Authority (HUD code KS001, (913) 281-3300), and on November 3, 2026 its voucher page said KCKHA is not accepting applications for the HCV/Section 8 Program. KCKHA's public housing wait list does take applications online. HAKC, on the Missouri side, says on its Public Housing page that "Anyone seeking housing assistance in the Kansas City, Missouri metropolitan area is eligible to apply for housing assistance," so you can also apply to HAKC. In Johnson County, the Johnson County Housing Authority in Lenexa runs a separate program.

How do I check my place on the list or update my address?

Log in to Bob.ai; HAKC runs its lists every Sunday night and emails your position on each one. To change your address, HAKC says to tell it in person or in writing: fill out a change of address form at 3822 Summit St., or fax its Change of Information form to (816) 968-4123. Changing your address with the post office does not change it with HAKC. HAKC's main number is (816) 968-4100, at the top and bottom of every HAKC page, on its Help Center page and in HUD's directory for MO002; for public housing applications, HAKC gives option 3 for the Tenant Selection Department. TTY users can call (800) 735-2966 or 711.
