The Colorado Springs Housing Authority (CSHA) is the public housing agency for Colorado Springs. HUD lists it under its legal name, the Housing Authority of the City of Colorado Springs, as PHA CO028 with a program type of Combined, so it runs both Housing Choice Vouchers and public housing. CSHA says it owns and manages 706 single-family and multi-family homes, and its main office is at 831 S. Nevada Ave., Floor 2. The short version today: nothing is open. CSHA's homepage says "None of our waiting lists are currently accepting online only pre-applications" and "The current directive from HUD to pause the issuance of new Housing Choice Vouchers is, unfortunately, still in effect. We will announce when voucher issuance resumes" (checked November 3, 2026). CSHA's voucher list is a lottery that it says "usually opens every July," but no 2026 lottery was announced; the latest one ran July 15 to 17, 2025. Its 2026 openings were all short public housing windows, the last of which closed at noon on October 30, 2026. This page covers CSHA's exact words, how its lottery and public housing lists work, how to check your ranking, and the other agencies around El Paso County that people confuse with CSHA.

Waiting-List Status

Waiting lists open and close frequently, so treat the status below as a starting point and confirm on the official site before you rely on it.

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8) lottery wait list: Closed; no 2026 lottery announced, and HUD's pause on new vouchers is still in effect. Checked November 3, 2026. CSHA's homepage reads: "None of our waiting lists are currently accepting online only pre-applications" and "The current directive from HUD to pause the issuance of new Housing Choice Vouchers is, unfortunately, still in effect." CSHA's Apply or Check Status page says "Our only wait list that opens on a regular basis is our annual HCV/Section 8 Lottery Wait List which usually opens every July," but its Wait List Opening Announcements page lists no 2026 voucher lottery. The most recent, the 2025 HCV Lottery, took online pre-applications only from noon on Tuesday, July 15, 2025 to noon on Thursday, July 17, 2025. That notice says every pre-application "will be randomly assigned a lottery number for selection when there are available vouchers," and "At the end of each lottery year, any remaining applications will be removed, and families must reapply when the waiting list reopens." CSHA gives no date for the next lottery.
  • Public Housing (0 to 4 bedrooms): Closed; the last 2026 window closed at noon on October 30, 2026. CSHA opened public housing lists in short online windows in 2026, and all of them are over: 1-bedroom elderly and disabled plus 2-bedroom units from noon on April 1 to noon on April 3, 2026; 1-bedroom elderly and disabled units at Franklin Square Apartments and Southview Plaza Apartments from noon on September 2 to noon on September 4, 2026; and 3- and 4-bedroom units from noon on Wednesday, October 28 to noon on Friday, October 30, 2026. The October notice says "All applicants will be placed on waiting lists in order of the date and time received," and that the lists for public housing 0, 1 and 2 bedrooms and Section 8 "REMAIN CLOSED." After October 30, the homepage again says no list is accepting pre-applications (checked November 3, 2026). CSHA says it announces openings on its website, its phone system and in The Gazette.

Programs CSHA Runs

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8), filled through an annual lottery wait list
  • Public Housing, 706 single-family and multi-family homes, including senior and disabled buildings
  • CSHA-managed senior and disabled housing and other managed properties, some with no El Paso County residency rule
  • Rio Grande Village apartments, with sliding-scale and market-rate rents

How to Apply

Apply directly through the authority's official application page: csha.us/apply-or-check-status/. If you have already applied, call (719) 387-6700 to verify your position and keep your contact information current. If the authority cannot reach you when your name comes up, you can be dropped from the list.

New to the process? Our step-by-step guide to applying for Section 8 walks through the whole thing, and how to find your PHA helps you locate the right authority.

Other Authorities Serving This Area

  • The El Paso County rule. CSHA says "Applicants must live and/or work in El Paso County, Colorado, at the time their application is submitted to be placed on most of our wait lists." CSHA's property pages mark the exceptions; on November 3, 2026 they included its Kiowa Properties, Pikes Peak Properties and Rio Grande Village. CSHA also says it is not staffed to help everyone fill out the online pre-application; ask for a reasonable accommodation if you have a medical reason.
  • There is no separate El Paso County housing authority. HUD's Colorado list has none, and El Paso County's own housing resources page sends residents to CSHA for "Public Housing and Section 8 Vouchers for Colorado Springs and El Paso County."
  • Fountain Housing Authority (HUD: Housing Authority of the City of Fountain, CO071), 501 E. Iowa Ave., Fountain, (719) 382-5639, serves the Fountain Valley south of Colorado Springs. Its site read on November 3, 2026: "Fountain Housing Authority waitlists are CLOSED."
  • Colorado Division of Housing (DOH), HUD code CO911, runs state voucher programs through local agencies, and says "Vouchers are given by those agencies, not the Division of Housing." On its Housing Voucher Programs page (checked November 3, 2026), its Housing Choice Voucher program reads "Program Status: Pause," as do its Family Unification Program and its State Housing Vouchers. Programs listed as open work only by referral: VASH for homeless veterans, "Referrals made through Colorado VA Medical Centers"; Community Access Team Vouchers for people with disabilities leaving or at risk of institutions; Section 811; and Project Based Assistance.
  • CSHA is not an emergency rent fund. For rent help, see our Colorado Springs emergency rent help guide: the state's Colorado Emergency Rental Assistance (CERA) and its monthly random selection, 211, and free eviction help from Colorado Legal Services.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Colorado Springs Section 8 waitlist open?

No. CSHA's homepage says "None of our waiting lists are currently accepting online only pre-applications" and "The current directive from HUD to pause the issuance of new Housing Choice Vouchers is, unfortunately, still in effect" (checked November 3, 2026). CSHA says its voucher lottery list "usually opens every July," but no 2026 lottery appears on its Wait List Opening Announcements page; the last one ran July 15 to 17, 2025. CSHA has not announced when it will open again or when voucher issuance will resume.

How does the CSHA Section 8 lottery work?

When the voucher list opens, CSHA takes online pre-applications only, for a few days, usually in July. Every pre-application gets a random lottery number, and CSHA selects by number "when there are available vouchers," so applying early does not help. The lottery list does not carry over: CSHA's 2025 notice says "At the end of each lottery year, any remaining applications will be removed, and families must reapply when the waiting list reopens." CSHA announces openings on its website, on its phone system and in The Gazette, and you can sign up on its site for email alerts.

When did CSHA last open public housing?

Its most recent window was for 3- and 4-bedroom public housing, from noon on Wednesday, October 28 to noon on Friday, October 30, 2026, and it is closed. Earlier 2026 windows ran April 1 to 3 (1-bedroom elderly and disabled, and 2-bedroom units) and September 2 to 4 (1-bedroom elderly and disabled units at Franklin Square and Southview Plaza). Each lasted two days and took online pre-applications only. For the next one, watch CSHA's Wait List Opening Announcements page or sign up for its email alerts.

How do I check my ranking on a CSHA waiting list?

Call (719) 387-6700 and press 1 to hear your ranking on any list you are on; CSHA says to allow at least one week after applying. If you applied online, log in to WaitList Check with the username and password you created. CSHA explains that a status of "Active" shows your position, while "Submitted" means "you were not placed on that specific wait list and will have to apply when it opens again." Keep your mailing address current with an Application Update Form, because CSHA says not answering its letters can get you removed.

What is CSHA's phone number and where is the office?

The main number is (719) 387-6700. It is on CSHA's homepage, its Apply or Check Status page, its FAQ and its Contact page, and HUD lists the same number for CO028. The main office is at 831 S. Nevada Ave., Floor 2, open Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday from 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. and Wednesday from 8 a.m. to 2 p.m. Housing Choice Voucher staff see visitors by appointment only. CSHA warns that it "does not send communication via text message to our clients," so ignore texts that claim to be from CSHA.

I live in Fountain or elsewhere in El Paso County. Where do I apply?

Most CSHA lists accept people who live or work anywhere in El Paso County, and El Paso County's own housing page points residents to CSHA for Section 8 and public housing. Fountain also has its own agency, the Fountain Housing Authority, (719) 382-5639, whose site said on November 3, 2026 that its waitlists are closed. The Colorado Division of Housing's general voucher program was paused on the same date.
