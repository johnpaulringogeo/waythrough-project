The San Francisco Housing Authority (SFHA) is the public housing authority (PHA) that administers Section 8 Housing Choice Vouchers and project-based rental assistance for the City and County of San Francisco. Separately, the city's DAHLIA portal runs ongoing lotteries for below-market-rate affordable apartments. This page summarizes SFHA's current waiting-list status and contact information, and links to the official sites for anything time-sensitive.

Programs SFHA Runs

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8)
  • Project-Based Vouchers (including RAD-converted developments)
  • Below-market-rate affordable units via the DAHLIA lottery (city-run, separate from SFHA)

How to Apply

Apply directly through the authority's official application page: sfha.org/. If you have already applied, SFHA's Waitlist page says its Customer Care Center, (415) 715-5200, "can confirm if applicants are active on the waitlist." Keep your contact information current: SFHA says changes to your address, phone, income or family size must be reported "within 10 business days of the change by letter or in person."

New to the process? Our step-by-step guide to applying for Section 8 walks through the whole thing, and how to find your PHA helps you locate the right authority.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the San Francisco (SFHA) Section 8 waiting list open?

No. On November 5, 2026, the San Francisco Housing Authority's Waitlist page listed the Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8), Project Based Voucher, RAD and Public Housing lists as closed, with no reopening date. If you already applied, SFHA's Customer Care Center at (415) 715-5200 can confirm whether you are still active on the waitlist. While the lists are closed, the most active path to affordable housing in San Francisco is the city's DAHLIA portal (housing.sfgov.org), which runs regular lotteries for below-market-rate apartments.

What is DAHLIA and how is it different from Section 8?

DAHLIA (housing.sfgov.org) is San Francisco's official portal for below-market-rate affordable apartments, awarded by lottery. It is run by the city and is separate from SFHA's Section 8 program — you do not need a voucher to apply. It is usually the most active option when the SFHA voucher list is closed.

Who do I call for emergency rent help in San Francisco?

SFHA does not run an emergency rent fund. The City's San Francisco Emergency Rental Assistance Program (SF ERAP) helps residents with household income at or below 50% of the Area Median Income; the City says funding is limited. Its helpline is (415) 653-5744. If you got a notice ending your tenancy or court papers, San Francisco's Tenant Right to Counsel program gives free legal help: call the Eviction Defense Collaborative at (415) 659-9184.
