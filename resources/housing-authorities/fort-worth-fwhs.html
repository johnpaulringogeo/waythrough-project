Fort Worth Housing Solutions (FWHS) is the Housing Authority of the City of Fort Worth under the name it uses today. HUD lists it as PHA TX004, the Housing Authority of Fort Worth, with a program type of Combined. Its office is at 1407 Texas St., Fort Worth, TX 76102, open Monday through Thursday from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. and closed on Fridays. As of November 11, 2026, the Section 8 list is closed: FWHS says it "is not currently accepting Wait List applications." FWHS also ran a short Project-Based Voucher (PBV) waitlist window at three properties, which opened Sunday, November 1, 2026, at 8:00 AM and closed on Thursday, November 5, 2026, at 5:00 PM. No new opening was posted when we checked on November 11, 2026; watch fwhs.org for the next one. This page covers FWHS's exact words, the three properties, what applicants can do now, its preferences, and the Tarrant County office that runs a separate list.

Waiting-List Status

Waiting lists open and close often. The status below is what we found the last time we checked; confirm it on the official site before you count on it.

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8): Closed. Checked November 11, 2026. FWHS's Housing Choice Voucher page says: "FWHS is not currently accepting Wait List applications. We encourage you to visit our Properties page and contact one of our affordable Fort Worth communities directly to determine whether you may qualify for reduced rent based on income." It also says "The agency maintains a program Wait List through a lottery system and assigns vouchers as they become available." The last voucher opening FWHS posted ran from July 7, 2025 at 8:00 AM to July 10, 2025 at 5:00 PM. No new voucher opening was posted.
  • Project-Based Vouchers at Hughes House III, Sedona Village Senior Living and Cowan Place: Closed (window ran November 1–5, 2026). FWHS's October 2, 2026 announcement said the PBV waitlist for these properties would open Sunday, November 1, 2026, at 8:00 AM and "close on Thursday, November 5, 2026, at 5:00 PM": Hughes House III, 4912 East Rosedale Street (1- and 2-bedroom units); Sedona Village Senior Living, 2800 Sedona Ranch Drive (2-bedroom units); and Cowan Place, 5400 East Rosedale Street (1- and 2-bedroom units). Applications went through the FWHS RentCafe portal, and FWHS allows two persons per bedroom. That window is now closed. When we checked on November 11, 2026, FWHS had posted no results, lottery details or next opening for these lists; check fwhs.org for the next opening.
  • RAD units and FWHS affordable communities: Apply at each property. FWHS says it "has transitioned out of traditional public housing." For Rental Assistance Demonstration units, it says "Contact the property directly to ask about the waitlist." Its Our Properties page lists the communities.

Programs FWHS Runs

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8), with a wait list kept "through a lottery system"
  • Project-Based Vouchers (PBV), with a separate list for each property
  • Rental Assistance Demonstration (RAD) units at FWHS communities, where tenants pay 30 percent of adjusted income
  • Mainstream vouchers for people ages 18 to 61 with disabilities
  • Homelessness programs for people referred by a partner agency, including veterans
  • Family Self-Sufficiency and homeownership programs
  • Mixed-income affordable apartment communities owned by FWHS

How to Apply

Apply on the authority's own application page: fwhs.org/housing-programs/housing-choice-voucher/. If you already applied, call (817) 333-3400 to check your place on the list, and give them your new phone number or address every time it changes. If they can't reach you when your name comes up, they can drop you from the list.

If this is your first time applying, our step-by-step guide to applying for Section 8 covers each stage, and how to find your PHA shows which authority covers where you live.

Other Authorities Serving This Area

  • The Tarrant County Housing Assistance Office is a separate, Section 8 only agency with its own list. HUD lists it as TX431, at 2100 Circle Drive, Suite 200, Fort Worth, TX 76119, (817) 531-7654. Its wait list page says "OUR WAIT LIST IS CURRENTLY "CLOSED" and we anticipate being closed for the next seven years, possibly longer" (checked November 3, 2026). That list opened on March 14, 2017 and took more than 14,900 applications. The office says it contacts applicants in writing only, not by email or phone, and that you must tell it about any change of address in writing.
  • Nearby city agencies keep their own lists, including the Arlington Housing Authority (HUD TX433, (817) 275-3351) and Grand Prairie Housing and Neighborhood Services (HUD TX434). Both numbers are as HUD lists them, so confirm with each agency.
  • FWHS is not an emergency rent fund. Our Fort Worth housing resources page lists shelters and more. If you already have FWHS housing or a voucher and fall behind, call FWHS at (817) 333-3400.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Fort Worth Housing Solutions Section 8 waitlist open?

No. As of November 11, 2026, FWHS's Housing Choice Voucher page says "FWHS is not currently accepting Wait List applications." FWHS keeps its voucher list through a lottery, and its last posted voucher opening ran July 7 to July 10, 2025. Its Project-Based Voucher window at three properties ran from November 1, 2026 at 8:00 AM to November 5, 2026 at 5:00 PM and is now closed. Check fwhs.org for the next opening.

Can I still apply for the November 2026 FWHS project-based voucher list?

No. That window closed at 5:00 PM on Thursday, November 5, 2026. It covered Hughes House III (1- and 2-bedroom units), Sedona Village Senior Living (2-bedroom units) and Cowan Place (1- and 2-bedroom units), with applications through FWHS's RentCafe portal. When we checked on November 11, 2026, FWHS had posted no new opening. If you applied, use the RentCafe portal to check your status and keep your contact information current. People with disabilities who need help can call 817-333-3400. For the next opening, check fwhs.org.

Who can apply for the Sedona Village project-based voucher units?

Check the household size first. The November 2026 window offered 2-bedroom units at Sedona Village Senior Living, and FWHS's subsidy standard is two persons per bedroom. When FWHS opened the Sedona Village list in June 2026, its notice said "A minimum of 3 to 4 people per household is required to qualify" and "Single applicants do not meet the household composition criteria for this specific property." If the portal says you do not qualify, FWHS said that is likely because of these household size rules. Call 817-333-3400 if you are unsure.

Does Fort Worth have public housing?

Not in the traditional sense. FWHS says it "has transitioned out of traditional public housing." It owns mixed-income communities and Rental Assistance Demonstration (RAD) units where tenants pay 30 percent of adjusted income. For RAD units, FWHS says to contact the property directly about its waitlist.

Who gets preference on the FWHS voucher list?

FWHS's Administrative Plan, dated January 2022 and posted on its site, lists local preferences for families displaced by disaster, government action or FWHS redevelopment, for VAWA emergency transfers of FWHS residents, and for FWHS residents whose disability needs cannot be met in their unit. It also sets aside vouchers for referrals: ten for homeless college students, twenty for homeless families with children in the Fort Worth school district or a neighboring district, and ten for people leaving a nursing facility. The plan says the voucher list "always remains open" to displaced families who qualify for that preference. Ask FWHS which preferences are in force today.

How do I check my FWHS waitlist status or change my address?

Use the FWHS RentCafe portal. FWHS says the portal lets applicants "view the status of your application" and update contact information. Applicants on the waitlist can also submit a question through FWHS's contact page or call (817) 333-3400, Monday through Thursday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. The office is closed on Fridays.
