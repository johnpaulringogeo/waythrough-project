The Newark Housing Authority (NHA) runs public housing and the Section 8 Housing Choice Voucher program in the City of Newark, along with Emergency Housing Vouchers and project-based vouchers. Its central office is at 500 Broad Street, Newark, NJ 07102, and its Customer Service Center number is 973-273-6000. The short version today: NHA says its Housing Choice Voucher waiting list is closed and it is not taking new applications, but it is accepting applications for its open public housing lists, which are most of its sites. Public housing applications are online only, through NHA's Rent Café portal. New Jersey's Department of Community Affairs also runs a statewide voucher program open to Newark residents, and its list is closed too. This page covers NHA's exact words, the open and closed sites, how to apply and check your status, the phone numbers, and where to turn if you need help with rent now.

Programs NHA Runs

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8); the waiting list is closed. HCV customer service: 973-273-6206, hcvcustomerservice@newarkha.org
  • Public housing at family sites, senior and disabled buildings, townhomes and scattered sites, each with its own waiting list, applied for online through NHA's Rent Café portal
  • Emergency Housing Vouchers
  • Project-based vouchers; NHA's HCV/PBV online services and forms are at newark.nanmckay.com

How to Apply

Apply on the authority's own application page: www.newarkha.org/Programs-Services/Public-Housing-Program. If you have already applied, check your status and update your contact information through NHA's waitlist status check (you register once), at the computer terminals in the lobby at 500 Broad Street (8:30 am to 4:30 pm, Monday to Friday), or by calling (973) 273-6127 (9 am to noon Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday; 1 pm to 4 pm Tuesday). Keep your address and phone current. If NHA cannot reach you when your name comes up, you can lose your place.

If this is your first time applying, our step-by-step guide to applying for Section 8 covers each stage, and how to find your PHA shows which authority covers where you live.

Other Authorities Serving This Area

  • New Jersey Department of Community Affairs (DCA) runs a statewide Housing Choice Voucher program that "is available to residents of all New Jersey counties." On November 12, 2026 its page said "THE WAITLIST IS NOW CLOSED." The last window ran January 17 to February 3, 2023, through WaitlistCheck.com. People who got on that list through the lottery show an ACTIVE status. DCA: 609-292-4080, option 9.
  • DCA's State Rental Assistance Program (SRAP), New Jersey's state-funded voucher, is closed: "The SRAP waiting list opening for pre-applications has closed." That window ran January 13 to 31, 2025, and DCA says to "continue to check DCA's website to see when new waiting lists will be opened in the future."
  • Facing eviction? Newark's Office of Tenant Legal Services gives free legal help to Newark tenant households facing eviction with gross income at or below 200% of the federal poverty level. 920 Mayor Kenneth A. Gibson Blvd., Room 110, 973-877-9424. If you live in NHA housing or have an NHA voucher, an eviction can cost you your assistance, so call right away.
  • NHA is not an emergency rent fund. If you are behind on rent, see our Newark emergency rent help guide: the State's eviction-prevention partners in Essex County, County Emergency Assistance, NJ 211, LIHEAP and USF, and New Jersey's eviction rules. If you already have NHA housing or a voucher and fall behind, call NHA at 973-273-6000.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Newark Section 8 waiting list open?

No. As of November 12, 2026, NHA says its Housing Choice Voucher waiting list is closed and that no new applications are being accepted. No reopening date is posted. New Jersey DCA's statewide voucher list is closed too. NHA's public housing lists are open at most sites, so you can still apply for those.

Which NHA public housing lists are open?

NHA's table shows open lists at Pennington Court, John W. Hyatt Court, its senior buildings (Stephen Crane, Kretchmer, Seth Boyden and James Baxter), Kretchmer Townhomes and its scattered sites. Bradley Court is closed. Four RAD sites show a date instead of yes or no, so ask NHA about those. Check NHA's Public Housing page before you apply.

How do I apply to NHA?

Public housing applications must be submitted online through NHA's Rent Café portal at portals.newarkha.org, unless you have an approved reasonable accommodation. The pre-application stays active for up to 18 months. The voucher list is closed, so there is no voucher application right now.

How do I check my place on an NHA list?

Use NHA's online waitlist status check (you register once), the computer terminals in the lobby at 500 Broad Street from 8:30 am to 4:30 pm on weekdays, or call (973) 273-6127. NHA's status page notes there are over 12,000 applicants on its waiting list.

Does New Jersey have its own Section 8 program for Newark residents?

Yes. New Jersey's Department of Community Affairs runs a statewide voucher program open to residents of every county, plus the state-funded SRAP. Both lists are closed as of November 12, 2026. DCA's last voucher window ran January 17 to February 3, 2023. Call 609-292-4080, option 9, with questions.

What does a Section 8 tenant pay in Newark?

Under HUD rules, a family with a voucher or in public housing usually pays about 30% of its adjusted monthly income toward rent and utilities, and the housing authority pays the rest of an approved rent. Adjusted income is your income after HUD's deductions, such as those for children, elderly or disabled family members, and some medical and child care costs.
