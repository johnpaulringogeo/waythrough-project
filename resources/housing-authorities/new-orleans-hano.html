The Housing Authority of New Orleans (HANO) is the public housing agency for the city of New Orleans. HUD lists it as PHA LA001 with a program type of Combined, so it runs both Housing Choice Vouchers and public housing. HANO's main phone is (504) 670-3300 (TTY (504) 670-3377), and its website lists an administrative office at 1555 Poydras St., Suite 1800, and a Client Services Center at 10001 Lake Forest Blvd., open Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. HANO says it serves "more than 17,000 families" with vouchers. The short version, checked November 5, 2026: HANO's voucher list is closed with "no future reopen date," and its applicant portal says "There are currently no waiting lists open." This page covers HANO's exact words, how its lists work, the minimum rent and hardship rules, and the other housing authorities around New Orleans.

Waiting-List Status

Waiting lists open and close frequently, so treat the status below as a starting point and confirm on the official site before you rely on it.

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8): Closed, no reopening date — Checked November 5, 2026: HANO's voucher page says "PLEASE NOTE: The HCVP waiting list is closed" and "Currently, the HANO HCV Waiting list is closed and no future reopen date has been determined. Public notice will be given when the list reopens." The last opening was in November 2022, when "over 10,500 applications were received." HANO says it has "a waiting list of approximately 20,000 additional families" and will "advertise in the major media outlets" when it takes new applications.
  • Public Housing (site-based lists): No list shown as open — HANO's My Housing applicant portal says "No Lists are Open" and "There are currently no waiting lists open" (checked November 5, 2026). HANO's Public Housing page says each list is announced when it opens: "When a Site-Based Waiting List is opened, it will be announced through public notice, advertisement in local area newspapers, notification to community stakeholders and posted on HANO's web page." It also says "There is no way to predict how long you may have to wait before you are called for a unit." Public housing applications line: (504) 670-3446.
  • Project-Based Vouchers (PBV): No list shown as open — HANO's PBV page says "A family may only apply to be placed on PBV waiting lists if the waiting list is open," and the My Housing portal showed no open lists on November 5, 2026. The most recent PBV notice on that page is for St. Bernard Circle Apartments, a lottery list that opened April 14, 2025 and closed April 15, 2025, so it is not open now.

Programs HANO Runs

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8): HANO says it serves "more than 17,000 families"; its draft plan for the fiscal year starting October 1, 2026 counts 18,305 vouchers
  • Public housing at 13 sites listed on HANO's website, including Bienville Basin, Harmony Oaks, Faubourg Lafitte, Marrero Commons, River Garden, Guste Homes and William J. Fischer, most with their own site-based waiting lists
  • Project-Based Vouchers: the draft plan says 2,457 units are under contract, with lists run by each property
  • Homeownership and Family Self-Sufficiency (FSS) programs for current HANO families
  • Special vouchers named in HANO's plans, including Mainstream vouchers for people with disabilities and Emergency Housing Vouchers

How to Apply

Apply directly through the authority's official application page: hano.myhousing.com/. If you are already on HANO's voucher list, log in to your My Housing account at hano.myhousing.com to update your contact information or household; HANO says "If you do not update your information, you may lose your place on the waiting list." For a public housing list, HANO says to call or visit the leasing office of the community where you applied.

New to the process? Our step-by-step guide to applying for Section 8 walks through the whole thing, and how to find your PHA helps you locate the right authority.

JPHSDD's online pre-application portal listed no open lists that day.
  • Housing Authority of the City of Kenner (HUD code LA012, (504) 467-9166): its site says "The HCV and PBV Waiting List are closed" (checked November 5, 2026).
  • Housing Authority of the City of Slidell (HUD code LA103, (985) 726-9000): its voucher page says "Currently the waiting list is closed until further notice" (checked November 5, 2026).
  • St. Bernard Parish Government (HUD code LA187) runs vouchers for St. Bernard Parish. Its last posted opening ran from September 1 to September 7, 2022, with 500 applicants picked at random; it posts no current status, so call (504) 278-4468.
  • HANO is not an emergency rent fund. If you are behind on rent, see our New Orleans emergency rent help guide: the City's Right to Counsel lawyers through Southeast Louisiana Legal Services and the Louisiana Fair Housing Action Center, VIA LINK 211, utility help and Louisiana's eviction deadlines. Frequently Asked Questions

Is the New Orleans Section 8 waiting list open?

No. As of November 5, 2026, HANO's voucher page says "The HCVP waiting list is closed" and that "no future reopen date has been determined." HANO last took applications in November 2022 and says it will give public notice and advertise in major media when the list reopens. Its applicant portal at hano.myhousing.com says "There are currently no waiting lists open," which covers public housing and project-based lists too.

How do I apply for public housing in New Orleans?

Only when a site's list is open. HANO says each site-based list is announced by public notice, local newspaper ads and its website when it opens. You can then apply on the My Housing website or at the public housing community, and you can apply at any community with an open list. People with disabilities can call the community to ask for a paper application. HANO's public housing applications line is (504) 670-3446. On November 5, 2026, the portal showed no open lists.

How do I update my information on HANO's waiting list?

For the voucher list, log in to your My Housing account at hano.myhousing.com and update your address, phone and household. HANO says if you do not, you may lose your place, and if it passes your number because it cannot reach you, you will be withdrawn. You can also email changes to info@hano.org with your name, Social Security number, old address and new address. For a public housing list, call or visit the leasing office of the community where you applied.

What is HANO's minimum rent, and can I get a hardship exemption?

HANO's Administrative Plan says "The minimum rent for this locality is $50." Most families pay about 30% of their adjusted monthly income instead, so the minimum only matters when income is very low. The plan says HANO can suspend or exempt a family from the minimum rent when a financial hardship exists, such as losing a job or waiting on a benefits decision. A short-term hardship suspends it for 90 days, and that amount must be paid back. Ask HANO in writing as soon as your income drops.

Who gets preference on HANO's voucher list?

HANO's Administrative Plan lists preferences for referrals through its partnerships with UNITY of Greater New Orleans and the New Orleans Family Justice Center (for people who are homeless, have disabilities or are survivors of domestic violence), for households moving on from supportive housing, for some non-elderly people with disabilities, and for a small number of homebuyer-ready public housing residents each year. These referral preferences do not open the list to the public.

I live in Jefferson Parish or Kenner. Where do I apply?

Not at HANO, which serves New Orleans. In Jefferson Parish, the Housing Authority of Jefferson Parish says it no longer handles Section 8 and sends voucher questions to the parish housing department at 504.342.4475. The Kenner Housing Authority says its voucher and project-based lists are closed. In St. Tammany, the Slidell Housing Authority says its list is closed until further notice. All were checked November 5, 2026.
