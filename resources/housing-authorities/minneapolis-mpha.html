Quick Facts

Serves: the City of Minneapolis, Minnesota
Phone: (612) 342-1400
Official site: mphaonline.org/
Apply: MPHA application page
Status last checked: November 3, 2026

The Minneapolis Public Housing Authority (MPHA) is the public housing agency for the City of Minneapolis. HUD lists it, under its legal name PHA in and for the City of Minneapolis, as PHA MN002 with a program type of Combined, so it runs both Housing Choice Vouchers and public housing. MPHA says it owns and operates nearly 6,000 rental units, most of them studio and one-bedroom apartments in 42 high-rises, and administers nearly 7,500 tenant-based and project-based vouchers. Its headquarters is at 1001 N Washington Ave., Minneapolis, MN 55401. The short version today: the federal Section 8 list is closed, the family public housing lists (2 to 5 bedrooms) are closed, and MPHA's high-rise lists for seniors, near-elderly and disabled applicants are open. A one-time window for the state-funded Bring It Home voucher ran October 13 to 15, 2026 and is over. The suburbs around the city are served by a different agency, Metro HRA, whose voucher list is also closed. This page covers MPHA's exact words, how to apply for a high-rise, the preference points, and which number to call.

Waiting-List Status

Waiting lists open and close frequently, so treat the status below as a starting point and confirm on the official site before you rely on it.

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8): Closed — Closed (checked November 3, 2026). MPHA's Tenant-Based Vouchers page is headed "Applying for Housing Vouchers – Waitlist Currently Closed" and says: "The Housing Choice Voucher waitlist is currently closed for new applications, you may check back periodically for any updates." MPHA's applicant portal lists "Housing Choice Voucher – Closed." MPHA gives no reopening date. Its October 1, 2026 news release called the Bring It Home window "the first time MPHA has accepted public applications for any tenant-based housing assistance voucher since 2019." If you think you are already on the list, MPHA says to call 612-335-4404 with the head of household's Social Security number and date of birth.
  • Bring It Home (state-funded voucher): Closed since October 15, 2026 — Bring It Home (BIH) is a Minnesota Housing voucher that works like Section 8; MPHA's BIH vouchers can only be used in Minneapolis. MPHA took applications only from 12 a.m. Tuesday, October 13, 2026 until 11:59 p.m. Thursday, October 15, 2026, when, in MPHA's words, "The waiting list will close for new applications." That window has passed, so do not apply until MPHA announces a new one (the portal line "Bring It Home - Open until 11:59pm on Thursday, October 15, 2026" refers to the old window). Applying entered a lottery for up to 1,500 waitlist spots, with households with children under 18 and households at or below 30 percent of area median income placed first. MPHA said applicants would see their status in RentCafe by November 1, 2026; log in at portal.mphaonline.org to check yours.
  • Public housing high-rises (studio and one-bedroom): Open: Near Elderly (50 to 61), Elderly (62+) and Disabled lists — MPHA's applicant portal, checked November 3, 2026, lists "Near Elderly - Open," "Elderly – Open" and "Disabled - Open" for its studio and one-bedroom high-rise apartments, for 1- and 2-person households with income at or below 80 percent of area median income. Near-elderly and disabled applicants pre-apply online at portal.mphaonline.org. Seniors 62 and older apply in person at 1001 Washington Avenue North or ask for a paper application by mail, because the portal says "Pre-applications for the Elderly Waiting List are not currently available online." MPHA's senior page lists in-person applications Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 3:30 p.m.
  • Public housing for families (2 to 5 bedrooms): Closed — MPHA's Housing for Families page says: "MPHA's waitlist for family housing is currently closed," and the portal lists its 2-, 3-, 4- and 5-bedroom wait lists as "Closed" (checked November 3, 2026). The last opening MPHA describes ran October 10 to 15, 2024 and was followed by a lottery. No reopening date is posted.

Programs MPHA Runs

  • Housing Choice Vouchers (Section 8), over 7,000 tenant-based vouchers
  • Project-Based Vouchers in privately owned buildings, filled from MPHA's voucher waiting list
  • Public housing and deeply affordable housing, nearly 6,000 units: 42 high-rises, 184 family homes at Glendale, more than 700 scattered-site family homes and new multiplexes
  • Bring It Home state vouchers, paid for by a two-year, $21 million Minnesota Housing grant and usable only in Minneapolis
  • Special purpose vouchers by referral, including Stable Homes Stable Schools, the Community Choice Demonstration with Metro HRA, HUD-VASH and Family Unification

How to Apply

Apply directly through the authority's official application page: portal.mphaonline.org/. If you have already applied, call 612-335-4404, the number MPHA's Tenant-Based Vouchers page gives for checking your place on the Section 8 waitlist (MPHA's main line is a voicemail box), and email hcvwaitinglist@mplspha.org to update your information.

Other Authorities Serving This Area

  • Metro HRA, the housing authority of the Metropolitan Council, HUD code MN163, program type Section 8, runs what it calls the largest voucher program in Minnesota for nearly 100 communities in Anoka, Carver and most of suburban Hennepin and Ramsey counties; the city list on its Service Area page does not include Minneapolis or St. Paul. Its Housing Choice Voucher page said on November 3, 2026: "The waitlist is currently closed. Applications are only accepted when Metro HRA announces an open waitlist period." Its Bring It Home page also showed "Waiting List Closed," with Metro HRA inviting households already on its voucher list. 390 Robert St. N., St. Paul; applications and wait list (651) 602-1880, general questions (651) 602-1428 (Metro HRA's Contact page).
  • Public Housing Agency of the City of St Paul, HUD code MN001, 555 N. Wabasha Street, St. Paul, (651) 298-5664 (HUD's Minnesota list), runs St. Paul's own vouchers and public housing.
  • Several suburbs run their own housing authorities, including the Bloomington HRA (HUD code MN152, (952) 563-8937), the Richfield HRA (MN216), the Plymouth HRA (MN170) and the Hopkins HRA (MN078), all on HUD's Minnesota list. HUD's list has no separate Hennepin County housing authority.
  • MPHA is not an emergency rent fund. If you are behind on rent, dial 211 and see our Minneapolis housing resources page for the City's emergency rental assistance, Hennepin County's rent help and free tenant advice from HOME Line.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Minneapolis Section 8 waiting list open?

No. As of November 3, 2026, MPHA's Tenant-Based Vouchers page says "The Housing Choice Voucher waitlist is currently closed for new applications," and MPHA's portal lists "Housing Choice Voucher – Closed." MPHA posts no reopening date, and says it had not taken public applications for any tenant-based voucher between 2019 and its October 2026 Bring It Home window, which closed October 15, 2026. If you live in the suburbs, Metro HRA's voucher list is closed too. The lists that are open in Minneapolis right now are MPHA's high-rise public housing lists for people 50 and older and for people with disabilities.

What was the Bring It Home waiting list, and how do I find out if I got on it?

Bring It Home is a state-funded rental voucher from Minnesota Housing that works like Section 8. MPHA took applications for it only from October 13 to October 15, 2026, for households that live in Minnesota, pay more than 30 percent of their income for rent, are not already in a Section 8 program, and are willing to live in Minneapolis. The window is over. Applying entered a lottery for up to 1,500 spots, with households with children under 18 and households at or below 30 percent of area median income placed first. MPHA said applicants would be notified of their status in RentCafe by November 1, 2026, so log in to portal.mphaonline.org to see yours. MPHA's Bring It Home email is bih@mplspha.org.

Can I apply for MPHA public housing right now?

Yes, if you are a 1- or 2-person household for a high-rise studio or one-bedroom. MPHA's portal lists the Near Elderly (ages 50 to 61), Elderly (62 and older) and Disabled lists as open, checked November 3, 2026. Near-elderly and disabled applicants pre-apply online at portal.mphaonline.org with a valid email address and full Social Security number. Seniors 62 and older must apply in person at 1001 Washington Avenue North or ask MPHA to mail an application. Family housing, 2 to 5 bedrooms, is closed. For help with the portal, MPHA gives 612-342-1390 and MPHAapplications@mplspha.org.

How does MPHA decide who gets a public housing unit first?

By preference points and date of application. MPHA's 2026 Statement of Policies says it "will not place any family on the waiting list that does not have a preference." For high-rises, an applicant who is 62 or older gets 40 points, one who is 50 to 61 gets 35, one with a disability gets 30, and a U.S. veteran gets 5, and applications are processed by date, time and points. Family housing applications are processed by date and time, not points, and veterans have a preference there too. MPHA also periodically asks applicants to update their information on the portal; miss the deadline and you are removed from the list.

Is Metro HRA the same as MPHA?

No. Metro HRA is the housing authority of the Metropolitan Council, HUD code MN163, based at 390 Robert St. N. in St. Paul. It serves nearly 100 suburban communities in Anoka, Carver and most of suburban Hennepin and Ramsey counties, but not Minneapolis or St. Paul. On November 3, 2026 its Housing Choice Voucher page said "The waitlist is currently closed." Metro HRA says it takes applications only during an announced open period, picks households at random, and estimates a wait of up to 5 years. For its application and wait list line, call 651-602-1880.

Which MPHA phone number should I call?

It depends on the question. MPHA's main line, 612-342-1400, is listed as a voicemail box; it is in the footer of every MPHA page, on the Contact page and in HUD's directory for MN002. For vouchers, the Housing Choice Vouchers office is 612-342-1480 (footer, Contact page and the Bring It Home release). For public housing applications and leasing, call 612-342-1390 (footer, Contact page and the high-rise application pages). To check your place on the Section 8 waitlist, MPHA's Tenant-Based Vouchers page gives 612-335-4404, and to update your waitlist information you can email hcvwaitinglist@mplspha.org.
