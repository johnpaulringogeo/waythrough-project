The Housing Authority of the City of Milwaukee (HACM) is the public housing authority (PHA) that administers Section 8 Housing Choice Vouchers, Project-Based Vouchers, and public housing for the City of Milwaukee. This page summarizes HACM's current waiting-list status, contact information, and how to apply, and links to the official HACM site for anything time-sensitive.

Waiting-List Status

Waiting lists open and close frequently, so treat the status below as a starting point and confirm on the official site before you rely on it.

  • Housing Choice Voucher (tenant-based Section 8): Closed — Closed (checked November 5, 2026). HACM's waiting lists page says the Section 8 Housing Choice Vouchers (Rent Assistance) list "remains CLOSED." HACM ran a random lottery on June 17, 2026 that placed 5,000 applicants from its December 15, 2021 to August 1, 2024 pre-application period on the official list, and says "At this time, HACM is not issuing vouchers." HACM says the next opening will be posted at waitlist.hacm.org at least 10 business days in advance. Apply to neighboring authorities in the meantime.
  • Project-Based Vouchers (PBV): Open until further notice — HACM says it is "currently accepting pre-applications" for Project-Based Voucher units at specific developments: buildings with a preference for seniors, near-elderly and people with disabilities, Scattered Sites and Town Homes at Carver Park for families, Victory Manor for veterans, and Westlawn Gardens. Apply online at hacm.org/subsidized (checked November 5, 2026).
  • Public Housing: Open — HACM: "The Public Housing waiting list is OPEN." Waiting-list applications are online only at hacm.org/subsidized, and HACM lists its public housing properties at hacm.org/phproperties. Public Housing Applications/Intake Office: (414) 286-5678 (checked November 5, 2026).

Programs HACM Runs

  • Housing Choice Voucher (tenant-based Section 8)
  • Project-Based Vouchers (PBV)
  • Public Housing
  • HUD-VASH (for veterans)
  • Mainstream vouchers (non-elderly people with disabilities)

How to Apply

Apply directly through the authority's official application page: www.hacm.org/subsidized. If you have already applied, HACM says it "is unable to provide individual status updates or position on the list beyond lottery selection results," and it emails applicants when they are selected, so keep your contact information current at myportal.hacm.org. Questions: (414) 286-5650 or hcvsupport@hacm.org.

New to the process? Our step-by-step guide to applying for Section 8 walks through the whole thing, and how to find your PHA helps you locate the right authority.

Other Authorities Serving This Area

  • Housing Authority of Milwaukee County is a separate authority that serves areas outside the City of Milwaukee limits.
  • Neighboring authorities that run their own separate waiting lists: West Allis, Waukesha County, Racine, Kenosha, and WHEDA (statewide).
  • HACM is not an emergency rent fund. If you are behind on rent, see our Milwaukee emergency rent help guide: free Eviction Free MKE lawyers, the Rental Housing Resource Center, Wisconsin Emergency Assistance and Wisconsin's eviction deadlines. If you already have HACM housing or a voucher and fall behind, call HACM right away.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Milwaukee (HACM) Section 8 waiting list open?

No. As of November 5, 2026, HACM says its Section 8 Housing Choice Voucher list "remains CLOSED." A lottery on June 17, 2026 placed 5,000 earlier applicants on the official list, and HACM says it is not issuing vouchers right now. HACM's public housing list and its Project-Based Voucher lists are open, and you apply online at hacm.org/subsidized. Always confirm the current status on HACM's site before relying on it.

How do I check my place on the HACM waiting list?

You can't get a place number. HACM says it "is unable to provide individual status updates or position on the list beyond lottery selection results," and it emails applicants when they are selected. Keep your address, phone and email current at myportal.hacm.org; if HACM cannot reach you, you can be removed from the list. For questions, the Housing Choice Voucher program is at (414) 286-5650 or hcvsupport@hacm.org.

Who do I contact if I live outside the City of Milwaukee?

The Housing Authority of Milwaukee County is a separate authority for areas outside the city limits. You can also apply to neighboring authorities such as West Allis, Waukesha County, Racine, Kenosha, and the statewide WHEDA program, each of which runs its own waiting list.
