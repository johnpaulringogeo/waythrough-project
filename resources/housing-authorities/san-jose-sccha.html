Santa Clara County Housing Authority (SCCHA)

Serves San José and all of Santa Clara County, California · Last checked November 3, 2026

Quick Facts

Serves: San José and all of Santa Clara County, California
Phone: (408) 275-8770
Official site: www.scchousingauthority.org/
Apply: SCCHA application page
Status last checked: November 3, 2026

The Santa Clara County Housing Authority (SCCHA) runs Section 8 for San José and the rest of Santa Clara County. The County Board of Supervisors created it in 1967 as the Housing Authority of the County of Santa Clara. HUD lists it as PHA CA059 with a program type of Combined, at 505 West Julian Street in San José. San José has no housing authority office of its own. HUD still lists a separate legal agency, the Housing Authority of the City of San Jose (CA056, Section 8 only), but at SCCHA's address and phone number, because SCCHA says it "has an agreement with the City of San José to administer and manage the Housing Authority of the City of San José (HACSJ)'s Housing Choice Voucher program." The two share one set of waiting lists, so you apply once, to SCCHA. SCCHA says more than 19,179 households have a voucher. The short version today: SCCHA does not open and close a list like most authorities. Its online interest lists are open all the time, and when vouchers become available it draws names at random. This page covers SCCHA's exact words, how to stay active, the residency preference, and SCCHA's own apartment buildings, which have separate applications.

Waiting-List Status

Waiting lists open and close frequently, so treat the status below as a starting point and confirm on the official site before you rely on it.

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8) interest lists: Open to sign-ups at any time — Open (checked November 3, 2026). SCCHA's Applicant Portal page says: "our new online interest lists are open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week." Signing up is not the same as being on the waiting list: "If rental assistance is available, applicants will be chosen randomly and added to the waiting list. Not everyone is guaranteed housing." SCCHA's New Applicants page says there are two interest lists you may sign up to and that more than 37,000 people are on them. To stay active, log in to the portal at least once a year.
  • SCCHA-owned apartments: Varies by property — These buildings are not part of the voucher program. SCCHA says they are managed by The John Stewart Company and FPI Management, and to contact each property manager directly. On November 3, 2026, SCCHA's site listed two new San José buildings as now leasing, Meridian Family Apartments and Hawthorn Senior (55 and older), with no closing date posted; apply through the property links on SCCHA's Properties page.
  • Public housing: Closed, per SCCHA's FY2026 report — SCCHA has only four public housing units left. Its FY2026 Moving to Work report, covering the year that ended June 30, 2026, lists the public housing site-based list as "Closed" and not opened during the year. SCCHA's website posts no public housing opening.

Programs SCCHA Runs

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8) for the County and for the City of San José, under one Moving to Work program since 2008
  • Project-Based Vouchers, including units for people referred through the County's Coordinated Entry System
  • More than 2,900 affordable apartments SCCHA owns or controls, run by third-party property managers with their own applications
  • A handful of remaining public housing units (four, per SCCHA's FY2026 Moving to Work report)
  • Family Self-Sufficiency program for voucher households

How to Apply

Apply directly through the authority's official application page: www.scchousingauthority.org/section-8/for-participants/for-new-applicants/applicant-portal/. If you have already signed up, there is no place in line to check, because SCCHA chooses at random from its interest lists; log in to the SCCHA portal at least once a year to show you are still interested, and keep your email address and phone current, because SCCHA sends status updates by email or text.

Other Authorities Serving This Area

  • There is no separate San José housing authority to apply to. HUD's California list shows the Housing Authority of the City of San Jose (CA056) at 505 West Julian Street with SCCHA's phone, (408) 275-8770, and SCCHA's FY2026 report describes a Moving to Work activity called "Combined Waiting Lists for the County of Santa Clara and the City of San José." One sign-up with SCCHA covers both.
  • City of San José Housing Department: SCCHA's New Applicants page points to housing.sanjoseca.gov for other affordable apartments, and the Meridian lease-up takes applications through the City's listing. These are income-restricted apartments, not Section 8.
  • If you are homeless, SCCHA says it receives referrals for its special voucher programs through the County's Coordinated Entry System. SCCHA lists access points including HomeFirst's Homeless Helpline, (408) 510-7600, and the Bill Wilson Center, (408) 243-0222. Frequently Asked Questions

Is the San José Section 8 waiting list open?

You can sign up any time, but it is a random draw, not a line. SCCHA, which runs Section 8 for San José, says "our new online interest lists are open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week" (checked November 3, 2026). When rental assistance is available, SCCHA chooses applicants at random from the interest lists and adds them to the waiting list, and it says "Not everyone is guaranteed housing." Sign up at portal.scchousingauthority.org; SCCHA says it takes about three minutes.

Does San José have its own housing authority?

Not one you apply to separately. HUD still lists a Housing Authority of the City of San Jose (CA056), but at SCCHA's address and phone number. SCCHA says it has an agreement with the City of San José to administer and manage that agency's Housing Choice Voucher program, and the County and City share one set of waiting lists. If you live in San José, you apply to SCCHA.

Does signing up early help my chances?

No. SCCHA picks people at random from its interest lists when vouchers become available, so the date you signed up does not move you ahead. What matters is staying active: SCCHA says "Logging in at least once a year tells us you are still interested in receiving a Housing Choice Voucher." Keep your email address and phone current in the portal, because SCCHA sends status updates by email or text.

Do I have to live in Santa Clara County to apply?

No, but locals get a preference. SCCHA says "You do not have to be a resident of Santa Clara County to apply, although preference will be given to those who have lived or worked in the county within the last five years." You must be 18 or older, be legally documented in the U.S., and be under SCCHA's income limits, which as of May 1, 2026 are $71,950 for one person and $102,750 for a household of four.

What are SCCHA's office hours and phone number?

SCCHA's main number is (408) 275-8770, and its TDD line is (408) 993-3041. Both are on every page of its website, and HUD lists the same main number. The lobby at 505 West Julian Street, San José, is open Monday through Thursday from 7:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., and every other Friday from 7:30 a.m. to 4 p.m. SCCHA posts its closure dates on its site; the next ones it listed were November 6 and November 11, 2026.
