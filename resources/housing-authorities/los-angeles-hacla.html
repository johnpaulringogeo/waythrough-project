Housing Authority

Housing Authority of the City of Los Angeles (HACLA)

Serves the City of Los Angeles, California · Last checked October 15, 2026

Waythrough is an independent resource, not HACLA. We summarize public information to help you get oriented. For applications and the most current waiting-list status, always use the official links below.

The Housing Authority of the City of Los Angeles (HACLA) runs Section 8 Housing Choice Vouchers and public housing for the City of Los Angeles. Unincorporated LA County and many smaller cities are served by a different agency, the Los Angeles County Development Authority (LACDA). We checked HACLA's own site on October 15, 2026: the Section 8 waiting list is closed, but the public housing waiting list takes online applications through RentCafe, except 1-bedroom applications. HACLA's phone number is (833) 422-5248, which HACLA spells 833-HACLA-4-U.

Waiting-List Status

Waiting lists open and close frequently, so treat the status below as a starting point and confirm on the official site before you rely on it.

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8): Closed — HACLA's Section 8 page says: "The Section 8 Waiting List is currently closed." (checked October 15, 2026). HACLA's FAQ adds that the list "only accepts new applicants during a specific window of time" and that when it opens, "it will be widely publicized via newspaper, television, radio, social media, etc. and the HACLA website will be updated." The same FAQ describes the list as "closed and unlikely to reopen soon." The last opening was an online lottery that took applications from 6:00 a.m. on October 17 to 5:00 p.m. on October 30, 2022. HACLA received 223,375 applications and used "a computer-randomized lottery to select up to 30,000 applicants," with a preference for people who live or work in the City of Los Angeles and for veterans. If you are already on the list, HACLA says "your wait time is dependent upon funding and the availability of vouchers." HACLA moved Section 8 participants to the Rent Café portal on January 1, 2026, for annual reexaminations and documents; that portal is for current participants and does not reopen the waiting list. Our guide to Section 8 lotteries explains how these openings work.
  • Public Housing: Open through RentCafe, except 1-bedroom applications — HACLA's FAQ says: "The Public Housing Waiting List is open" and "Please note HACLA is not accepting 1-bedroom applications for Public Housing at this time." (checked October 15, 2026). It is one citywide list for all of HACLA's public housing developments. HACLA says "Applicants cannot apply for a specific site," and that placement "is based on date/time of the application and family eligibility status." Preferences go to households whose head or co-head works at least 20 hours a week, is a full-time student, is a veteran or active member of the military, or is disabled or 62 or older, and families of veterans get priority within each preference. You get up to three unit offers at three different sites and have five business days to answer each one; refusing all three removes you from the list. HACLA says you may be on this list and the Section 8 list at the same time. How to apply is below.
  • Project-based vouchers and HACLA's other buildings: No public list for project-based vouchers; HACLA's other buildings take applications one by one — HACLA's FAQ says its Project-Based Voucher units have "No Waiting List; PBV vouchers are available through referrals made from the Los Angeles Homeless Services Authority (LAHSA) through partner organizations or LAHSA service centers." Separately, HACLA's Asset Management department oversees more than 2,500 apartments that are not public housing: market-rate, income-restricted and senior buildings. HACLA says "Our Asset Management properties each have different eligibility criteria" and that to apply you should "contact the Site Manager and/or the Property Management Company directly." HACLA's Online Recertification and Waitlists page lists 12 of these buildings, each with its own page; Apperson Apartments, for example, has a RentCafe "Sign up for Waitlist" link. HACLA also posts a current vacancy list on its home page. If a site manager does not answer, Asset Management is at (213) 252-8840 or assetmanagement@hacla.org.
  • Homeless and specialty vouchers (HUD-VASH, EHV, Stability Vouchers, supportive housing): By referral only — These are not open to public sign-up. HACLA says it works with the County Departments of Mental Health and Health Services for referrals to its Homeless Program, partners with the West L.A. VA Medical Center for HUD-VASH, and fills its Stability Vouchers through "direct referrals from the CoC." HACLA's FAQ says it "does not offer homeless services directly to individuals" and points people to 2-1-1 for LA County, 311 for the City, and LAHSA. The HOPWA tenant-based program for people living with HIV/AIDS is "Currently closed for new applicants."

Programs HACLA Runs

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8)
  • Public Housing
  • Project-Based Vouchers (PBV)
  • HUD-VASH (for homeless veterans, with the West L.A. VA Medical Center)
  • Homeless and supportive-housing vouchers: Homeless Program, Tenant-Based Supportive Housing, Continuum of Care, Emergency Housing Vouchers and Stability Vouchers
  • Family Unification Program (FUP) and Mainstream vouchers
  • Housing Opportunities for Persons with AIDS (HOPWA)
  • Family Self-Sufficiency (FSS)

How to Apply

Apply directly through the authority's official application page: www.hacla.org/en/about-public-housing/public-housing-applicants. If you have already applied, call (833) 422-5248 to verify your position and keep your contact information current — if the authority cannot reach you when your name comes up, you can be dropped from the list.

Other Authorities Serving This Area

  • Los Angeles County Development Authority (LACDA) serves unincorporated LA County and many smaller cities, with its own lists. Its Section 8 list is closed to public registration; LACDA says the program is "open to prioritize and continually accept referrals from a Coordinated Entry System (CES) and/or other partnering agencies for families that are homeless." LACDA's public housing site-based waiting lists took registrations from August 16 to September 16, 2026, and its How To Apply page now reads "WAITING LISTS FOR ELDERLY AND GENERAL OCCUPANCY PUBLIC HOUSING SITES ARE CLOSED" (checked October 15, 2026). LACDA: (626) 262-4511.
  • Other LA-area authorities with separate waiting lists: Long Beach (see our Long Beach HACLB page), Pasadena, Burbank, Glendale, Compton, Inglewood, and Pomona.
  • What to do while the Section 8 list is closed. Apply for HACLA public housing through RentCafe now (HACLA is not taking 1-bedroom applications at the moment), and ask about HACLA's other buildings listed above. Apply to LIHTC (tax credit) apartments building by building; see how to find LIHTC housing. If you are behind on rent or have an eviction notice, call or text 2-1-1, LA County's 24/7 line, to be screened for prevention programs, and call Stay Housed LA at 1-888-694-0040 (stayhousedla.org) for free eviction legal help and connections to rental assistance. LA County's 2026 Emergency Rent Relief round closed on March 11, 2026; watch lacounty.gov/prevention for a new one. If you are homeless, 2-1-1 is also the way into Coordinated Entry, which is how HACLA's and LACDA's homeless vouchers are filled. Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Los Angeles (HACLA) Section 8 waiting list open?

No. HACLA's Section 8 page says: "The Section 8 Waiting List is currently closed." (checked October 15, 2026). HACLA's FAQ says the list "only accepts new applicants during a specific window of time" and that an opening will be announced in the news, on social media and on hacla.org. The last opening was an online lottery from October 17 to October 30, 2022, when HACLA received 223,375 applications and picked up to 30,000 for the list. HACLA public housing is a separate list that is taking online applications, except 1-bedroom applications.

How do I apply for HACLA public housing?

Online through RentCafe. HACLA says: "To apply for the Public Housing Program online you must register for a RentCafe account with a valid email address." After you submit, you get a Registration Code; keep it, because you need it to update your application later. You can also request a paper application at application.help@hacla.org or from HACLA's Customer Contact Center at (833) 422-5248. HACLA's FAQ says it is not accepting 1-bedroom applications at this time, and you cannot choose a specific development (checked October 15, 2026).

What is HACLA's phone number?

(833) 422-5248, which HACLA also writes as 833-HACLA-4-U. HACLA's contact page lists it for the main office, the Section 8 program and the Public Housing Application Center, and HACLA's FAQ gives the same number for checking the status of a Section 8 or public housing application. Hours are 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, and the TDD line is (213) 252-5313. HACLA's Asset Management department, which handles its buildings that are not public housing, is (213) 252-8840. The older (213) 252-2500 number is not listed on HACLA's contact page (checked October 15, 2026).

What is the difference between HACLA and LACDA?

HACLA serves the City of Los Angeles. The Los Angeles County Development Authority (LACDA) serves unincorporated LA County and many smaller cities, and runs its own lists. LACDA's Section 8 list is closed to public registration but takes referrals for homeless families through the Coordinated Entry System. Its public housing site-based lists took registrations from August 16 to September 16, 2026, and are now closed (checked October 15, 2026). LACDA's number is (626) 262-4511.

Can I get a HACLA voucher if I am homeless?

Not by signing up directly. HACLA's FAQ says it "does not offer homeless services directly to individuals." Its homeless and supportive-housing vouchers, including HUD-VASH for veterans, Stability Vouchers and project-based supportive housing, are filled by referral from LAHSA, the County health and mental health departments, the VA and the Continuum of Care. Start by calling or texting 2-1-1; veterans should also contact the VA.

What can I do while the HACLA Section 8 list is closed?

Apply for HACLA public housing through RentCafe, and apply to HACLA's other buildings and to LIHTC (tax credit) apartments one building at a time. If you are behind on rent or got an eviction notice, call or text 2-1-1 to be screened for LA County prevention programs, and call Stay Housed LA at 1-888-694-0040 for free legal help and connections to rental assistance. Keep your contact information current on every list you join, because HACLA removes applicants who do not respond.
