The Indianapolis Housing Agency is the public housing agency for Indianapolis and Marion County, listed by HUD as PHA IN017 with a program type of Combined. Its board has been HUD appointed since a Cooperative Endeavor Agreement signed on April 10, 2024, under which HUD says it "retains ultimate authority over IHA's recovery." The news is the November 2026 waiting-list opening. In a press statement dated November 2, 2026, IHA announced that its Housing Choice Voucher, Public Housing and Project-Based waitlists "will open on Monday, November 16, 2026, at 7:00 a.m." The voucher list was a lottery with a hard deadline of Friday, November 20, 2026, at 5:00 p.m., and that window is now closed. The public housing and project-based lists had no end date: IHA said they close when its application limit is reached. As of November 25, 2026, IHA had posted no lottery results and no closing notice for those two lists, and its Apply Now page, which used to link the online Applicant Portal, was blank. So call IHA's application help line, 317-261-7224, before you count on applying (checked November 25, 2026). IHA's older voucher FAQ still describes the Section 8 list as closed since a 2016 lottery.

Waiting-List Status

Waiting lists open and close often. The status below is what we found the last time we checked; confirm it on the official site before you count on it.

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8): Closed. The lottery application window ran from Monday, November 16, 2026, 7:00 a.m., to Friday, November 20, 2026, 5:00 p.m. (checked November 25, 2026). IHA's press statement of November 2, 2026, in its own words: "IHA will accept applications for the Housing Choice Voucher Program through a lottery process. The application period will open on Monday, November 16, 2026, at 7:00 a.m., and close Friday, November 20, 2026, at 5:00 p.m. Applications must be submitted before the closing deadline to be considered for the lottery process." That deadline has passed, so this window is over, and IHA has not announced another one. As of November 25, 2026, IHA had posted no lottery results and nothing on next steps; its news page has nothing newer on the waitlists than the November 2 statement (checked November 25, 2026). Being in the lottery is not the same as getting a voucher. IHA says "Opening a waitlist does not guarantee placement or the immediate availability of housing assistance," and it has not published how many applicants it will select or when. If you applied, keep your mailing address, phone and email current with IHA, because IHA contacts applicants by mail. Questions about an application: 317-261-7224. One caution in our own voice: IHA's HCV FAQ page still says the Section 8 list is closed and points to the 2016 lottery. That page is older than the November 2026 lottery.
  • Public Housing (Concord/Eagle Creek, John J. Barton Towers, John J. Barton Annex): Opened Monday, November 16, 2026, at 7:00 a.m.; closes when IHA's application limit is reached. No closing notice posted as of November 25, 2026. These are the three communities named in IHA's press statement. In IHA's words: "The Public Housing Waitlist will close once the established application limit has been reached. Applicants are encouraged to submit their applications as early as possible, as the waitlist may close at any time after opening." There is no fixed end date. On November 25, 2026, IHA's news page showed no closing notice, but IHA's Apply Now page, which used to link the Applicant Portal, was blank (checked November 25, 2026). We cannot tell from IHA's site whether this list is still taking applications, so call 317-261-7224 before you try to apply. Applications for this list go through the same online Applicant Portal as the voucher lottery. The Georgetown is not on IHA's list for this opening. Once you are on a list, IHA says it does not disclose your position, will contact you by mail when you are pulled, and estimates "anywhere from six (6) months up to four (4) years." Community office numbers, if you have a question about a property: Concord and Eagle Creek (317) 261-7344, John J. Barton Annex (317) 261-7213, John J. Barton Tower (317) 261-7447.
  • Project-Based Housing (The Martindale, Indiana Avenue Apartments): Opened Monday, November 16, 2026, at 7:00 a.m.; closes when IHA's application limit is reached. No closing notice posted as of November 25, 2026. IHA's press statement: "Applications will also be accepted for the following Project-Based Housing properties: The Martindale, Indiana Avenue Apartments. The Project-Based Housing Waitlist will close once the established application limit has been reached. Applicants are encouraged to apply as early as possible." Same Applicant Portal, same help line (317-261-7224), and the same status as public housing: no end date, no closing notice on IHA's site as of November 25, 2026, and an Apply Now page that no longer links the portal (checked November 25, 2026). Call before you try to apply. Property office numbers: Indiana Avenue Apartments (317) 261-7221, The Martindale (317) 261-7296.

Programs IHA Runs

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8)
  • Public Housing, leased directly by IHA at its communities
  • Project-Based Vouchers and project-based rental assistance at RAD converted properties
  • Resident programs and services at IHA communities

How to Apply

Apply on the authority's own application page: www.indyhousing.org/apply-now. If you have already applied, IHA does not disclose your position on the list and says it will contact you by mail once you are pulled, so keep your mailing address current with IHA.

If this is your first time applying, our step-by-step guide to applying for Section 8 covers each stage, and how to find your PHA shows which authority covers where you live.

Other Authorities Serving This Area

  • The Indiana Housing and Community Development Authority (IHCDA) is the state agency, HUD code IN901, at 30 S. Meridian St., Indianapolis, (317) 232-1257, s8waitlist@ihcda.in.gov. It administers Section 8 in Indiana counties that IHA does not cover, and it is a different agency from IHA despite sharing a city.
  • If you live just outside Marion County, check with the state rather than assuming. HUD's Indiana report contains no separate entries for Beech Grove, Lawrence, Speedway, Southport, Carmel or Hamilton County. That is an absence in HUD's list, not a statement that IHA covers those places, and IHA's township list naming Lawrence refers to Lawrence Township rather than the separately incorporated city. Ask IHCDA where you fall.
  • Two IHA portal addresses no longer work. applicant.indyhousing.org and portal.indyhousing.org/WebApp/Default.aspx both fail to load. The applicant portal IHA used for the November 2026 opening was a RentCafe portal linked from its Apply Now page; on November 25, 2026 that page was blank and no longer linked it. The portal is an account gate rather than a status page: it shows no waiting-list information before you register.
  • IHA is not an emergency rent fund. If you are behind on rent, the public door is your township trustee: each Marion County township runs its own township assistance office, and Center Township is at 317-633-3610. We could not confirm that the IndyRent rental assistance program is taking applications (checked November 3, 2026). See our Indianapolis emergency rent help guide: township trustee help, IndyEAP, the City's Tenant Information Hotline, free legal aid, and Indiana's 10-day notice.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Indianapolis Section 8 waiting list open?

No. IHA's Section 8 lottery window has closed. Its press statement of November 2, 2026 said the Housing Choice Voucher application period "will open on Monday, November 16, 2026, at 7:00 a.m., and close Friday, November 20, 2026, at 5:00 p.m." and that "Applications must be submitted before the closing deadline to be considered for the lottery process." That deadline has passed. As of November 25, 2026, IHA had not posted lottery results or announced the next opening (checked November 25, 2026). If you applied, keep your mailing address current with IHA; questions about an application go to 317-261-7224. IHA's older HCV FAQ page still says the list is closed and refers to the 2016 lottery.

Which public housing and project-based lists opened, and are they still open?

IHA's November 2, 2026 press statement names three Public Housing communities (Concord/Eagle Creek, John J. Barton Towers and John J. Barton Annex) and two Project-Based Housing properties (The Martindale and Indiana Avenue Apartments). Both lists opened Monday, November 16, 2026, at 7:00 a.m., with no end date. IHA says the Public Housing Waitlist "will close once the established application limit has been reached" and "may close at any time after opening." As of November 25, 2026, IHA had posted no closing notice for either list, but its Apply Now page no longer links to the Applicant Portal (checked November 25, 2026). Call 317-261-7224 to find out whether a list is still taking applications before you try. Earlier this year we could not confirm a claimed March 2026 public housing opening on IHA's site, and we still do not repeat it.

How do I actually apply for public housing here?

For the lists that opened November 16, 2026, online only. IHA's press statement says "Applications must be submitted online through the IHA Applicant Portal at www.indyhousing.org" and that you must "first register for an account before signing in and completing the applicable application." The portal was linked from IHA's Apply Now page, but on November 25, 2026 that page was blank, so call 317-261-7224 to ask how to reach the portal and whether the list is still open; applicants with disabilities who need help can also use 317.261.7299. When no list is open, IHA's FAQ routes public housing questions to the individual communities: Concord and Eagle Creek (317) 261-7344, Indiana Avenue Apartments (317) 261-7221, John J. Barton Annex (317) 261-7213, John J. Barton Tower (317) 261-7447, The Georgetown (317) 261-7245, and The Martindale (317) 261-7296.

Is IHA run by HUD now, and does that change anything for me?

The board is HUD appointed, and that is as far as the documentation goes. IHA's Board of Commissioners page lists its members under the heading "Commissioners (HUD Appointed)" and publishes a full 2026 meeting schedule, so it is current. HUD's own April 2024 release describes a Cooperative Endeavor Agreement signed April 10, 2024 with the City of Indianapolis under which "HUD retains ultimate authority over IHA's recovery," with HUD's Indiana Field Office Director as board chair and a City-appointed Recovery Monitor. What we cannot tell you is whether anything changed for applicants: nothing on indyhousing.org directs applicants to HUD or to a third-party administrator, and intake still runs through IHA staff and its RentCafe portal. IHA's current CEO is Yvonda A. Bean.

What number should I call, and what is the right address?

Call 317.261.7200. It appears in body content on IHA's Help Center office locator and is used consistently across the site. The phone number in HUD's directory for IHA (IN017) is outdated: it is really IHA's fax line, so use 317.261.7200 instead. There is no separate Section 8 line. Other useful numbers: landlords 317.261.7201, applicants who need disability assistance 317.261.7299, and rental listings 1-877-428-8844. On the address, IHA's own pages disagree: the Help Center and About pages give 1919 N. Meridian Street, while the Board of Commissioners page and the RentCafe portal footer give 1935 N. Meridian Street. We publish 1919 as the main office and leave the discrepancy visible rather than quietly picking one. Office hours are Monday through Friday, 8:00 am to 5:00 pm.

Are there preferences that would move me up the list?

IHA does not publish any, and we are not going to import generic language to fill the gap. No IHA page lists preference categories or a points schedule for either program. What it does publish is screening criteria: income limits, criminal history, and no lifetime sex offender registration. On timing, IHA says "It may take up to four (4) years to be pulled from the waiting list and receive assistance," and elsewhere gives a range of six months to four years. IHA does not disclose your position on the list, and says that once you are pulled it will contact you by mail.

How current are the income limits IHA publishes?

It depends which page you land on, which is the whole problem. IHA's "Am I Eligible" page carries a table labeled FY 2025, which is one year behind as of September 2026. The Housing Choice Voucher FAQ is far worse: it says a family of four in Marion County can earn up to $32,150 to count as Very Low Income or $51,450 as Low Income, and labels those figures "(effective July 2014)." Do not budget against either set. HUD republishes income limits annually, and the reliable move is to call 317.261.7200 and ask what limits IHA is applying to your household size right now.
