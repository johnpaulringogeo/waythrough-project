Quick Facts

Phone: (216) 348-5000

Status last checked: November 3, 2026

The Cuyahoga Metropolitan Housing Authority (CMHA) is the public housing agency for Cleveland and the rest of Cuyahoga County. It is not the Columbus Metropolitan Housing Authority, which also calls itself CMHA but is a separate agency for Franklin County, with its own lists and phone numbers. HUD lists the Cuyahoga agency as PHA OH003 with a program type of Combined, so it runs both Housing Choice Vouchers and public housing. CMHA says it was chartered in 1933 as the first public housing authority in the country and counts about 15,000 vouchers and 10,000 units across 60 properties. Its Main Campus is at 8120 Kinsman Road, Cleveland, OH 44104. The short version today: the voucher list never closes. CMHA takes preliminary applications at any time, picks from them by lottery, and lets each application expire after 18 months. This page covers CMHA's exact words on that, the project-based and public housing lists, which phone number to call, CMHA's preferences, and the other agencies around Cleveland that people mix up with CMHA.

Waiting-List Status

Waiting lists open and close frequently, so treat the status below as a starting point and confirm on the official site before you rely on it.

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8): Open year-round, with no closing date. Checked November 3, 2026. CMHA's Housing Choice Voucher page says: "CMHA is now accepting year-round preliminary applications for Housing Choice Vouchers" and "Lottery-style selections from the preliminary applications will be ongoing, and the waiting list will have no closing date." The same page adds: "Preliminary applications are accepted at any time!" Selection is random: "CMHA will select applications through multiple mini lotteries from the preliminary application database," and "your name may come up within a few days, or it may take months." Each preliminary application "will be active for 18 months. If your application expires before you're selected, you can immediately submit another one." Applying early does not put you ahead.
  • Project-Based Vouchers: Some property lists open, for applicants who meet set criteria. CMHA's Project Based Vouchers page says: "Several project-based waiting lists remain open with preferences for families meeting certain criteria." It names two: Chronically Homeless (a person with a disabling condition, or an adult in a homeless family, who has been homeless for a year or more or at least four times in three years) and Fairfax Intergenerational (adults 55 and older with legal custody of one or more minor children). You apply through the same CMHA applicant portal, and CMHA's PBV and Mod Rehab Property List shows the buildings (checked November 3, 2026).
  • Public housing (CMHA Properties): No open-or-closed status posted; a list of currently leasing properties dated February 20, 2026. CMHA's CMHA Properties page gives no open or closed status. It says "Approximate waitlist times vary by bedroom size, type of unit (family vs. high rise) and admission preferences," and that an Intake Specialist will contact you when your name reaches the top. CMHA's "Currently Leasing Properties" guide, which says "This list was updated on 2/20/26," names 11 properties: one-bedroom apartments at Addison Square, Ambleside, Apthorp Tower (East Cleveland), Bohn Tower, Riverview Tower and Springbrook High Rise; one-bedrooms and some studios at Scranton Manhattan; studios and one-bedrooms in the Bellaire Gardens high-rises and three- and four-bedroom Bellaire Wanda Townhomes; two- to five-bedroom apartments at King Kennedy Family; and one- to four-bedroom apartments at Olde Cedar and Outhwaite Homes. Several converted properties have their own RentCafe application link in that guide; the rest use CMHA's applicant portal. Because the guide is from February, call the CMHA Properties Applications Team at (216) 361-3700 before you count on a building (checked November 3, 2026).

Programs CMHA Runs

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8), about 15,000 vouchers used across Cuyahoga County
  • Project-Based Vouchers in privately owned buildings, where the subsidy stays with the unit
  • Public housing and converted (RAD) CMHA properties, about 10,000 units across 60 properties, from high-rises to scattered single-family houses
  • HUD-VASH vouchers for homeless veterans referred by the VA, and Family Unification Program vouchers referred by Cuyahoga County Children and Family Services
  • Family Self-Sufficiency and other resident services

How to Apply

Apply directly through the authority's official application page: applications-cmha.securecafe.com/onlineleasing/cuyahoga-metropolitan-housing-authority/guestlogin.aspx. If you have already applied, there is no place in line to check, because CMHA draws from its preliminary applications by lottery; keep your email, phone, address and income current in the CMHA applicant portal, and if your application expires after 18 months without being picked, CMHA says you can submit a new one right away.

New to the process? Our step-by-step guide to applying for Section 8 walks through the whole thing, and how to find your PHA helps you locate the right authority.

Other Authorities Serving This Area

  • Columbus Metropolitan Housing Authority, HUD code OH001, is the other Ohio "CMHA." It serves Franklin County and central Ohio, not Cleveland, and has its own waiting lists and portal; applying to one does nothing at the other. See our CMHA Columbus page.
  • Parma Public Housing Agency, HUD code OH073, program type Section 8, is a separate voucher agency inside Cuyahoga County, at 1440 Rockside Rd., Parma, OH 44134, (216) 661-2015 (HUD's Ohio list). It now goes by North Coast Housing Connections (NCHC), and its page on the City of Parma's website says it administers 802 Housing Choice Vouchers throughout Cuyahoga County and that its voucher waiting list "is currently closed and not open for applications" (checked November 3, 2026).
  • Lake Metropolitan Housing Authority, HUD code OH025, program type Combined, serves Lake County to the east from 189 First Street, Painesville, (440) 354-3347. Lorain Metropolitan Housing Authority, HUD code OH012, serves Lorain County to the west from 1600 Kansas Avenue, Lorain, (440) 288-1600. Both numbers are from HUD's Ohio list.
  • CMHA is not an emergency rent fund. A voucher picked by lottery will not arrive in time to stop an eviction. If you are behind on rent, Cuyahoga County's Prevention, Retention and Contingency program helps families with children (up to $1,500 a year), and CHN Housing Partners' Family Stability Initiative says it will reopen on Monday, December 7 at 8 a.m. (checked November 4, 2026). See our Cleveland emergency rent help guide: PRC and CHN, United Way 211, Cleveland's free Right to Counsel lawyers, Pay to Stay, and Ohio's 3-day notice.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Cleveland (CMHA) Section 8 waiting list open?

Yes, and it does not close. As of November 3, 2026, CMHA's Housing Choice Voucher page says CMHA "is now accepting year-round preliminary applications" and that "the waiting list will have no closing date." Applying puts you into a lottery pool, not a line: CMHA selects by "multiple mini lotteries," so someone who applied last year has no edge over you. Your preliminary application stays active for 18 months, and if it expires before you are picked, CMHA says you can immediately submit another one. Keep your email, phone, address and income current in the portal, because that is how CMHA reaches you.

How do I apply to CMHA for a Housing Choice Voucher?

Online, through CMHA's applicant portal, which is the Apply button on CMHA's Housing Choice Voucher page. You need an active private email account, because CMHA sends every notice by email, plus your Social Security number, the names and dates of birth of everyone in your household, and each member's income. CMHA says you can use the kiosks and computers at its Main Campus, 8120 Kinsman Road, and borrow internet hotspots from the Cleveland Public Library or the Cuyahoga County Public Library. If you applied in earlier years, CMHA says you need to reapply. CMHA's own FAQ still points to cmha.net/apply, but that address showed a "not found" page on November 3, 2026, so use the Apply button instead.

Can I apply for CMHA public housing right now?

CMHA does not post an open or closed status for its properties. What it does post is a "Currently Leasing Properties" guide, last updated February 20, 2026, that lists 11 properties, mostly one-bedroom high-rise apartments, plus family units at King Kennedy Family (two to five bedrooms), Olde Cedar and Outhwaite Homes (one to four bedrooms) and the Bellaire Wanda Townhomes (three and four bedrooms). Apply through the link for that property or through CMHA's applicant portal. Because the guide is months old, call CMHA's Properties Applications Team at (216) 361-3700 to confirm a building is still leasing.

Is Cleveland's CMHA the same agency as CMHA in Columbus?

No. The Cuyahoga Metropolitan Housing Authority, HUD code OH003, serves Cleveland and Cuyahoga County. The Columbus Metropolitan Housing Authority, HUD code OH001, serves Franklin County. They share initials and nothing else: separate waiting lists, separate portals and separate phone numbers, and applying to one does not put you on the other. Inside Cuyahoga County there is also a small separate voucher agency, the Parma Public Housing Agency, HUD code OH073.

Does CMHA give some applicants priority?

Yes. CMHA's 2025 PHA Plan ranks its voucher preferences by points: 50 points for families cut from CMHA's voucher program for lack of funding; 40 points for families displaced from CMHA properties, disaster victims, VAWA emergency transfers, homeless veterans referred by the VA for HUD-VASH, Ohio Home Choice referrals, people ready to move on from permanent supportive housing, and Mobility Demonstration families; and 15 points for Family Unification referrals. For public housing, the plan lists ten high-priority preferences, including homeless families with children, involuntary displacement and youth who aged out of foster care, and three standard ones: homeless; working families and people unable to work because of age or disability; and veterans and their families.

Which CMHA phone number should I call?

For a voucher application, call the Applications Division at (216) 431-1471, option 6, or the Client Information Center at (216) 431-1471, option 1. CMHA's Housing Choice Voucher page gives both, and (216) 431-1471 is also the number on CMHA's applicant portal and its Contact page. For CMHA properties, the Contact page lists the Properties Applications Team at (216) 361-3700. CMHA's main number is (216) 348-5000, on its Contact page, in the footer of every CMHA page and in HUD's directory for OH003. Office hours are Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.
