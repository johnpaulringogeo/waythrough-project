Status last checked: November 3, 2026

The Mesa Housing Authority is part of the City of Mesa. HUD lists it as PHA AZ005, the City of Mesa Housing Authority, with a program type of Section 8, and the City's accommodation notices call the office City of Mesa Housing and Community Development. Mesa is a voucher-only agency: its FY 2026/2027 Annual Plan lists 1,642 Housing Choice Vouchers and zero public housing units, so there is no Mesa public housing list to join. The office is at 200 S. Center St., Building 1. The short version today: the Section 8 wait list is closed, and Mesa's applicant portal shows no open lists. This page covers Mesa's exact words, what happened at the last opening in August 2022, how the lottery and the two preferences work, how Mesa says it will announce the next opening, and the county authority people often confuse with Mesa.

Waiting-List Status

Waiting lists open and close frequently, so treat the status below as a starting point and confirm on the official site before you rely on it.

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8): Closed — Closed (checked November 3, 2026). Mesa's Application & Wait List page reads: "The Section 8 Housing Choice Voucher Program Wait List application is currently closed." Mesa's applicant portal, mesa.myhousing.com, says: "There are currently no waiting lists open." Mesa gives no reopening date. The last opening Mesa documents ran from August 17 to August 30, 2022: the City's Housing Services Governing Board minutes of September 8, 2022 report 20,110 applications and a lottery that picked 4,000 of them for the list.
  • Public Housing: None in Mesa — Mesa does not own or run public housing. Its FY 2026/2027 Annual Plan lists "Number of Public Housing (PH) Units 0" (checked November 3, 2026). For income-restricted apartments in Mesa, see the Housing Authority of Maricopa County below and HUD's LIHTC database.
  • Project-Based Vouchers (La Mesita Apartments, Escobedo at Verde Vista Phase II): Referral only — Mesa's Project-Based Voucher page lists both properties as "Referral Only" and says applicants must be chronically homeless and elderly, disabled, or a family needing supportive services (checked November 3, 2026). Adults without children can start at the Brian Garcia Welcome Center, 602-229-5155; families with minor children can call the Family Housing Hub at 602-595-8700.
  • HUD-VASH and Family Unification Program (FUP): Referral only — For HUD-VASH, Mesa sends veterans to the VA Community Resource & Referral Center, 1500 E. Thomas Rd., Suite 106, Phoenix, 602-248-6040 or 602-568-7843. For FUP, Mesa says to contact your caseworker at the Arizona Department of Child Safety (checked November 3, 2026).

Programs the Mesa Housing Authority Runs

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8), 1,642 vouchers in the FY 2026/2027 Annual Plan
  • Project-Based Vouchers at La Mesita Apartments and Escobedo at Verde Vista Phase II, by referral only
  • HUD-VASH vouchers for homeless veterans, by referral from the VA in Phoenix
  • Family Unification Program (FUP) vouchers, by referral from the Arizona Department of Child Safety
  • Family Self-Sufficiency (FSS) for current voucher holders
  • No public housing: the Annual Plan lists zero public housing units

How to Apply

Apply directly through the authority's official application page: www.mesaaz.gov/Resident-Resources/Housing/Application-Wait-List. If you are already on the wait list, Mesa says it will contact you only by mail, so report any change of address, phone number, income or household members within 10 business days through the Applicant Portal; for wait list questions, Mesa lists 480-644-3536 (press 2) and hcvwaitinglist@mesaaz.gov.

New to the process? Our step-by-step guide to applying for Section 8 walks through the whole thing, and how to find your PHA helps you locate the right authority.

Other Authorities Serving This Area

  • Housing Authority of Maricopa County (HAMC) is a separate county agency, HUD code AZ009, program type Combined, at 8910 N. 78th Ave., Peoria, (602) 744-4500. Its Wait List Schedule, dated September 15, 2026, shows its Housing Choice Voucher list "Closed - August 25, 2023" and its Clare Feldstadt Apartments in Mesa as "Open - Until filled." But an HAMC public notice says it is closing all of its open property wait lists, for all bedroom sizes, effective 8:00 a.m. on December 1, 2026 (Arizona time); after that, those lists are closed until further notice, so check maricopahousing.org for the next opening (checked November 3, 2026).
  • City of Phoenix Housing Department, HUD code AZ001, runs its own separate lists for Phoenix. See our Phoenix Housing Department page.
  • Next door, HUD's Arizona list shows separate agencies for Tempe (Tempe Housing Authority, AZ031, (480) 350-8950) and Chandler (Chandler Housing & Redevelopment Division, AZ028, (480) 782-3200). Each has its own list, so applying to Mesa does not put you on theirs.
  • The Housing Authority is not an emergency rent fund. Mesa's Security & Utility Deposit Assistance Program is now run by City of Mesa Human Services, not the Housing Authority. It pays the refundable part of a deposit for a unit inside Mesa, for households at or below 80% of the area median income, and you must apply before you sign the lease or move in (checked November 3, 2026). For rent help, see our Mesa housing resources page and our Phoenix emergency rent help guide, which covers Maricopa County and Mesa programs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Mesa Section 8 waiting list open?

No. As of November 3, 2026, Mesa's Application & Wait List page says: "The Section 8 Housing Choice Voucher Program Wait List application is currently closed." Mesa's applicant portal at mesa.myhousing.com says there are currently no waiting lists open. Mesa gives no reopening date, so treat any date you see elsewhere as unconfirmed unless Mesa's own site shows it.

When did Mesa last open the list, and how were people picked?

The last opening Mesa documents ran from August 17 to August 30, 2022. Minutes of the City's Housing Services Governing Board from September 8, 2022 report 20,110 applications and a lottery that picked 4,000 of them at random. Mesa's 2026 Administrative Plan keeps that method: "All applicants will be randomly selected by a lottery process." The people picked are then sorted by lottery number and by Mesa's two preferences, so applying on the first day does not put you ahead.

Who gets preference on the Mesa list?

Mesa's Administrative Plan ranks two preferences, in this order. First, chronically homeless people as HUD defines it, verified by a referral letter from a nonprofit or a printout from the Homeless Management Information System. Second, current Mesa residents and people who work or have been hired to work inside Mesa city limits, at least 51% of the time and not remotely. Everyone else comes after.

How will I know when Mesa reopens the list?

Mesa's Administrative Plan says it will announce a reopening at least 10 business days before it accepts applications, in outlets including the East Valley Tribune, La Voz, AZ Central, the East Valley Independent, the City of Mesa website and the City's email list. Mesa's Application & Wait List page also has a sign-up form for waiting list updates by email or text.

Does Mesa have public housing?

No. Mesa's FY 2026/2027 Annual Plan lists zero public housing units and 1,642 Housing Choice Vouchers. Mesa's two project-based voucher properties, La Mesita Apartments and Escobedo at Verde Vista Phase II, take referrals only. The separate Housing Authority of Maricopa County owns Clare Feldstadt Apartments in Mesa; its notice says all of its open property lists close at 8:00 a.m. on December 1, 2026, and stay closed after that until further notice.

What number should I call?

(480) 644-3536 is Mesa's housing number. It is on the Housing page, the Application & Wait List page (press 2), the Project-Based Voucher page, the Housing Staff Contacts page and the Annual Plan, and HUD lists it for AZ005. For waiting list questions, the Staff Contacts page gives 480-644-5831 and the email hcvwaitinglist@mesaaz.gov. The office is at 200 S. Center St., Building 1; the Wait List page lists hours of Monday through Thursday, 7:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., and the Annual Plan says the office is closed Friday.

I am already on the Mesa list. How do I keep my place?

Mesa says that once you are on the list it will only contact you by mail, and if you do not respond, or your letter comes back, your name will be removed. Report any change of address, phone number, income or household members within 10 business days, through the Applicant Portal or with the change form, which you can return in person, by fax to 480-644-2923 or by mail to PO Box 1466, Mesa, AZ 85211.
