Housing Authority

Home Forward (formerly the Housing Authority of Portland)

Serves Portland, Gresham and the rest of Multnomah County, Oregon · Last checked November 3, 2026

Waythrough is an independent resource, not Home Forward. We summarize public information to help you get oriented. For applications and the most current waiting-list status, always use the official links below.

Quick Facts

Serves: Portland, Gresham and the rest of Multnomah County, Oregon
Phone: (503) 802-8300
Official site: www.homeforward.org/
Apply: Home Forward application page
Status last checked: November 3, 2026

Home Forward is the public housing agency for Portland. Portland City Council created it on December 11, 1941 as the Housing Authority of Portland, and that is still the name HUD uses: PHA OR002, program type Combined, so it runs both Housing Choice Vouchers and public housing. Home Forward says it serves Portland, Multnomah County, the city of Gresham and other communities in east Multnomah County, runs "the largest federally-funded housing programs in Oregon," and serves nearly 15,000 households. Its main office is at 135 SW Ash Street. The short version today: every general list is closed. Home Forward says "The Housing Choice Voucher waitlist is closed" and that it "is not issuing new vouchers from our HCV waitlist due to funding and budget constraints in 2026 and 2027." Its 2026 application for five apartment-community waitlists ran September 9 to 21, 2026 and is closed, with lottery results expected in late 2026. Two narrow priority lists stay open (checked November 3, 2026). This page covers Home Forward's exact words, how its lotteries work, how to check your status, and the neighboring authorities people confuse with it.

Waiting-List Status

Waiting lists open and close frequently, so treat the status below as a starting point and confirm on the official site before you rely on it.

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8): Closed, and no new vouchers are being issued from it in 2026 or 2027. Checked November 3, 2026. Home Forward's Housing Choice Voucher page reads: "The Housing Choice Voucher waitlist is closed." It says the list "was last opened in April 2025." Home Forward's Federal Funding and Policy Information page, marked "Last Updated October 2026," says "Home Forward is not issuing new vouchers from our HCV waitlist due to funding and budget constraints in 2026 and 2027." Its September 2026 press release repeats that the tenant-based list "remains closed, and we are not issuing vouchers from that waitlist at this time." No Home Forward page gives a reopening date. Home Forward offers an email sign-up for waitlist openings on its site.
  • 2026 apartment-community (project-based) waitlists: Closed; the application window ran September 9 to 21, 2026. Home Forward took applications from 9:00 a.m. on Wednesday, September 9 to 11:59 p.m. on Monday, September 21, 2026 for five project-based waitlists: Meadowlark Place, Dahlke Manor, Hollywood East, Medallion Apartments and Gresham Civic Station. That window is over. On November 3, 2026, its Waitlists page read: "2026 Waitlist Application is closed. All qualified applications will be entered into a random lottery." It says "Home Forward expects to notify applicants in late 2026 by mail, email, and through their RentCafe account," and that "2026 waitlist positions will not appear until the lottery is completed and positions are assigned." Home Forward says it picks enough applicants "to fill spots for the next three years." Watch homeforward.org/waitlists for the next opening; no date has been announced.
  • Priority lists for terminal illness and ADA-accessible units: Always open, with medical verification. Home Forward's Waitlists page says "Waitlists for Housing Choice Voucher and Project Based apartment communities are always open for anyone who has been diagnosed with a terminal illness and life expectancy of less than 12 months," after a Priority Verification Due to Health form completed with a medical provider. It also says the waitlists for ADA-accessible apartment communities it manages "are open for people who require ADA features," after a Priority Verification ADA form (checked November 3, 2026).
  • Other Home Forward apartment communities: Ask each leasing office. Home Forward's housing FAQ says "For most Home Forward apartment communities, you can contact the leasing office directly to see if there are apartments available," with contact details on each property page. For the communities it manages centrally, it says "We open those waitlists when the estimated wait time for an apartment is less than a year, and we typically open the waitlist for several properties at once."

Programs Home Forward Runs

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8), tenant-based
  • Project-based vouchers tied to specific apartment communities
  • Public housing and other Home Forward apartment communities
  • Short-Term Rent Assistance (STRA) and Regional Long-term Rent Assistance (RLRA), reached through partner agencies
  • VASH vouchers for veterans, Shelter Plus Care and Mod-Rehab

How to Apply

Apply directly through the authority's official application page: www.homeforward.org/waitlists/. If you have already applied, call (503) 802-8300 to verify your position and keep your contact information current. If the authority cannot reach you when your name comes up, you can be dropped from the list.

New to the process? Our step-by-step guide to applying for Section 8 walks through the whole thing, and how to find your PHA helps you locate the right authority.

Other Authorities Serving This Area

  • How the lotteries work. Home Forward says its application "is not first-come, first-served": everyone who applies in the window "has the same chance in the lottery," and "Being added to a waitlist does not guarantee rental assistance or housing placement." For the 2026 lists, a household had to earn less than 50 percent of Area Median Income, the head of household had to be 18 or an emancipated minor, at least one member had to be a U.S. citizen or have eligible immigration status, and every adult had to agree to a criminal background check. Keep your contact details current in RentCafe at connect.homeforward.org; Home Forward warns that "If a communication from Home Forward is returned, your name may be removed from the waitlist."
  • Housing Authority of Washington County serves Beaverton, Hillsboro, Tigard and the rest of Washington County. HUD lists it as OR022, 161 NW Adams Ave, Suite 2000, Hillsboro, (503) 846-4794.
  • Housing Authority of Clackamas County, HUD code OR001, 13930 South Gain Street, Oregon City, (503) 655-8267. Its site said on November 3, 2026: "All HACC waitlist are currently closed" and "The Section 8 wait list is currently closed."
  • Vancouver Housing Authority, across the Columbia River in Washington, is HUD code WA008 (Housing Authority of the City of Vancouver), 2500 Main Street, Vancouver, (360) 694-2501. Washington State rules, not Oregon's, apply there.
  • Home Forward is not an emergency rent fund for the public. Its Short-Term Rent Assistance is reached through 211info and partner agencies, not by applying to Home Forward. For rent help now, Home Forward's FAQ says to call 2-1-1, and our Portland emergency rent help guide covers Multnomah County's Emergency Rent Assistance, the state's eviction-court program and free legal help.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Portland Section 8 waitlist open?

No. Home Forward says "The Housing Choice Voucher waitlist is closed" and that it "was last opened in April 2025" (checked November 3, 2026). Its Federal Funding and Policy Information page, updated October 2026, adds that it "is not issuing new vouchers from our HCV waitlist due to funding and budget constraints in 2026 and 2027." No reopening date has been announced. The only general exception is a priority list that is always open to people with a terminal illness and a life expectancy of less than 12 months.

I applied in September 2026. When will I hear back?

Home Forward says it "expects to notify applicants in late 2026 by mail, email, and through their RentCafe account," and that it will announce on its website when notices are available. Until the lottery is processed, your 2026 waitlist position will not show in RentCafe. You can log in at connect.homeforward.org and choose Waiting List Status to confirm your application was received. Make sure your mailing address and email are current, because returned mail can get you removed.

Does applying on the first day help?

No. Home Forward says its waitlist application "is not first-come, first-served" and that "Every qualified applicant who applies during the application period has the same chance in the lottery." For the September 2026 opening, it even suggested applying later in the week to avoid heavy website traffic. What matters is applying before the window closes and qualifying when you are selected.

Can I get on a Home Forward list right now?

Only in narrow cases. Home Forward says its Housing Choice Voucher and Project Based waitlists "are always open for anyone who has been diagnosed with a terminal illness and life expectancy of less than 12 months," and its ADA-accessible apartment waitlists are open to people who need ADA features, each with a verification form completed by a medical provider. For many Home Forward apartment communities, it also says to contact the leasing office directly to ask whether apartments are available.

What is Home Forward's phone number?

The main number is (503) 802-8300, listed on Home Forward's Contact page and in HUD's directory for OR002. For questions about the voucher program, Home Forward's Housing Choice Voucher page and its Contact page give Rent Assistance at (503) 802-8333. Property Management is (503) 280-3750 and TTY is (503) 802-8554. The office at 135 SW Ash Street has a lobby window open Tuesday to Thursday, 9 a.m. to noon and 1 to 3 p.m.; Home Forward says it is closed to the public on Fridays without an appointment.

I live in Beaverton or Oregon City. Is that Home Forward?

No. Home Forward serves Portland, Gresham and the rest of Multnomah County. Washington County, including Beaverton and Hillsboro, has the Housing Authority of Washington County, (503) 846-4794. Clackamas County, including Oregon City, has the Housing Authority of Clackamas County, (503) 655-8267, which said on November 3, 2026 that all of its waitlists are closed. You can apply to more than one authority when their lists are open.
