The Wichita Housing Authority (WHA) is part of the City of Wichita's Housing & Community Services department. It runs Section 8 Housing Choice Vouchers, project-based vouchers and public housing, and it says it serves Sedgwick, Harvey (except the City of Newton) and Butler Counties. Its office is at 455 N. Main, 10th Floor, Wichita, KS 67202; the main line is 316-462-3700, Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., and the City says only people with appointments are seen by Housing staff. WHA says it administers over 3,000 vouchers and that participants pay about 30% of their adjusted income toward rent. The short version today: the Section 8 list is open, and the City says preliminary applications are being accepted online. The project-based voucher list for families who need 5 or 6 bedrooms is also open. WHA's portal shows no open public housing list. This page covers WHA's exact words, each list, the phone numbers, and where to turn if you need rent help now.

Waiting-List Status

Waiting lists open and close often. The status below is what we found the last time we checked; confirm it on the official site before you count on it.

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8): Open. Checked November 25, 2026: the City's How to Apply page says "Preliminary applications are being accepted online." WHA's applicant portal lists "Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8)" under "Open Waiting List(s)" with "Start: 07/22/2020 09:00 AM" and no end date. The portal says applications "can be received online anytime during the wait list period" and that applying does not guarantee a place on any list. Submit only one application; WHA says duplicates will not be accepted. Apply at wichita.myhousing.com.
  • AHF Project-Based Voucher list (5 or 6 bedrooms): Open. WHA's public notice says that on May 15, 2025 it opened the Project Based Voucher waiting list for Sedgwick, Butler and Harvey County (except the city of Newton), that "This list is only available for families who will qualify for a 5 or 6-bedroom voucher," and that it "will remain open indefinitely." The portal lists "Affordable Housing Fund" with "Start: 05/15/2025 07:00 AM." Applications that are mailed, faxed, emailed or hand delivered are not processed. People already on the regular list who qualify can log in and update their application to add this list (checked November 25, 2026).
  • Public housing: Not posted. WHA's applicant portal does not show a public housing list among its open waiting lists (checked November 25, 2026). The City's Public Housing page says WHA is "beginning to reposition 352 single family units in its holdings" and is selling homes approved for sale by HUD. Call 316-462-3700 to ask whether any public housing list is taking names.

Programs WHA Runs

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8): the waiting list is open for online pre-applications. WHA says participants pay about 30% of their adjusted income to the landlord and WHA pays the rest
  • Project-based vouchers: rental assistance tied to 18 single-family homes with 4 to 6 bedrooms in South City, Northeast and Southwest Wichita. The list for families who qualify for a 5- or 6-bedroom voucher is open
  • Public housing: WHA says it has 578 units (352 single-family houses and 226 apartments) and charges no more than 30% of adjusted income or a flat rent. The City is selling the single-family homes in groups approved by HUD
  • Family Self-Sufficiency (FSS) for voucher holders, and protections under the Violence Against Women Act (VAWA)
  • Small Area Fair Market Rents: WHA says changes are coming to its payment standards and to the Request for Tenancy Approval process, and posts an affordability calculator by ZIP code

How to Apply

Apply on the authority's own application page: wichita.myhousing.com/. If you have already applied, WHA says it will notify you by mail when your name comes to the top of the list or when it updates its files, and that if you do not respond or your letter comes back undeliverable, "your name will be removed from the waiting list." Report any change of address, phone number, income or family size within 10 business days. WHA says "ALL CHANGES MUST BE IN WRITING. No changes will be taken over the phone," and asks you to put your Social Security number on any letter. You can update your application on WHA's online portal; for help, call 316-462-3700.

If this is your first time applying, our step-by-step guide to applying for Section 8 covers each stage, and how to find your PHA shows which authority covers where you live.

Other Authorities Serving This Area

  • WHA is not an emergency rent fund. If you are behind on rent in the Wichita area, call the Center of Hope at 316-267-0222 (9 a.m. to noon, weekdays), and see our Wichita emergency rent help guide for Kansas Legal Services, the court's Eviction Resolution Program, 211, LIEAP and Kansas's eviction deadlines.
  • Already have a WHA voucher or public housing and fell behind? Call WHA at 316-462-3700 right away and call Kansas Legal Services at 316-267-3975. An eviction can cost you your assistance.
  • Help applying or an interpreter: WHA asks people who need help with an application to call Housing and Community Services at 316-462-3721 at least 5 business days ahead, and says the same number arranges oral interpretation ("Si requiere una interpretacion oral... por favor llame al (316) 462-3721"). Requests for a disability accommodation must be made in writing to the WHA office.
  • If you live outside Sedgwick, Butler or Harvey County, or in Newton, a different housing authority serves you. Use HUD's PHA directory to find it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Wichita Section 8 waiting list open?

Yes. On November 25, 2026, the City of Wichita said preliminary applications are being accepted online, and WHA's portal listed the Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8) list as open, with a start date of July 22, 2020 and no end date. Apply once at wichita.myhousing.com.

Is the 5- and 6-bedroom project-based list open?

Yes. WHA opened its project-based voucher list for families who qualify for a 5- or 6-bedroom voucher on May 15, 2025, and says it will remain open indefinitely. Apply online only; mailed, faxed, emailed or hand-delivered applications are not processed. If you are already on the regular list, log in and update your application to add it.

How do I apply to the Wichita Housing Authority?

Online at wichita.myhousing.com, in English or Spanish. Have Social Security cards, names and dates of birth for everyone in the household, and everyone's income ready, and write down your confirmation number. Submit only one application. For help, call 316-462-3721 at least 5 business days ahead.

How do I keep my place on a WHA list?

WHA notifies applicants by mail when their name comes to the top. If you do not respond, or the letter comes back undeliverable, WHA removes your name. Report a change of address, phone, income or family size within 10 business days, in writing; WHA takes no changes over the phone.

Who can WHA serve?

WHA says it serves Sedgwick, Harvey (except the City of Newton) and Butler Counties. The head of household must be at least 18 or an emancipated minor, income must be under HUD's limits, and all adults must pass a criminal background check. Anyone who owes money to WHA or another housing authority must pay it in full before getting assistance.

Can a Wichita landlord refuse my voucher?

Yes. Neither Kansas law nor Wichita's Non-Discrimination Ordinance lists source of income as protected, and Senate Bill 391 (2026) bars Kansas cities and counties from making landlords accept vouchers, so a landlord can turn down a voucher. A landlord still cannot refuse you because of race, disability, family status or another protected trait; Wichita's ordinance also covers sexual orientation, gender identity, age and veteran status.
