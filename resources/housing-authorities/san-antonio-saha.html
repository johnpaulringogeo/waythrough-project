Waythrough is an independent resource, not Opportunity Home. We summarize public information to help you get oriented. For applications and the most current waiting-list status, always use the official links below.

Quick Facts

Serves: the City of San Antonio, Texas
Phone: (210) 477-6000
Official site: homesa.org/
Apply: Opportunity Home application page
Status last checked: November 3, 2026

Opportunity Home San Antonio is the new name of the San Antonio Housing Authority (SAHA); its own Moving to Work Plan describes it as "Formerly San Antonio Housing Authority," and HUD still lists PHA TX006 under the old name, with a program type of Combined. Its headquarters is at 818 S. Flores St., San Antonio, TX 78204, (210) 477-6000. As of November 3, 2026, the Section 8 list is closed: Opportunity Home says "Applications for the Housing Choice Voucher Program are accepted only when the waitlist is open. Openings will be advertised on our home page," and its home page announced no opening. Public housing is different. Opportunity Home's application portal lists two open public housing lists, Elderly/Disabled Housing and 504 Accessible Housing, while the family public housing list has been closed since May 26, 2025. This page covers its exact words, which lists are open, what the wait looks like, its point-based preferences, and the Bexar County authority that runs a separate list.

Waiting-List Status

Waiting lists open and close frequently, so treat the status below as a starting point and confirm on the official site before you rely on it.

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8): Closed — Checked November 3, 2026. Opportunity Home's Apply for Housing page says "Applications for the Housing Choice Voucher Program are accepted only when the waitlist is open. Openings will be advertised on our home page," and its home page announced no opening. Its Moving to Work Plan for July 1, 2026 to June 30, 2027 lists the voucher list as "Closed," with 17,122 households on it as of February 1, 2026, and answers "No" to whether it plans to open the list during that year. The plan says Opportunity Home will instead send "notices of continued interest" to the people still on the list, so answer any letter you get.
  • Public Housing: Elderly/Disabled and 504 Accessible lists: Open — Opportunity Home's application portal, homesa.myhousing.com, shows two "Open Waiting List(s)" on November 3, 2026: "Elderly/Disabled Housing" and "504 Accessible Housing," each with a start date of January 14, 2022 and no end date posted. Its Moving to Work Plan describes the elderly list as for applicants "62 and above or who have a documented disability" and the accessible list as for applicants "who require a 504 accessible unit." Its Apply for Housing page gives a public housing wait of 2 to 4 years.
  • Family Public Housing: Closed — The Moving to Work Plan lists Family Public Housing as "Currently closed as of 5/26/25," with 40,376 households on it, and answers "No" to plans to open it during the year that ends June 30, 2027. The portal did not list it as open on November 3, 2026. The Apply for Housing page still says you "can apply any time" for public housing; in practice that means the elderly/disabled and accessible lists.
  • Project-Based Voucher and Mod Rehab site lists: Closed — The Moving to Work Plan lists every project-based and Mod Rehab site list as closed or referral-only, including Aspire at Tampico, East Meadows, Gardens at San Juan Square, Snowden and La Posada. It marks Wheatley Park Senior as one it plans to open before June 30, 2027, but gives no date, and no opening was posted on November 3, 2026.

Programs Opportunity Home Runs

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8)
  • Public Housing, with three lists: Family, Elderly/Disabled and 504 Accessible
  • Project-Based Vouchers at specific properties
  • Special voucher programs: Set Aside Homeless Vouchers (by referral), Mainstream vouchers for non-elderly people with disabilities, and HUD-VASH for homeless veterans referred by the VA
  • Moderate Rehabilitation (Mod Rehab) project-based assistance
  • Beacon Communities and other affordable housing communities, where you apply at the property

How to Apply

Apply directly through the authority's official application page: homesa.org/housing/find-a-home/apply-for-housing/. If you have already applied, call (210) 477-6000 to verify your position and keep your contact information current — if the authority cannot reach you when your name comes up, you can be dropped from the list.

New to the process? Our step-by-step guide to applying for Section 8 walks through the whole thing, and how to find your PHA helps you locate the right authority.

Other Authorities Serving This Area

  • The Housing Authority of Bexar County (HABC) is a separate agency with its own Section 8 list. HUD lists it as TX452, at 1954 E. Houston Street, Suite 104, San Antonio, TX 78202, (210) 225-0071, the same number HABC posts. Its applicant portal page says "Notice: the housing choice voucher program waiting list is currently closed" (checked November 3, 2026). HABC's last posted opening ran from December 16, 2024 at 6:00 AM to December 20, 2024 at 11:59 PM, online only.
  • The Texas Department of Housing and Community Affairs (TDHCA) runs a separate Section 8 program for parts of Texas without a local housing authority.
  • Opportunity Home is not an emergency rent fund. Its waitlist FAQ says "Opportunity Home does not offer emergency housing assistance." If you are behind on rent, see our San Antonio emergency rent help guide for the City's monthly rent application window, utility help, and the free Right to Counsel lawyers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Opportunity Home (SAHA) Section 8 waitlist open?

No. As of November 3, 2026, Opportunity Home says "Applications for the Housing Choice Voucher Program are accepted only when the waitlist is open. Openings will be advertised on our home page," and its home page announced no opening. Its Moving to Work Plan for July 2026 through June 2027 says it does not plan to open the voucher list during that year. Watch the home page at homesa.org, and apply to the Housing Authority of Bexar County when its list opens too.

Can I apply for public housing in San Antonio right now?

Yes, if you qualify for one of the two open lists. On November 3, 2026, Opportunity Home's application portal listed "Elderly/Disabled Housing" and "504 Accessible Housing" as open waiting lists. The elderly list is for applicants 62 or older or with a documented disability, and the accessible list is for applicants who need a 504 accessible unit. The family public housing list has been closed since May 26, 2025. Apply online from the Apply for Housing page at homesa.org; Opportunity Home lists a public housing wait of 2 to 4 years.

Is Opportunity Home the same as the San Antonio Housing Authority?

Yes. Opportunity Home San Antonio is the new name of the San Antonio Housing Authority (SAHA). HUD still lists PHA TX006 as the San Antonio Housing Authority. The address and main phone are the same: 818 S. Flores St., San Antonio, TX 78204, (210) 477-6000. Opportunity Home's contact page also lists a TTY line, 210-224-7124, and a text line, 210-477-6055.

How do I check my Opportunity Home waitlist status?

Through the applicant portal. Opportunity Home says to create a portal account 24 hours after you submit your application, then use it to check your status and keep your contact information up to date. When your name reaches the top, it mails a pre-application packet that is due back within 10 business days. Opportunity Home says "If we are unable to make contact, you will be removed from the waitlist." For login problems, its waitlist FAQ gives waitlist@homesa.org and 210-477-6000.

Who gets preference on the Opportunity Home voucher list?

Opportunity Home ranks preference applicants by points. Its Administrative Plan says "Opportunity Home will accept applications from preference applicants even when the waiting list may otherwise be closed," and places them on the list "in order of total combined preference points." Its 2025 to 2026 plan added a 25-point San Antonio residency preference for families who live in, work in, or have been hired to work in San Antonio or surrounding municipalities, and its 2026 to 2027 plan lists a 75-point College and University Homeless Assistance Program preference for homeless students referred by a participating college. Ask Opportunity Home which preference fits your situation.

How long is the wait for Opportunity Home housing?

Years. Opportunity Home's Apply for Housing page lists a wait of 2 to 4 years for public housing and up to 4 years for vouchers, and says "Wait times can be anywhere between two to seven years." As of February 1, 2026, its Moving to Work Plan counted 64,532 unique households across all of its waiting lists.
