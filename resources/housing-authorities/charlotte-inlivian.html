Housing Authority

INLIVIAN (Charlotte Housing Authority)

Serves Charlotte and Mecklenburg County, North Carolina · Last checked October 28, 2026

Waythrough is an independent resource, not INLIVIAN. We summarize public information to help you get oriented. For applications and the most current waiting-list status, always use the official links below.

INLIVIAN is the new name of the Charlotte Housing Authority. Its own Administrative Plan defines INLIVIAN as "Formerly The Housing Authority of the City of Charlotte," an agency that dates back to 1939, and some of its systems still carry the old CHA name, such as the cha-nc.org email address for the voucher waiting list. HUD lists it as PHA NC003 with a program type of Combined, so it runs both Housing Choice Vouchers and public housing, from 400 East Boulevard in Charlotte. INLIVIAN has been a Moving to Work agency since 2008, which lets it set some of its own rules, including a work requirement for voucher families. The short version today: the Section 8 list is closed, with more than 6,000 households already on it, and there is no single public housing list. You apply community by community, and no INLIVIAN page says a community list is open right now. This page covers INLIVIAN's exact words, how its community lists work, who gets picked first, and the neighboring housing authorities you can apply to as well.

Waiting-List Status

Waiting lists open and close frequently, so treat the status below as a starting point and confirm on the official site before you rely on it.

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8): Closed — Closed (checked October 28, 2026). INLIVIAN's HCV Waiting List Information page reads: "The tenant-based Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8) waiting list is currently closed to new applicants. There are currently more than 6,000 households on this list." Its Application Portal page repeats: "Please keep in mind our Housing Choice Voucher waitlist is currently closed," and its FAQ tells you to check whether the list is open, adding "as of today it is not." No INLIVIAN page gives a reopening date. If you are already on the list, INLIVIAN sends an annual letter, and you must answer it every year to stay on.
  • Income-based apartments (community lists): No agency-wide list; no INLIVIAN page says a community list is open as of October 28, 2026 — INLIVIAN's FAQ says "you will need to complete a separate application for each community" and that the wait "will vary by property." Its January 9, 2026 notice said nine communities managed by Blue Horizon Management Company would open waitlists on January 12, 2026: Autumn Place, Edwin Towers and Woodlawn House for seniors (2 bedrooms), and Cedar Knoll, Claremont, McMullen Woods, Robinsdale, Tarlton Hills and Wallace Woods for families (mostly 3 bedrooms). It gave no closing date. Checked October 28, 2026, the Tarlton Hills and Meadow Oaks pages say "This site is not currently accepting applications"; eight community pages (Arbor Glen, Fairmarket Square, Glen Cove, McMullen Wood, Nia Point, Springfield Gardens, Sunridge and The Park at Oaklawn) say availability "is currently for those not needing rental assistance"; and the other community pages give no status. Log in to the Applicant Portal to see which lists will take an application.

Programs INLIVIAN Runs

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8), tenant-based and project-based
  • Income-based apartments in INLIVIAN communities; its homepage says it owns and manages 45 properties, and its property search lists 43 communities managed by Blue Horizon Management Company
  • Mixed-income, workforce and market-rate apartments in the same communities, with their own income rules
  • Family Self-Sufficiency, Destination Homeownership, senior services and youth programs for residents

How to Apply

Apply directly through the authority's official application page: www.inlivian.com/application-portal/. If you have already applied, call (704) 336-5183 to verify your position and keep your contact information current — if the authority cannot reach you when your name comes up, you can be dropped from the list.

New to the process? Our step-by-step guide to applying for Section 8 walks through the whole thing, and how to find your PHA helps you locate the right authority.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Charlotte Section 8 waiting list open?

No. As of October 28, 2026, INLIVIAN's HCV Waiting List Information page says: "The tenant-based Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8) waiting list is currently closed to new applicants. There are currently more than 6,000 households on this list." Its Application Portal page and its FAQ say the same. INLIVIAN publishes no reopening date, and its FAQ says it will update its website with any announcement of an opening, so treat any date you see elsewhere as unconfirmed unless INLIVIAN's own site shows it.

Is INLIVIAN the same as the Charlotte Housing Authority?

Yes. INLIVIAN's Administrative Plan defines it as "Formerly The Housing Authority of the City of Charlotte," and its History page traces the agency back to 1939. HUD still lists it under PHA code NC003, now with the name INLIVIAN, at 400 East Boulevard. Old letters, the cha-nc.org email address and the applicant portal's web address still use the CHA name, so do not be thrown off if you see both.

Can I apply for an INLIVIAN apartment right now?

Only community by community, and no INLIVIAN page says a list is open today. INLIVIAN's FAQ says you need a separate application for each community. Its January 12, 2026 openings covered nine communities and had no closing date, but as of October 28, 2026 the Tarlton Hills and Meadow Oaks pages say they are not accepting applications, and eight other community pages say availability is only for people who do not need rental assistance. The Applicant Portal is where you see and sign up for open lists; call (704) 336-5183 if you cannot tell.

Who gets a voucher first when INLIVIAN pulls names?

INLIVIAN's 2025 Amended Administrative Plan says it selects, in order: families being displaced from INLIVIAN or affiliate properties because of relocation or recapitalization; families receiving a HUD special funded voucher, such as an Emergency Housing Voucher, whose funding will end; and then everyone else by date and time of application. The plan also lists preferences for homeless families in a self-reliance supportive service program and for victims of domestic violence. If INLIVIAN cannot verify a preference you claimed, its HCV page says your name goes back on the list by date and time.

Does INLIVIAN have a work requirement?

Yes. INLIVIAN's Housing Choice Voucher page says that effective January 1, 2018, "All non-elderly/non-disabled households will be expected to work 20 hours per week," and that families moving their voucher to Charlotte must already work in the Charlotte Mecklenburg area or bring a job offer letter. For income-based apartments, its FAQ lists proof of full-time employment (over 30 hours) for 6 months, plus credit, criminal and eviction background checks, and says Family Self-Sufficiency program eligibility may be required.

How do I update my information if I am already on the list?

Use INLIVIAN's Applicant Portal. INLIVIAN says anyone who used its old system must register again on the new portal, using a registration code, an email address and a Social Security number. If you cannot log in, it gives the email HCVwaitlist@cha-nc.org (include your name and Social Security number). Without internet access, write to HCV Wait List Update, P.O. Box 36795, Charlotte, NC 28236. Answer the annual letter, or you can be dropped.

What number should I call?

(704) 336-5183 is INLIVIAN's main number. It is in the footer of every INLIVIAN page, in the body of the HCV Waiting List Information page, on the Application Portal page as the Customer Service Center, and at the top of the RentCafe applicant portal, and HUD's directory lists the same number for NC003. For moving a voucher from another housing authority, INLIVIAN's HCV page and FAQ both give a portability hotline, (704) 336-8271.

Can INLIVIAN help me with rent or housing tonight?

No. INLIVIAN's FAQ says "No, INLIVIAN does not provide emergency or temporary housing," and the voucher program is a long wait. If you are behind on rent, dial 211 and see our Charlotte emergency rent help guide. If you already live in INLIVIAN housing or have a voucher and fall behind, call (704) 336-5183 right away and get legal help, because an eviction can cost you your assistance.
