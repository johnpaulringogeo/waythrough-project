Detroit Housing Commission (DHC)

Serves Detroit, Michigan · Last checked November 3, 2026

Waythrough is an independent resource, not DHC. We summarize public information to help you get oriented. For applications and the most current waiting-list status, always use the official links below.

Quick Facts

Serves: Detroit, Michigan
Phone: (313) 877-8000
Official site: www.dhcmi.org/
Apply: DHC application page
Status last checked: November 3, 2026

The Detroit Housing Commission is the public housing agency for the City of Detroit, and it calls itself Michigan's largest public housing agency. HUD lists it as PHA MI001 with a program type of Combined, so it runs both Housing Choice Vouchers (Section 8) and public housing, which DHC calls LIPH. Its administrative office is at 1301 E. Jefferson, and its Customer Service Center is at 2211 Orleans. The short version today: DHC's Housing Choice Voucher list is closed, its public housing list is closed, and DHC says it is only taking applications for Project Based properties. This page quotes DHC on each list, explains the state's own voucher program, run by MSHDA, which also serves Detroit, and lists the smaller housing commissions nearby.

Waiting-List Status

Waiting lists open and close frequently, so treat the status below as a starting point and confirm on the official site before you rely on it.

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8): Closed — Closed (checked November 3, 2026). DHC's Applicant FAQ says: "The HCV Waiting list is currently closed, we are only accepting applications for Project Based Properties only." Its Applicants page says: "The Housing Choice Voucher Program (formerly Section 8) waitlist is closed. DHC is not accepting applications at this time." No DHC page gives a reopening date. DHC says that when the list opens it will post a notice on its homepage, in local newspapers and through community media and social service providers, and that "Historically DHC waiting list closes with a lottery."
  • Public Housing (LIPH): Closed — DHC's Real Estate Management page says: "NOTICE: The LIPH waiting list is currently closed." (checked November 3, 2026). Each DHC property keeps its own waitlist, and DHC says you can apply to as many as you like "if the waiting list is open for that site and the household is qualified."
  • Project-Based Vouchers: DHC says it is accepting applications for Project Based properties — DHC does not post which project-based properties are taking names. Its PBV FAQ says one electronic pre-application covers every list you join, and that the available waiting lists appear in the applicant portal once your application is complete. Each property has its own list, DHC works each list in the order names were added, and a property's preference, if it has one, moves qualifying families up.

Programs DHC Runs

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8), tenant-based
  • Project-Based Vouchers at specific properties, each with its own waiting list
  • Low-Income Public Housing (LIPH): 3,403 public housing and scattered-site units, each property with its own waitlist
  • Family Unification Program, Moderate Rehabilitation and the Family Self-Sufficiency (FSS) program

How to Apply

Apply directly through the authority's official application page: portal-dhcmi.securecafe.com/onlineleasing/detroit-live/guestlogin.aspx. If you have already applied, call (313) 877-8000 to verify your position and keep your contact information current — if the authority cannot reach you when your name comes up, you can be dropped from the list.

New to the process? Our step-by-step guide to applying for Section 8 walks through the whole thing, and how to find your PHA helps you locate the right authority.

Other Authorities Serving This Area

  • Michigan State Housing Development Authority (MSHDA), HUD code MI901, program type Section 8, 735 E. Michigan Ave., Lansing. MSHDA calls itself "the largest statewide PHA in the country" and says it assists over 29,000 families "across all 83 counties in Michigan," Wayne County included, so a Detroit resident can apply to MSHDA's Wayne County list as well as DHC's whenever each is open. MSHDA's lists are county by county: you must live or work in the county, names go on in date and time order, and you get one application, online only. On November 3, 2026 its waiting-list page said: "No Housing Choice Voucher (HCV) waiting lists currently open." MSHDA's local housing agents for Wayne County include Community Management Associates at 882 Oakman Blvd., Suite 101, Detroit, and RPI Management at 3 Parklane Blvd., Dearborn.
  • MSHDA project-based lists: MSHDA's page lists Lee Plaza I and Lee Plaza II in Wayne County with a date of August 5, 2026, among lists that "have opened recently or will be opening soon." MSHDA gives no closing date and says PBV lists "MAY remain open." You cannot apply online: only the Lead Agency assigned to the property can place you on its list, so check MSHDA's page and call the Lead Agency before you go.
  • Other housing commissions nearby run their own lists (HUD codes, program types and phones from HUD's Michigan PHA list): Inkster Housing Commission, MI027, Combined, (313) 561-2600; Dearborn Housing Commission, MI003, Low-Rent (public housing only), (313) 943-2391; Hamtramck Housing Commission, MI004, Low-Rent, (313) 868-7445; and Highland Park Housing Commission, MI105, Low-Rent, (313) 868-4500. HUD's Michigan list has no Wayne County Housing Authority.
  • DHC is not an emergency rent fund. If you are behind on rent, see our Detroit emergency rent help guide: State Emergency Relief, Detroit's free eviction lawyers through Right to Counsel, and more. If you already have DHC housing or a voucher and fall behind, call DHC at (313) 877-8000.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Detroit Section 8 waiting list open?

No. As of November 3, 2026, DHC's Applicant FAQ says: "The HCV Waiting list is currently closed, we are only accepting applications for Project Based Properties only." No DHC page gives a reopening date. DHC says it announces openings on dhcmi.org, in local newspapers and through community groups. The state's MSHDA program, which also covers Detroit, said the same day that no Housing Choice Voucher waiting lists are open.

How do I apply for a Project Based property with DHC?

Through DHC's RentCafe applicant portal at portal-dhcmi.securecafe.com. DHC's PBV FAQ says one electronic pre-application covers every list you add your name to, and that the available waiting lists appear in the portal once your application is complete. Each property has its own list, so your place on one does not affect the others. DHC says it works each list in the order names were added, with priority for families who meet a property's preference.

Does MSHDA take Section 8 applications from Detroit residents?

Yes, when its Wayne County list is open. MSHDA says it serves families across all 83 Michigan counties and that you must live or work in the county you apply for, with one online application. On November 3, 2026 MSHDA's waiting-list page said: "No Housing Choice Voucher (HCV) waiting lists currently open." If you are already on an MSHDA list, update your information in its applicant portal at mshda.myhousing.com.

Is DHC public housing open?

No. DHC's Real Estate Management page says "The LIPH waiting list is currently closed" (checked November 3, 2026). DHC has 3,403 public housing and scattered-site units, and each property keeps its own waitlist. When lists open, DHC says to include everyone in the household, mark only the preferences that apply, such as VAWA, elderly or disabled, displaced by a federal disaster, or veterans, and keep your contact information current in the RentCafe portal.

Can a Detroit landlord refuse my voucher?

Most cannot. Since April 2, 2025, Michigan's Elliott-Larsen Civil Rights Act, MCL 37.2502, bars landlords from turning a tenant away based on source of income, and that includes a rent voucher or subsidy. Landlords with fewer than 5 rental units in Michigan are exempt, so the smallest landlords may still say no.

What number should I call?

DHC's main line is (313) 877-8000, Monday to Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. It is in the footer of every DHC page and in HUD's directory for MI001. The TDD/TTY line is 800-222-3679, and the email is info@dhcmi.org. The administrative office is at 1301 E. Jefferson and the Customer Service Center at 2211 Orleans, both in Detroit, MI 48207.
