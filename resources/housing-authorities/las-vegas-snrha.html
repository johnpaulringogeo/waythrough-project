Southern Nevada Regional Housing Authority (SNRHA)

Serves Las Vegas, North Las Vegas, Henderson, Mesquite, Laughlin and the rest of Clark County, Nevada · Last checked October 28, 2026

Waythrough is an independent resource, not SNRHA. We summarize public information to help you get oriented. For applications and the most current waiting-list status, always use the official links below.

The Southern Nevada Regional Housing Authority (SNRHA) is the public housing agency for all of Clark County. Its Applicant Overview page says it provides housing "throughout Clark County, including Las Vegas, North Las Vegas, Henderson, Mesquite, and Laughlin." HUD lists it as PHA NV018 with a program type of Combined, at 340 North 11th Street, Las Vegas, NV 89101. SNRHA runs four programs, each with its own waiting list: Housing Choice Vouchers, public housing, RAD/PBV communities and non-aided affordable housing. The short version today: the Section 8 voucher list is closed, but SNRHA is taking online entries for its 2026 Project Based Voucher waitlist from October 26 to November 16, 2026, and its public housing and RAD/PBV pages both say "Waitlist is Open." This page covers each list in SNRHA's own words, the six PBV properties and their bedroom sizes, the preference points, how the RentCafe portal works, and what veterans can do at any time.

Waiting-List Status

Waiting lists open and close frequently, so treat the status below as a starting point and confirm on the official site before you rely on it.

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8): Closed — Closed (checked October 28, 2026). The status line on SNRHA's How to Apply for HCV Housing page reads "Waitlist is Closed," and the page says "SNRHA accepts pre-applications only when a wait list becomes open." When it does open, you apply on the RentCafe portal, and "After the wait list closes, SNRHA will run a lottery to randomly select applicants from the waiting list." No SNRHA page gives a reopening date.
  • 2026 Project Based Voucher (PBV) waitlist: Open October 26 to November 16, 2026, online only — SNRHA's homepage reads "PBV Waitlist is Now OPEN!!!" and its public notice says it "is opening its 2026 PBV Waitlist," with online entries accepted "beginning on Monday October 26, 2026 at 8 a.m. and closing Monday, November 16, 2026 at 5 p.m." The notice lists six properties, as of October 28, 2026: Buffalo Cactus Senior (62 and older, 1 bedroom), Hampton Court in Henderson (family, 1, 2 and 3 bedrooms), James Down Towers (seniors 62 and older, 1 and 2 bedrooms), Pearson Pines Senior (62 and older, 1 and 2 bedrooms), Robert Gordon Plaza (62 and older, 1 and 2 bedrooms) and Vera Johnson Manor B (family, 2 and 3 bedrooms). "All applications submitted successfully will be placed on the waiting list." SNRHA is not accepting paper applications, and only one entry per family counts.
  • Public Housing: "Waitlist is Open" (SNRHA's status line) — The status line on SNRHA's How to Apply for Public Housing page reads "Waitlist is Open" (checked October 28, 2026). That page does not name the open properties; it says to go to the RentCafe applicant portal "and make note of any open wait lists listed in the header above the login form." On October 28, 2026 the portal header read "Waiting Lists Are Now Open!" and "We Are Currently Accepting Applications For Available Waiting Lists," without naming them, so the property and bedroom choices only appear once you register and start an application.
  • RAD/PBV Housing: "Waitlist is Open" (SNRHA's status line) — The status line on SNRHA's How to Apply for RAD Housing page reads "Waitlist is Open" (checked October 28, 2026). SNRHA says that after a list closes it selects "by time and date which means, a first come first serve basis." The 2026 PBV waitlist above is the opening SNRHA has announced.
  • Affordable Housing (non-aided): Closed — The status line on SNRHA's How to Apply for Affordable Housing page reads "Waitlist is Closed" (checked October 28, 2026). When a list opens, you apply through each community's Apply Now button, and SNRHA says it selects applicants "by date and time."

Programs SNRHA Runs

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8); SNRHA says it assists "many thousands of households"
  • Public Housing communities owned and managed by SNRHA across Southern Nevada
  • RAD/PBV communities: SNRHA properties renovated through HUD's Rental Assistance Demonstration and filled through Project-Based Vouchers
  • Affordable Housing communities, a non-aided program without direct HUD funding, "over a thousand housing units"
  • Family Self-Sufficiency, FLEX, Senior Services and other supportive services for residents

How to Apply

Apply directly through the authority's official application page: onlineportal-snvrha.securecafe.com/onlineleasing/snrha-live/guestlogin.aspx. If you have already applied, call (702) 477-3100 to verify your position and keep your contact information current — if the authority cannot reach you when your name comes up, you can be dropped from the list.

New to the process? Our step-by-step guide to applying for Section 8 walks through the whole thing, and how to find your PHA helps you locate the right authority.

Other Authorities Serving This Area

  • No separate authority for Henderson or North Las Vegas. HUD's Nevada list has only three housing authorities, and SNRHA is the one for Clark County. The other two are the City of Reno Housing Authority, HUD code NV001, program type Combined, 1525 E 9th Street, Reno, NV 89512, (775) 329-3630, and the Nevada Rural Housing Authority, HUD code NV905, program type Section 8, 3695 Desatoya Drive, Carson City, NV 89701, (775) 887-1795.
  • SNRHA is not an emergency rent fund. Its PBV notice says "SNRHA does not have immediate or emergency housing." If you are behind on rent, dial 211 and see our Las Vegas emergency rent help guide for the help that still exists, free legal aid, and Nevada's 7-day notice.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Las Vegas Section 8 waiting list open?

No. As of October 28, 2026, the status line on SNRHA's How to Apply for HCV Housing page reads "Waitlist is Closed," and SNRHA says it "accepts pre-applications only when a wait list becomes open." What is open is different: SNRHA's 2026 Project Based Voucher waitlist takes online entries from 8 a.m. on Monday, October 26 to 5 p.m. on Monday, November 16, 2026, and its public housing and RAD/PBV pages say "Waitlist is Open." A project-based voucher stays with the apartment, so you live at that property rather than renting anywhere.

How do I apply for the 2026 PBV waitlist?

Online only, on SNRHA's RentCafe portal at onlineportal.snvrha.org, by 5 p.m. on November 16, 2026. SNRHA's notice says you must be 18 or older or legally emancipated, a U.S. citizen or eligible immigrant, and have an email address. You enter the Social Security number of the head of household and co-head or spouse, and only one entry per family counts; multiple entries are voided. Free computer help is at 340 N. 11th Street, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m., Monday through Thursday. For a reasonable accommodation, the notice gives 702-477-3206.

What preference points are on the PBV list?

SNRHA's 2026 PBV notice lists these possible preference points: working 20 or more hours a week; full-time students 18 and older in an accredited school or training program; receiving SSI or SSD, or proof of inability to work; age 62 or older; families who live or work in Clark County; veterans or spouses of veterans; disabled veterans with a service-connected disability; and spouses of veterans whose death was service-connected. Claim every one you qualify for.

Which public housing lists are open?

SNRHA's public housing page says "Waitlist is Open," but it does not name the properties. SNRHA tells you to check the header of its RentCafe applicant portal, and on October 28, 2026 that header only said "We Are Currently Accepting Applications For Available Waiting Lists." You see the open properties after you register and start an application, and SNRHA asks you to apply only for properties you are eligible for. If you are unsure, call (702) 477-3100.

Can veterans apply when a list is closed?

Yes, for the property lists. SNRHA's Applicant Overview page says "VETERANS can apply to any AFFORDABLE, RAD/PBV, OR PUBLIC HOUSING community anytime," online through RentCafe or in person at its administrative offices at 340 N 11th Street, Las Vegas, Monday through Thursday, 7:00 am to 6:00 pm. That does not include the Section 8 voucher list, which is closed.

Who can SNRHA turn down?

SNRHA's PBV notice says criminal background checks are run on everyone 18 and older, and that SNRHA will deny any household member who is subject to a lifetime sex offender registration requirement, or who has been convicted of making methamphetamine in public housing or in a Section 8 assisted property. Every household must also be income qualified; SNRHA says applicants "are required to be income qualified prior to becoming a resident."

What number should I call?

(702) 477-3100 is SNRHA's main number. It is in the footer of every snvrha.org page, on its Contact page, at the top of the RentCafe applicant portal and in the 2026 PBV notice, and HUD's directory lists the same number for NV018. For TDD, SNRHA gives 7-1-1, and its PBV notice also gives 1-800-833-5833. The reasonable accommodation line, 702-477-3206, appears only in the PBV notice. The portal lists office hours of 7 a.m. to 6 p.m., Monday through Thursday, and SNRHA says calls may be recorded.

If I get a voucher, how long do I have to find a place?

SNRHA's HCV page says you have 90 days to find housing on the private market and can apply for a 30-day extension. If you do not find housing in time, it says you can submit another preliminary application. You and the landlord complete the Request for Tenancy Approval in RentCafe, and SNRHA inspects the unit before you sign the lease.
