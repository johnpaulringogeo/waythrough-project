Quick Facts

Serves: the City of Richmond, Virginia
Phone: (804) 780-4200
Official site: www.rrha.com/
Apply: RRHA application page
Status last checked: November 5, 2026

The Richmond Redevelopment and Housing Authority (RRHA), HUD code VA007, runs Section 8 Housing Choice Vouchers and public housing for the City of Richmond. Its 2027 agency plan counts 4,060 vouchers and 3,245 public housing units. Two things matter most right now. First, the voucher waiting list is closed, and RRHA has not issued new tenant-based vouchers from it since it paused issuance on May 20, 2025 because of federal funding. Second, RRHA opens its public housing, senior and project-based lists in short windows, and the last one closed on May 8, 2026. This page gives each list's status in RRHA's own words, how to reach the right office, and where to turn if you need help this week.

Waiting-List Status

Waiting lists open and close frequently, so treat the status below as a starting point and confirm on the official site before you rely on it.

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8), tenant-based: Closed; voucher issuance paused since May 20, 2025. Closed (checked November 5, 2026). RRHA's FY2027 agency plan answers "Is this waiting list closed?" with "Yes" for its voucher list of 3,650 families and says it plans to reopen the list during the plan year, with no date given. Its April 17, 2026 notice about other lists said: "This waitlist opening does NOT include RRHA's voucher waitlist." On May 20, 2025, RRHA said it "has suspended the issuance of tenant-based vouchers from the Housing Choice Voucher (HCV) Program" waiting list, and "Once funding is available, RRHA will once again begin selecting from the waitlist." We found no RRHA notice lifting that pause.
  • Public housing family communities: Closed; last window May 4–8, 2026. RRHA's last opening for public housing family communities with 4-bedroom units ran "Beginning Monday, May 4, 2026 at 8:30 a.m. through Friday, May 8, 2026 at 5 p.m." RRHA has announced no opening since (checked November 5, 2026). Watch rrha.com news for the next window.
  • RRHA senior communities (55 and older): Closed; last window May 4–8, 2026. Senior sites were part of the same May 4 to May 8, 2026 opening. No newer opening has been announced (checked November 5, 2026).
  • Project-based voucher (PBV) communities: Site-based lists; check the Applicant Portal. RRHA "utilizes site-based waiting lists for all Project-based Voucher ("PBV") communities." Its PBV page says "PBV applications are accepted year-round" and "RRHA is unable to determine or estimate wait time for housing assistance," but its news releases describe PBV lists opening in set windows, most recently May 4–8, 2026. Check the Applicant Portal, or call (804) 780-4200, before you count on a list being open (checked November 5, 2026).
  • HUD-VASH (veterans): By referral. RRHA said in May 2025 that "Veterans Affairs Supportive Housing (VASH) vouchers are not impacted" by the voucher pause. VASH vouchers come by referral from the VA, not from the waiting list.

Programs RRHA Runs

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8): 4,060 vouchers, per the FY2027 agency plan
  • Public housing: 3,245 units in family and senior communities
  • Project-based vouchers at site-based communities, including Creighton Renaissance and Armstrong Renaissance
  • HUD-VASH vouchers for veterans, by referral from the VA
  • Redevelopment of Creighton Court, Gilpin Court and other older public housing communities, using a build-first approach

How to Apply

Apply directly through the authority's official application page: myportal-rrha.securecafe.com/onlineleasing/richmond-live-0/guestlogin.aspx. If you have already applied, log in to the same RRHA Applicant Portal to check your status; RRHA asks applicants to make contact changes online there. Keep your address and phone current: in its March 2–31, 2026 purge, RRHA removed applicants who did not update their information.

Other Authorities Serving This Area

  • RRHA's voucher office is not at headquarters. The Housing Choice Voucher Program and Tenant Selection office is at 918 Chamberlayne Parkway, Richmond, VA 23220, open Monday to Friday 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m., with walk-in hours on Tuesday from 9 a.m. to noon and Thursday from 1 to 4 p.m. The main office is 600 E. Broad Street. For TDD, dial 711.
  • If you need a place to sleep, not a place on a list, call the Homeless Connection Line at 804-972-0813, for people who are homeless or within 3 days of losing their housing. If you are more than 3 days away, call the Housing Resource Line at 804-422-5061.
  • Neighboring counties are separate. Richmond is an independent city. Living in Henrico or Chesterfield County does not put you in RRHA's service area, and applying to RRHA does not put you on any other agency's list.
  • Behind on rent? The City's Eviction Diversion Program, run by Southside Community Development & Housing Corp., is the main door, and Central Virginia Legal Aid Society (804-648-1012) gives free legal help. Our Richmond emergency rent help guide explains Virginia's 14-day notice, the right to pay and stay, and every program that is open now.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the RRHA Section 8 waiting list open?

No. As of November 5, 2026, RRHA lists its Housing Choice Voucher waiting list as closed. When it opened other lists in May 2026, it said: "This waitlist opening does NOT include RRHA's voucher waitlist." Its FY2027 agency plan says it plans to reopen the voucher list during the plan year, but no date has been posted. Watch rrha.com and keep your contact information current if you are already on the list.

Is RRHA still giving out vouchers?

Not from the regular waiting list, as far as RRHA has posted. On May 20, 2025, RRHA said it "has suspended the issuance of tenant-based vouchers from the Housing Choice Voucher (HCV) Program" waiting list because of federal funding, and "Once funding is available, RRHA will once again begin selecting from the waitlist." We found no notice lifting the pause as of November 5, 2026. VASH vouchers for veterans were not affected.

When did RRHA last open its public housing list?

From Monday, May 4, 2026 at 8:30 a.m. through Friday, May 8, 2026 at 5 p.m. That window covered public housing family communities with 4-bedroom units, RRHA senior communities and some project-based communities. RRHA's FY2027 plan also notes it may reopen lists during the year. Windows are short, so check rrha.com news often.

How do I check my application or update my address?

Log in to the RRHA Applicant Portal. RRHA asks applicants to make contact changes online there. This matters: in March 2026 RRHA purged its waiting list, and applicants who did not update by March 31, 2026 were removed. You can also visit the voucher and tenant selection office at 918 Chamberlayne Parkway during walk-in hours, Tuesday 9 a.m. to noon or Thursday 1 to 4 p.m.

What is happening at Creighton Court and Gilpin Court?

RRHA is redeveloping older public housing communities. Creighton Court is being rebuilt as Creighton Renaissance, and Gilpin Court has build-first projects in planning. RRHA says its build-first model "builds new units before any demolition activity occurs to avoid displacement of residents." Its FY2027 plan says it is working with Southside Community Development to offer eviction diversion clinics for Creighton and Mosby South residents before relocation. If you live in one of these communities, ask your property manager about your relocation rights.

Can a Richmond landlord refuse my voucher?

Usually not. The Virginia Fair Housing Law makes source of funds, including a Housing Choice Voucher, a protected class (Va. Code 36-96.3). There are exceptions for an owner of four or fewer rental units in Virginia, and for a case where the voucher agency does not approve the tenancy within 15 days (Va. Code 36-96.2). If you are refused, contact the Virginia Fair Housing Office or Central Virginia Legal Aid Society at 804-648-1012.
