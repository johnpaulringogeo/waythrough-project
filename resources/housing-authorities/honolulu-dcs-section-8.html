Quick Facts

Serves: The island of O‘ahu (City and County of Honolulu), Hawai‘i
Phone: (808) 768-7077
Official site: www.honolulu.gov/dcs/cad/
Apply: DCS Section 8 application page
Status last checked: November 5, 2026

On O‘ahu, two public agencies run rental assistance, and they keep separate lists. The City and County of Honolulu's Department of Community Services (DCS), through its Community Assistance Division, runs the City's Section 8 Housing Choice Voucher program. HUD lists it as PHA HI003, program type Section 8, and DCS says the voucher program is "the largest part of the Section 8 program, with about 4,100 units." The State's Hawai‘i Public Housing Authority (HPHA) runs public housing across the state and its own voucher program, which "covers the island of Oahu only" (HUD codes HI001 and HI901). The short answer: the City's list is closed. DCS took online pre-applications for one week in July 2025, placed 3,000 people on the list by random selection, and says it "is not expected to reopen for approximately three years." HPHA's O‘ahu voucher and federal public housing lists are also closed. This page gives each agency's exact words, how to check a 2025 application, what the voucher pays, and the one HPHA list that is taking names now.

Waiting-List Status

Waiting lists open and close frequently, so treat the status below as a starting point and confirm on the official site before you rely on it.

  • City and County of Honolulu Section 8 Housing Choice Voucher (DCS): Closed — Closed (checked November 5, 2026). DCS's Section 8 page says the program "accepted pre-applications to the Waitlist from Monday, July 14, 2025, 7:45 a.m. through Friday, July 18, 2025, 4:30 p.m (HST). The Waitlist is now closed." It adds that "3,000 applicants have been placed on the wait list" by randomization and that "At this time, the wait list is not expected to reopen for approximately three years." Its FAQ answers "Is the Waiting List currently open?" with "No. The Waiting List is currently closed." DCS no longer takes paper applications.
  • HPHA Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8) on O‘ahu: Closed — HPHA's How to Apply page, checked November 5, 2026, reads under "HCV Program (Section 8) on Oahu": "Waiting List(s) are CLOSED. All waiting lists for this program are currently closed. Please check back later." No reopening date is given. HPHA uses an online pre-application form when a list opens.
  • HPHA federal public housing on O‘ahu: Closed — The same HPHA page reads under "Federal Public Housing on Oahu": "Waiting List(s) are CLOSED. All waiting lists for this program are currently closed. Please check back later." (checked November 5, 2026). HPHA manages 5,406 federal public housing units statewide and 864 State-funded units. The How to Apply page gives no separate status for State public housing, so ask HPHA at (808) 204-9042.
  • HPHA Rent Supplement Program for Kūpuna (interest list): Interest list open — HPHA's home page says "Interest List is Open!" and "The HPHA is now accepting names for the program's interest list" (checked November 5, 2026). The program helps "elderly families and individuals experiencing homelessness or at imminent risk of homelessness" pay part of their rent, up to $500 a month, for households at or below 50% of area median income. HPHA says joining the interest list gets you "alerts about future waiting list openings" and "does not guarantee rental assistance."

Programs DCS Section 8 Runs

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8) on O‘ahu, about 4,100 vouchers (City and County of Honolulu, DCS)
  • Mainstream disability vouchers, Family Unification Program vouchers and Family Self-Sufficiency (DCS)
  • Moderate Rehabilitation Program units (DCS)
  • Separate State programs: HPHA federal and State public housing statewide, HPHA's own O‘ahu voucher program, and HPHA's Rent Supplement Program for Kūpuna

How to Apply

Apply directly through the authority's official application page: www.honolulu.gov/dcs/dcs-cad-section-8-housing-choice-voucher/. If you applied in July 2025, check your result at waitlistcheck.com with your username and password: DCS says an ACTIVE status "means that your application has been randomly selected for the wait list" and an INACTIVE status means it was not. For help, call DCS at (808) 768-7077, or the Intake Clerk at (808) 768-7098 if you cannot get into your account. DCS says "All changes to your contact information or household information must be submitted to the office in writing" (Section 8 Rental Assistance, Attn: Intake, 842 Bethel Street, 1st Floor, Honolulu, HI 96813), and that failing to update "may result in missed correspondence or removal from the Waiting List."

Other Authorities Serving This Area

  • Hawai‘i Public Housing Authority (HPHA), the State agency, runs federal and State public housing statewide and its own voucher program on O‘ahu. HUD lists it as HI001 (public housing) and HI901 (Section 8) at 1002 N. School Street, Honolulu. HPHA's own site gives (808) 204-9042, HPHA@hawaii.gov for general and public housing questions, and DHS.HPHA.Section8@hawaii.gov for Section 8; its mailing address is PO Box 17907, Honolulu, HI 96817. You can be on HPHA's lists and the City's list at the same time.
  • Other islands have their own voucher agencies, as HUD lists them: County of Hawai‘i (HI002, Hilo), County of Maui (HI004, Wailuku) and Kaua‘i County Housing Agency (HI005, Līhu‘e). DCS says that if you apply from off O‘ahu, "federal regulations require you to lease a unit within our jurisdiction" for at least the first 12 months.
  • DCS Section 8 is not an emergency rent fund. DCS says "Rental assistance is not available while you are on the Waiting List." If you are behind on rent, see our Honolulu emergency rent help guide: the State's Hawai‘i Relief Program through Catholic Charities Hawai‘i, Aloha United Way 211, the Legal Aid Society of Hawai‘i, free mediation under Act 278, H-HEAP and Hawaiian Electric programs. If you already have a DCS voucher and fall behind, call DCS at (808) 768-7077 right away.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Honolulu Section 8 waiting list open?

No. As of November 5, 2026, the City and County of Honolulu's Section 8 page says "The Waitlist is now closed" and its FAQ says "No. The Waiting List is currently closed." The last pre-application window ran from July 14, 2025 at 7:45 a.m. to July 18, 2025 at 4:30 p.m. Hawai‘i time. DCS says the list "is not expected to reopen for approximately three years." The State's HPHA voucher and federal public housing lists on O‘ahu are also closed.

I applied in July 2025. How do I check my status?

Go to waitlistcheck.com and log in with the username and password you made when you applied. DCS says an ACTIVE status means your application was randomly selected for the wait list and an INACTIVE status means it was not. DCS placed 3,000 applicants on the list and says it will contact them as vouchers become available. For help, call (808) 768-7077 or email cchs8@honolulu.gov. Being on the list does not mean you are approved, and DCS says there is no guarantee how long the wait will be.

How much rent will I pay with a Honolulu voucher?

DCS says families "generally pay approximately 30% to 40% of their adjusted monthly income toward rent and utilities." Under HUD's rules, the base share is about 30% of adjusted monthly income, meaning gross income minus allowances such as those for dependents and, for elderly or disabled households, some medical costs. You pay more than 30% when the unit you choose rents above the DCS payment standard. DCS posts its 2026 payment standards and utility allowances on its Section 8 page.

What are the income limits for Section 8 in Honolulu?

DCS lists these 2026 limits by household size: $53,900 for 1 person, $61,600 for 2, $69,300 for 3, $77,000 for 4, $83,200 for 5 and $89,350 for 6. HPHA uses the same HUD limits for its programs. Meeting the limit does not get you a voucher while the lists are closed.

Who runs public housing in Honolulu?

The State's Hawai‘i Public Housing Authority, not the City. HPHA manages 5,406 federal public housing units and 864 State-funded units statewide. Its How to Apply page says the federal public housing lists on O‘ahu are closed. When a list opens, HPHA uses an online pre-application and gives you a confirmation number. Call HPHA at (808) 204-9042 with questions, or (808) 832-5961 if you need a reasonable accommodation to apply.

What is happening to Emergency Housing Vouchers in Honolulu?

DCS says HUD estimates the Department "will only have sufficient funds to cover all EHVs through most (but not all) of Calendar Year 2026." It also says "the vouchers of EHV families will be transitioned into the regular HCV program automatically" and "No action is required by the family since the City and County of Honolulu has received authorization from HUD." If you hold an EHV, read the DCS letter to EHV participants on its Section 8 page and call (808) 768-7077 with questions.
