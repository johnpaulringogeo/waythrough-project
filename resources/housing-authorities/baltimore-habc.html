Housing Authority of Baltimore City (HABC)

Waythrough is an independent resource, not HABC. This is a summary of public information. To apply, or to check the current waiting-list status, use the official links below.

Quick Facts

Serves: Baltimore City, Maryland (not Baltimore County)
Phone: (410) 396-3232
Official site: www.habc.org/
Apply: HABC application page
Status last checked: November 3, 2026

The Housing Authority of Baltimore City is the public housing agency for Baltimore City. HUD lists it as PHA MD002 with a program type of Combined, so it runs both the Housing Choice Voucher Program (HCVP, or Section 8) and public housing. Its central office is at 417 E. Fayette Street, and the Section 8 program works out of 1225 W. Pratt Street. The short version today: HABC's tenant-based Section 8 list and its public housing lists are both closed, and no HABC page gives a reopening date. This page quotes HABC's exact words on each list, covers the November 2026 closure of the Pratt Street office, how HABC picked names the last times its lists opened, the preference for City residents, and the Baltimore County Office of Housing, a separate agency that people often confuse with HABC.

Waiting-List Status

Waiting lists open and close often. The status below is what we found the last time we checked; confirm it on the official site before you count on it.

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8), tenant-based: Closed. Closed (checked November 3, 2026). HABC's HCVP Applicants page says: "Currently, the Housing Authority of Baltimore City HCVP Tenant Based Waiting List is closed to new applicants." Its waiting-list page says: "The HCVP Tenant-Based Waiting List Has CLOSED." No HABC page gives a reopening date. The last opening ran from 9:00 a.m. on October 2, 2023 to 11:59 p.m. on October 13, 2023. HABC says it accepted "all 27,842 applications" from that period and placed names through a Representative Random Selection Process, so applying early did not put anyone ahead.
  • Public Housing: Closed. HABC's My Application page says: "The HCVP Tenant-Based and the Public Housing waiting list(s) are CLOSED." The most recent public housing opening, posted on HABC's application portal, ran from 9:00 a.m. on Monday, August 17, 2026 to 11:59 p.m. on Monday, August 31, 2026, only for senior housing (62 and older), mixed-population housing and accessible units. It closed after August 31, and HABC said "A lottery system will be used to select applicants randomly" from those pre-applications. HABC also says its public housing lists are "changing to a Location-Based selection": you may pick up to three developments, or a first-available list, and you must update your application in the MyHousing portal when HABC's letter reaches you.
  • Project-Based Voucher (PBV): Closed; last window January 14–23, 2026. HABC's 2026 Project-Based Voucher page (checked November 3, 2026) now posts no opening or closing date, but the same page as archived on January 17, 2026 said the list "is open from 9:00 a.m. on January 14th, 2026 through 11:59 p.m. January 23rd, 2026." That window is over. The page says to read the FAQ "before the waiting list opens" and points to the MyHousing portal to check or update an application; its link to the public notice is empty.

Programs HABC Runs

  • Housing Choice Voucher Program (HCVP, Section 8), tenant-based vouchers
  • Project-Based Vouchers (PBV), attached to specific units, for households needing up to five bedrooms, elderly households and non-elderly single people with disabilities
  • Public housing, including senior, mixed-population and accessible units, with location-based waiting lists
  • Rental Assistance Demonstration (RAD) properties, former public housing converted to project-based subsidy, with a Choice Mobility option for some residents

How to Apply

Apply on the authority's own application page: habc-onthelist.myhousing.com/. If you have already applied, check or update your application in HABC's MyHousing applicant portal and keep your contact information current there, because HABC uses it to reach you; HABC does not publish list positions.

If this is your first time applying, our step-by-step guide to applying for Section 8 covers each stage, and how to find your PHA shows which authority covers where you live.

Other Authorities Serving This Area

  • Baltimore County Office of Housing (BCOH) is a separate agency for Baltimore County, the suburbs that surround the City. HUD lists it as MD033 with a program type of Section 8, so it runs vouchers and no public housing. Office: Drumcastle Government Center, 6401 York Road, First Floor, Baltimore, MD 21212, 410-887-2766 (on its own page and in HUD's list). Its Housing Choice Voucher page said on November 3, 2026: "There are currently no waiting lists open," and "BCOH will not be accepting any preliminary applications until further notice." BCOH says "Families with the earliest date and time of application are served first."
  • Other nearby agencies with their own lists (HUD codes and phones from HUD's Maryland PHA list): Howard County Housing Commission, MD023, (443) 518-7800; Housing Commission of Anne Arundel County, MD018, (410) 222-6205; Harford County Housing Agency, MD025, (410) 638-3045; and the Maryland Department of Housing and Community Development, MD901, which runs vouchers in parts of the state, (301) 429-7400.
  • HABC is not an emergency rent fund. If you are behind on rent, dial 211 and see our Baltimore emergency rent help guide: the City's CAP Centers, Maryland's free eviction lawyers and more. HABC's own public housing page sends people who need help now to the Baltimore City Shelter Hotline, 443-984-9540 (Monday to Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.), and to 211 Maryland (2-1-1 or 1-800-492-0618).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Baltimore Section 8 waiting list open?

No. As of November 3, 2026, HABC's HCVP Applicants page says: "Currently, the Housing Authority of Baltimore City HCVP Tenant Based Waiting List is closed to new applicants." No HABC page gives a reopening date. The last opening ran from October 2 to October 13, 2023, and HABC accepted 27,842 applications. If another website shows a newer date, check habc.org before you rely on it.

Can I apply for public housing in Baltimore right now?

No. HABC's My Application page says "The HCVP Tenant-Based and the Public Housing waiting list(s) are CLOSED." The last public housing opening ran from August 17 to August 31, 2026, only for senior, mixed-population and accessible housing, and it closed at 11:59 p.m. on August 31. HABC picked names from that window by lottery. Watch HABC's application portal, habc-onthelist.myhousing.com, for the next announcement.

Is the HABC Section 8 office open in November 2026?

Not to walk-ins. HABC says its HCVP office at 1225 W. Pratt Street "will be closed to the public due to renovations for the month of November 2026." Staff keep working, November appointments are held at the other location named in your appointment letter, and HABC asks people to use its online inquiry form or the HCVP Call Center at 443-984-2222. The closure is set for November only; before you go in person in December or later, check the notice on habc.org, and keep in mind HABC says the HCVP office requires an appointment.

I already applied. How do I check or update my HABC application?

Use HABC's MyHousing applicant portal at habc-onthelist.myhousing.com. HABC says applicants can update their contact information and family composition there, and that it uses the information on your pre-application to reach you. For the public housing lists, HABC says changes must be reported within 10 business days, and that if you do not respond to its notices or a letter comes back undeliverable, your name will be removed. HABC does not publish list positions; it says your wait depends on how many applications rank higher than yours and on funding.

Does HABC give a preference to Baltimore City residents?

Yes, according to HABC's proposed FY2026 Administrative Plan for the voucher program. Within each preference level, HABC gives priority to families who live in Baltimore City, and people who work or have been hired to work in the City count as residents. Families outside the City are considered only after the City list is exhausted. The top preference is for referrals only, such as emergency relocations; families with children, seniors, non-elderly people with disabilities and other families are treated equally at the next level.

What number should I call?

HABC's main office number is 410-396-3232. It is on HABC's Contact Us page and in HUD's directory for MD002. For Section 8, the HCVP Customer Relations Call Center is 443-984-2222, listed on the Contact Us page, the HCVP Applicants page (which calls it an automated line available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week) and the November renovation notice. Public housing Admissions and Leasing is 410-396-3225, from the Contact Us page. Baltimore County residents should call the Baltimore County Office of Housing at 410-887-2766 instead.
