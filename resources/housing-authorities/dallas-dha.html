Housing Authority

Dallas Housing Authority (DHA)

Serves Dallas and seven North Texas counties: Collin, Dallas, Denton, Ellis, Kaufman, Rockwall and Tarrant · Last checked October 15, 2026

Waythrough is an independent resource, not DHA. We summarize public information to help you get oriented. For applications and the most current waiting-list status, always use the official links below.

The Dallas Housing Authority, legally the Housing Authority of the City of Dallas, Texas, now calls itself DHA, Housing Solutions for North Texas. HUD lists it as PHA TX009 with a program type of Combined, and DHA says it provides housing across seven North Texas counties: Collin, Dallas, Denton, Ellis, Kaufman, Rockwall and Tarrant. Dallas works differently from most big cities. There is no closed list to wait out: DHA takes preliminary applications for both Section 8 and public housing at any time, then picks people by lottery when vouchers or units come free, and an application that is not picked expires after 18 months. Since July 13, 2026 it has run public housing lotteries every week on a published schedule. This page covers DHA's exact words, how the lottery and the 18-month clock work, the October 2026 lottery schedule, and why the phone number is harder to find than it should be.

Waiting-List Status

Waiting lists open and close frequently, so treat the status below as a starting point and confirm on the official site before you rely on it.

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8): Accepting preliminary applications at any time — Checked October 15, 2026. DHA's How to Apply page reads: "You may apply for the program through our open lottery system at any time," "DHA accepts pre-applications on an ongoing basis instead of limiting it to a few days," and "You can submit a preliminary application at any time." DHA's Administrative Plan says it has accepted preliminary applications since January 21, 2020 and that "until further notice, DHA will maintain a continuously open waiting list." Selection is random: "As voucher funds become available, DHA will run a lottery to randomly select applicants from the waiting list." An application that is not selected expires after 18 months, and DHA says "you may re-apply."
  • Public Housing (DHA-owned properties): Accepting preliminary applications at any time, by property — DHA's public housing apply page uses the same words, "ongoing basis" and "at any time," and you choose the properties you want. DHA's lottery page says: "We review each Public Housing property for vacancies once per month and conduct a lottery based on unit availability." A DHA news post dated July 10, 2026 says it now runs "a lottery each week for a set of properties," starting July 13, 2026. As of October 15, 2026, the lottery page listed pulls on October 5 (11 units), October 12 (19 units), October 19 (8 units) and October 26 (30 units), each naming the properties with vacancies.
  • Project-based and special purpose vouchers: Separate lists — DHA's Administrative Plan says project-based voucher properties built with Low Income Housing Tax Credits "will have separate waiting lists, maintained jointly by DHA and the property owner at the property," and that DHA keeps a separate waiting list for each HUD special use voucher. Walker Settlement vouchers are limited to class members. Ask the property, or DHA, about these lists directly.

Programs DHA Runs

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8), which DHA says serves more than 16,250 households
  • Public Housing at DHA-owned properties, which DHA describes as 31 properties with about 5,000 units
  • Project-Based Vouchers, including tax credit properties with their own lists
  • Special purpose vouchers and Walker Settlement vouchers, with separate waiting lists

How to Apply

Apply directly through the authority's official application page: dhantx.com/applicants/housing-choice-voucher-program/how-to-apply/. If you have already applied, call (214) 951-8300 to verify your position and keep your contact information current — if the authority cannot reach you when your name comes up, you can be dropped from the list.

New to the process? Our step-by-step guide to applying for Section 8 walks through the whole thing, and how to find your PHA helps you locate the right authority.

Other Authorities Serving This Area

  • Dallas County Housing Assistance Program is a separate, Section 8 only agency. HUD lists it as TX559, (214) 819-1871.
  • City programs nearby keep their own lists, including the Garland Housing Authority (TX435, (972) 205-3391), Grand Prairie Housing and Neighborhood Services (TX434, (972) 237-8176), the City of Mesquite Housing Office (TX436, (972) 329-8739), the Housing Authority of Plano (TX128, (972) 423-4928) and the Housing Authority of McKinney (TX027, (972) 542-5641).
  • Fort Worth is served by its own housing authority (HUD code TX004, (817) 333-3400) and Tarrant County by the Tarrant County Housing Assistance Office (TX431, (817) 531-7654). All of the numbers in this section are as HUD lists them, so confirm each one with the agency.
  • DHA is not an emergency rent fund. If you are behind on rent, the City of Dallas's own social services page says "Rental Assistance-Not accepting applications at this time" (checked October 21, 2026). Dallas County helps with rent only for residents with a disability, through its Welfare Assistance program at (214) 819-1800, and one of its requirements is "Not receiving housing assistance (e.g., Section 8)." Frequently Asked Questions

Can I apply for Section 8 in Dallas right now?

Yes. As of October 15, 2026, DHA's How to Apply page says "You can submit a preliminary application at any time" and "DHA accepts pre-applications on an ongoing basis instead of limiting it to a few days." Its Administrative Plan calls this a continuously open waiting list, in place since January 21, 2020. You apply by registering an account on DHA's RentCafe portal, linked from the How to Apply page, and DHA says the preliminary application takes about 20 minutes. You need an email address, the names and ages of everyone in your household, and your annual household income.

If the list is always open, why have I not been called?

Because DHA picks by lottery, not by date. DHA says: "As voucher funds become available, DHA will run a lottery to randomly select applicants from the waiting list." Its Administrative Plan says applicants are processed in lottery number order within each preference group. Applying early does not move you up, and being in the pool is not a promise of a voucher. If you are selected, DHA contacts you by email and mail, and you then have 14 days to complete the full application with documents such as proof of income, bank statements and Social Security information.

Does my DHA application expire?

Yes, after 18 months. DHA's How to Apply page says "Once you apply, your application will stay on the waiting list until you are selected, or for up to 18 months," and "Preliminary applications not selected through the lottery process expire in 18 months, but you may re-apply." DHA's lottery page says it will notify you as your application nears the expiration date. Write down the date you applied and re-apply when it runs out rather than assuming you are still in the pool.

How do the weekly public housing lotteries work?

DHA reviews each public housing property for vacancies once a month and draws from the applicants for the properties that have openings. Since July 13, 2026 it has run a lottery each week for a set of properties, and it publishes the schedule. As of October 15, 2026, its lottery page listed 11 units for October 5, 19 for October 12, 8 for October 19 and 30 for October 26, with the property names for each. If you are selected, DHA emails the address on your preliminary application, so check that inbox and its spam folder.

How do I check my DHA application status?

Online. DHA's applicant pages say: "You can check the status of your application at any time by logging into the RentCafe Applicant Portal." Use the same email and password you registered with. For other questions, DHA's current client pages give the customer service email dhacustomerservice@dhantx.com.

What is DHA's phone number?

(214) 951-8300. It is the number HUD lists for TX009, and DHA prints it on its own forms, including its Change of Ownership and/or Management packet for landlords and its Visitor Registration Form. It does not appear on DHA's web pages, which list no general phone number and only an email, info@dhantx.com. DHA's headquarters is at 3939 N. Hampton Road, Dallas, TX 75212, open Monday through Thursday from 8 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. and Friday from 8 a.m. to noon.

What are DHA's income limits?

DHA publishes the limits HUD set for Dallas on May 1, 2026, with an area median income of $121,100. The very low income limit, 50 percent of median, is $42,400 for one person, $48,450 for two, $54,500 for three and $60,550 for four. The extremely low income limit, 30 percent, is $25,450 for one person and $36,350 for four, and the low income limit, 80 percent, is $67,850 for one person and $96,900 for four. DHA's Applicants page has the full table up to eight people.

Can DHA help me if I need housing this week?

Not quickly. DHA says: "DHA is not an emergency housing provider. Our programs are often full and it can take months for resources to open up for new candidates to have access to housing assistance through DHA." Apply anyway, because the pool only helps you if you are in it, but look for shelter and emergency rental help at the same time. DHA keeps an Emergency Housing Resources page for that.
