Miami-Dade Housing and Community Development (HCD, formerly PHCD)

Serves Miami-Dade County, Florida, including the City of Miami · Last checked November 3, 2026

Quick Facts

Serves: Miami-Dade County, Florida, including the City of Miami
Phone: (786) 469-4100
Official site: www.miamidade.gov/global/housing/home.page
Apply: Miami-Dade HCD application page
Status last checked: November 3, 2026

Miami-Dade County's Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) says it "serves as the County's official Public Housing Agency (PHA)." It used to call itself Public Housing and Community Development (PHCD), and HUD's Florida list still shows it under that older name as PHA FL005, program type Combined, at 701 NW 1st Court in Overtown. HCD says it manages about 6,500 public housing units and provides rental assistance to more than 19,000 households through Housing Choice Vouchers. The short version today: HCD's single waiting list for Section 8 and its project-based units is closed for registration. The last voucher registration ran February 5 to 19, 2024, and a computer drawing on March 18, 2024 picked 5,000 applications for the list. This page covers HCD's exact words, how the drawing and the preferences work, which phone number does what, and the separate authorities people confuse with HCD: the City of Miami's own small Section 8 program, Hialeah, Miami Beach and Homestead.

Waiting-List Status

Waiting lists open and close frequently, so treat the status below as a starting point and confirm on the official site before you rely on it.

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8) and project-based units (Public Housing, Moderate Rehabilitation, Section 8 New Construction): Closed — Closed (checked November 3, 2026). HCD's Housing and Section 8 page says there is "a single online application available only during an open registration period" for both project-based and tenant-based units, and reads: "The waiting list is closed for registration." HCD's voucher portal, miamidadevoucher.myhousing.com, reads: "REGISTRATION IS CLOSED." The portal's older text estimates that registration "may open again in about 12 to 18 months" but gives no date, and that estimate dates from the 2024 round, so do not count on it. The last registration HCD documents ran from midnight February 5, 2024 to 11:59 p.m. February 19, 2024, online only, and the random drawing on March 18, 2024 picked 5,000 applications.
  • County-owned mixed-income properties: Apply at each property — HCD's page for its mixed-income properties, such as Lakeside Towers and Park Lake in Kendall, says "all affordable properties accept applications onsite and you should contact the management office directly for information about availability" (checked November 3, 2026). HCD does not post an open or closed status for them, so call the property.

Programs Miami-Dade HCD Runs

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8), more than 19,000 households
  • Public Housing, about 6,500 units across Miami-Dade
  • Section 8 Moderate Rehabilitation, about 2,097 units in 48 privately owned buildings
  • Section 8 New Construction and County-owned mixed-income properties
  • Affordable housing development, homeownership and Documentary Surtax programs as the County's community development office

How to Apply

Apply directly through the authority's official application page: www.miamidade.gov/global/service.page?Mduid_service=ser1544119346614848. If you are on an HCD waiting list, HCD says you can look up your ranking/position number on its voucher portal or in the posted list for your year, and that the number never changes; to update your mailing address or household members, mail or drop off HCD’s change forms at the Applicant Leasing Center, 701 NW 1st Court, 14th Floor, 786-469-4206, because the appointment letter is sent by mail.

New to the process? Our step-by-step guide to applying for Section 8 walks through the whole thing, and how to find your PHA helps you locate the right authority.

Other Authorities Serving This Area

  • The City of Miami runs its own, separate Section 8 program. HUD lists it as PHA FL145, program type Section 8, under the name "Miami Hsg Conservation," at 444 SW 2nd Ave., (305) 416-2080. The City's Section 8 page reads: "The City's Section 8 program is not accepting new applications," and its questions and answers say both its voucher and project-based programs "ARE PRESENTLY CLOSED AND NOT ACCEPTING APPLICATIONS." The City says its voucher application period closed in October 2014 and it is still working through that list; people on it should call 305-416-1572 to update their contact information (checked November 3, 2026).
  • Hialeah Housing Authority is a separate agency for the City of Hialeah, HUD code FL066, program type Combined, at 75 E. 6th St., 305-888-9744. Its application page reads: "Currently the Section 8/HCVP waiting list is closed." Its last documented opening ran April 21 to May 4, 2017, when a lottery picked 2,500 of 60,919 pre-applications (checked November 3, 2026).
  • Housing Authority of the City of Miami Beach, HUD code FL017, program type Combined, at 200 Alton Road, 305-532-6401. It says its voucher jurisdiction "covers Miami Beach and extends ten miles outside the city." On November 3, 2026, its Waitlists page marked every list it names, from the Rebecca Tower North project-based list to its affordable buildings, "Waitlist CLOSED."
  • Housing Authority of the City of Homestead, HUD code FL068, program type Section 8, at 29355 S. Federal Highway, (305) 247-0639, per HUD's Florida list.
  • HCD is not an emergency rent fund. If you are behind on rent, call the County's Housing Advocacy Hotline at 786-469-4545 and the Homeless Helpline at 1-877-994-4357 (press 1 for prevention), and see our Miami emergency rent help guide: what is open, what is closed or paused, light bill help, free eviction lawyers, and Florida's 3-day notice and 5-day answer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Miami-Dade Section 8 waiting list open?

No. As of November 3, 2026, HCD's Housing and Section 8 page says: "The waiting list is closed for registration." The same single application covers Section 8 vouchers and HCD's project-based units, including public housing. HCD's voucher portal also reads "REGISTRATION IS CLOSED." HCD gives no date for the next registration, so treat any date you see elsewhere as unconfirmed unless HCD's own site shows it.

When did HCD last open the list, and how were people picked?

The last registration ran from midnight February 5, 2024 to 11:59 p.m. February 19, 2024, online only. All applications went into a computer-generated drawing on March 18, 2024, and only 5,000 were picked, so the day you applied did not matter. HCD's 2024 FAQ ranks the people picked by these preferences: terminated by HCD for insufficient funding, displaced by a natural disaster, displaced by government action, homeless, veterans, the Mainstream preference, and Miami-Dade County residency. HCD says it announces registration in the Miami Herald, the Miami Times, Diario Las Americas and Le Floridien.

Is PHCD the same as HCD?

Yes. The County department now calls itself Miami-Dade Housing and Community Development (HCD). Its 2024 Section 8 notices used the older name, Public Housing and Community Development (PHCD), and HUD's PHA list still shows Miami Dade Public Housing and Community Dev under code FL005. It is the same agency at 701 NW 1st Court.

Does the City of Miami have its own Section 8 program?

Yes, a small one, separate from the County's. HUD lists it as PHA FL145. The City's Section 8 page says "The City's Section 8 program is not accepting new applications," and that both its voucher and project-based programs are closed. Its voucher application period closed in October 2014. If you are on that City list, call 305-416-1572 to update your contact information. Most Miami residents get vouchers through the County's HCD.

Are the Hialeah and Miami Beach housing authorities part of HCD?

No. Each runs its own lists. The Hialeah Housing Authority (305-888-9744) says its Section 8 waiting list is currently closed; its last opening was in 2017. The Housing Authority of the City of Miami Beach (305-532-6401) marked every list on its Waitlists page closed on November 3, 2026. Homestead has its own housing authority too, at (305) 247-0639 per HUD's list.

What number should I call?

786-469-4100 is HCD's main number; it is on every HCD page. For the voucher program, HCD's Housing Choice Voucher page and its 2024 FAQ give 786-654-8440, and HCD's Contact page gives 305-403-3222 to request an appointment. HCD's own pages disagree on the Applicant Leasing Center: the Housing and Section 8 page lists 786-469-4206, while the Contact page lists 786-469-4300; both put the center at 701 NW 1st Court, 14th Floor. HUD's list shows (786) 469-4106 for FL005.
