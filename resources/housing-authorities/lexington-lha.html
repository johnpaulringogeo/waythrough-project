Quick Facts

Official site: www.lexha.org/
Apply: LHA application page
Status last checked: November 12, 2026

The Lexington-Fayette Urban County Housing Authority, which calls itself LHA or the Lexington Housing Authority, runs Section 8 Housing Choice Vouchers, public housing and project-based vouchers in Lexington. HUD lists it as PHA KY004, under the name Housing Authority of Lexington, with a program type of Combined, at 300 West New Circle Road, Lexington, KY 40505. Its main line is (859) 281-5060, Monday through Friday, 8:00 am to 4:30 pm, and its offices see people by appointment. LHA says it manages 880 public housing units, 600 mixed-finance units and over 3,600 Section 8 vouchers, and it is a Moving to Work agency. The short version today: the Section 8 voucher list is closed, but LHA has open lists for one-bedroom senior apartments at Ballard and Connie Griffith and for two- and three-bedroom units at Centre Meadows, a project-based voucher site with a work requirement. All applications are online. This page covers LHA's exact words, each open list, the phone numbers, and where to turn if you need rent help now.

Waiting-List Status

Waiting lists open and close often. The status below is what we found the last time we checked; confirm it on the official site before you count on it.

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8): Closed. Checked November 12, 2026: LHA's Housing Choice Voucher page says "The LHA's HCV waiting list is currently closed." Its homepage says the same: "The LHA Section 8 waiting list is currently closed." No reopening date is posted.
  • Ballard and Connie Griffith Apartments (seniors 62+, 1 bedroom): Open. LHA's homepage says "The Lexington Housing Authority wait list is open for ONE (1) bedroom units at the Ballard and Connie Griffith Apartments for elderly persons (62 years of age or older) households, located at 650 Tower Plaza." LHA's applicant portal lists Connie Griffith Manor and Ballard Place as open from June 26, 2023 to June 30, 2029. Apply online at lexha.myhousing.com or call (859) 281-5053 (checked November 12, 2026).
  • Centre Meadows, 2 bedrooms (project-based voucher): Open. LHA says "The Centre Meadows waiting list is open for TWO (2) bedroom units at the Centre Meadows located at 1301, 1317, & 1333 Centre Parkway." No closing date is posted. Centre Meadows is a self-sufficiency site: the head of household or spouse or co-head must show work of at least 20 hours a week, with exceptions for elderly and disabled applicants who meet the other rules. LHA says "Interested persons MUST apply online ONLY"; if you have no internet, call the Centre Meadows office at 859-281-5087 for help (checked November 12, 2026).
  • Centre Meadows, 3 bedrooms (project-based voucher): Dated window. LHA's notice says it "will accept applications" for Centre Meadows three-bedroom units "starting 12:00 a.m., Monday, October 12, 2026 through 11:59 p.m. Friday, November 13, 2026." LHA's applicant portal shows a later end date for Centre Meadows, December 13, 2026, so the two LHA pages do not match. Apply as early as you can and do not count on the later date. If the window has passed when you read this, check lexha.org for the next opening. Same work requirement and online-only rule as the two-bedroom list (checked November 12, 2026).
  • Other public housing lists: Not posted. LHA's Public Housing page still says "LHA to open Northeast Public Housing Waitlist June 21, 2026," but posts no current details, and its applicant portal does not list that list among its open waiting lists. When a list opens, LHA says applications are taken online. Call (859) 281-5060 to ask which public housing lists are taking names (checked November 12, 2026).

Programs LHA Runs

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8); the waiting list is closed. LHA says voucher holders pay the greater of $50, 30% of adjusted income or 10% of gross income
  • Public housing: LHA says rent is the greater of $150, 30% of adjusted income or 10% of gross income, or a flat rent set for each property. About 850 units are self-sufficiency units, where a household member must work at least 20 or 37.5 hours a week, depending on the site
  • Ballard and Connie Griffith Apartments, 650 Tower Plaza: one-bedroom independent living for people 62 and older, all utilities included; call (859) 281-5053
  • Centre Meadows, 1301, 1317 and 1333 Centre Parkway: project-based voucher (RAD-PBV) apartments with a 20-hour-a-week work requirement; management office (859) 281-5087
  • Special partner programs that pair vouchers with services, including HUD-VASH with the Lexington VA Medical Center and Greenhouse17 for survivors of domestic violence
  • Homeownership through the voucher program, for current voucher or public housing participants

How to Apply

Apply on the authority's own application page: lexha.myhousing.com/. If you have already applied, LHA says it will notify you by mail when your name comes to the top of the list, and that if you do not respond or your letter comes back undeliverable, "your name will be removed from the waiting list." Report any change of address, phone, income or family size within 10 business days, in writing: LHA says "No changes will be taken over the phone." You can update your application on LHA's online portal; for help, call (859) 281-5060.

If this is your first time applying, our step-by-step guide to applying for Section 8 covers each stage, and how to find your PHA shows which authority covers where you live.

Other Authorities Serving This Area

  • LHA is not an emergency rent fund. If you are behind on rent in Fayette County, call the City of Lexington's Community & Resident Services at (859) 300-5300 to book an emergency financial assistance appointment, and see our Lexington emergency rent help guide for Legal Aid of the Bluegrass, 211, LIHEAP and Kentucky's eviction deadlines.
  • Already have LHA housing or a voucher and fell behind? Call LHA at (859) 281-5060 right away and call Legal Aid of the Bluegrass at 859-431-8200. Legal Aid of the Bluegrass says it helps people facing termination of public housing or Section 8, and an eviction can cost you your assistance.
  • Disability accommodations: LHA says accessible apartments are available at ten locations and that questions go to its 504 Coordinator at (859) 281-5060. People with hearing or speech impairments can reach LHA through Kentucky Relay at 711.
  • If you live outside Fayette County, a different housing authority serves you. Use HUD's PHA directory to find it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Lexington Section 8 waiting list open?

No. As of November 12, 2026, LHA says its Housing Choice Voucher waiting list is currently closed, and no reopening date is posted. Some LHA lists are open, though: one-bedroom senior apartments at Ballard and Connie Griffith for people 62 and older, and two-bedroom units at Centre Meadows, a project-based voucher site with a work requirement.

Is the Centre Meadows three-bedroom list open?

LHA's notice says it is taking applications from October 12 through 11:59 p.m. on Friday, November 13, 2026. LHA's applicant portal shows a later end date of December 13, 2026. Apply online at lexha.myhousing.com as early as you can and do not count on the later date. If the window has closed, check lexha.org for the next one.

How do I apply to the Lexington Housing Authority?

Online at lexha.myhousing.com, during the period a list is open. LHA asks for the name, date of birth and Social Security number of everyone in your household, your mailing address and your total household income. Submit only one application. If you need help with the online form, call (859) 281-5060, or 859-281-5087 for Centre Meadows.

How do I keep my place on an LHA list?

LHA notifies applicants by mail when their name comes to the top. If you do not respond, or the letter comes back undeliverable, LHA removes your name. Report a change of address, phone, income or family size within 10 business days, in writing. LHA says it takes no changes over the phone.

What does an LHA tenant pay?

LHA says Section 8 voucher holders pay the greater of $50, 30% of adjusted income or 10% of gross income. In public housing, rent is the greater of $150, 30% of adjusted income or 10% of gross income, or the flat rent set for the property. New public housing tenants also pay a security deposit, and at some sites the utility deposits and bills.

Can a Lexington landlord refuse my voucher?

Yes. Since March 6, 2024, Kentucky law (House Bill 18) bars any city or urban-county government from adopting or enforcing a rule that stops a landlord from refusing a tenant whose rent includes federal housing assistance. A landlord still cannot refuse you because of race, disability, family status or another protected trait under federal fair housing law; report that to HUD.
