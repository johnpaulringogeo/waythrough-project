Housing Authority

Atlanta Housing (Atlanta Housing Authority)

Serves the City of Atlanta, Georgia · Last checked October 15, 2026

Waythrough is an independent resource, not Atlanta Housing. We summarize public information to help you get oriented. For applications and the most current waiting-list status, always use the official links below.

Atlanta Housing is the name the Housing Authority of the City of Atlanta, Georgia has used since its 2018 rebranding, and many people still call it AHA or the Atlanta Housing Authority. HUD lists it as PHA GA006 with a program type of Combined, and it is a Moving to Work agency, which is why it uses its own names for programs: HomeFlex for what used to be called Project Based Rental Assistance, Mixed for the mixed-income communities built on former public housing sites, and AH-owned for the buildings it still owns. The answer most people want is short. The Housing Choice Voucher list is closed, and the last opening closed on March 27, 2017. Everything else runs through property-by-property waiting lists, and Atlanta Housing does not publish a current list of which ones are open. This page covers AH's exact words, how it says it will announce a voucher opening, how each kind of list picks people, and the other housing authorities around Atlanta.

Waiting-List Status

Waiting lists open and close frequently, so treat the status below as a starting point and confirm on the official site before you rely on it.

  • Housing Choice Voucher Program (Section 8): Closed — Closed (checked October 15, 2026). Atlanta Housing's FAQ reads: "No, the Housing Choice Voucher Program is not accepting applications at this time." AH's Housing Choice overview, linked as its Waiting List page, says: "The most recent opening of the Housing Choice Voucher Program Waiting closed on March 27, 2017." AH's FY2026 Moving to Work report, adopted in September 2026, lists the voucher waiting list at 20,874 households and closed, and its FY2027 plan says AH does not plan to change the waiting list. No reopening date is published.
  • HomeFlex, Mixed and AH-owned communities: Site-based lists; no current open list published — AH's Housing Options page says "Each property maintains a site-based waiting list," and its Housing Programs page tells applicants they "must first contact each community's property management office to find out if the waiting list is open." The open-or-closed list AH links from that page is dated March 2018, so do not rely on it. AH's FY2026 Moving to Work report counts some site-based lists as open and others as closed as of June 30, 2026, without naming the properties. Its current property listings, dated July 10, 2026 for HomeFlex and January 1, 2025 for AH-owned communities, give names and contacts but no waiting-list status.

Programs Atlanta Housing Runs

  • Housing Choice Voucher Program (tenant-based Section 8)
  • HomeFlex, formerly called Project Based Rental Assistance: the subsidy stays with the property
  • Mixed communities, formerly called AH Sponsored Communities, on the sites of former public housing
  • AH-owned residential communities, including senior high-rises and small family communities
  • Supportive housing (HAVEN) and homeownership programs

How to Apply

Apply directly through the authority's official application page: my.atlantahousing.org/. If you have already applied, call (404) 892-4700 to verify your position and keep your contact information current — if the authority cannot reach you when your name comes up, you can be dropped from the list.

New to the process? Our step-by-step guide to applying for Section 8 walks through the whole thing, and how to find your PHA helps you locate the right authority.

Other Authorities Serving This Area

  • Housing Authority of Fulton County is a separate agency from Atlanta Housing. HUD lists it as GA264, program type Combined, phone (404) 588-4950.
  • Housing Authority of DeKalb County, HUD code GA237, program type Combined, (404) 270-2500. The Housing Authority of the City of Decatur is GA011, (404) 270-2100.
  • Smaller city authorities in the metro include East Point (GA078, (404) 768-0078), College Park (GA232, (404) 761-2805) and Marietta (GA010, (770) 419-3200).
  • Georgia Department of Community Affairs runs the statewide voucher program for areas without a local housing authority; HUD lists it as GA901, Georgia Residential Finance, (404) 679-4940. All of the numbers in this section are as HUD lists them, so confirm each one with the agency.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Atlanta Section 8 waiting list open?

No. As of October 15, 2026, Atlanta Housing's FAQ says: "No, the Housing Choice Voucher Program is not accepting applications at this time." The last opening closed on March 27, 2017, according to AH's own Housing Choice overview, and AH's September 2026 Moving to Work report still lists the voucher list as closed, with 20,874 households on it. AH publishes no reopening date.

How will Atlanta Housing announce the next voucher opening?

AH's Housing Programs page says: "When AH opens the waiting list, we will put a notice on our website and an advertisement in the legal section of the Atlanta Journal Constitution. We also will get the message out to the public through radio and other media." Its FAQ adds local media outlets and social service agencies. AH also says "You can only register when the waiting list is open," so there is no way to get on the voucher list early.

How does Atlanta Housing pick people from the voucher list?

At random. AH's FAQ says it uses "a computerized, random selection process to draw applicants from the Waiting List on a periodic basis," and that "All Housing Choice Voucher Program applicants have an equal chance of being drawn." AH's FY2027 Moving to Work plan describes a local residency and employment preference that helps families who live or work in the City of Atlanta get a voucher before others. The FAQ also says the program does not have a preference for homeless, veteran, disabled, elderly or domestic violence circumstances.

How do I find out which Atlanta properties are taking applications?

Call the properties. AH's Housing Programs page says you "must first contact each community's property management office to find out if the waiting list is open," and that property managers "must pull people from their waiting lists in order of the date each person applied." The open-or-closed list it links is dated March 2018 and is out of date. AH's current HomeFlex and AH-owned property listings name the communities, so use them to find who to call. Once you are on a list, answer every letter the community sends and keep your contact details current.

I applied years ago. How do I check my status?

For the voucher list, AH's FAQ says: "To determine your status on the Housing Choice Voucher Program waiting list, please contact customer services at 404-892-4700." For a HomeFlex, Mixed or AH-owned community, ask that community's management office, because each keeps its own list. AH's applicant and participant portal is at my.atlantahousing.org.

What phone number and office should I use?

(404) 892-4700 is Atlanta Housing's number. It is in the footer of every AH page, in its FAQ as the customer services line, and on its applicant portal, and HUD lists the same number for GA006. The office is at 230 John Wesley Dobbs Avenue NE, Atlanta, GA 30303, open Monday through Friday except holidays, 8 am to 5 pm. AH's site currently warns of higher than normal call volume and says a staff member will respond to its online contact form within 24 to 48 hours.

What rules come with an Atlanta Housing voucher?

A few that surprise people. AH's Housing Programs page says: "During the first year of using a voucher, you must live within the city of Atlanta." AH's FAQ sets the tenant's share of rent at 30 percent of adjusted monthly income or a $125 minimum rent, whichever is higher. AH's eligibility page also has a work requirement: one adult between 18 and 61 who is not disabled must work at least 20 hours a week, and other adults in that group must work or be in school or training for 20 hours.

Is the Housing Authority of Fulton County the same as Atlanta Housing?

No. HUD lists them as two different agencies: Atlanta Housing is GA006 and serves the City of Atlanta, while the Housing Authority of Fulton County is GA264. DeKalb County, Decatur, East Point, College Park and Marietta each have their own authority too, and each keeps its own waiting list, so applying to Atlanta Housing does not put you on theirs.
