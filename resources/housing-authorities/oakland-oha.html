Quick Facts

Status last checked: November 3, 2026

The Oakland Housing Authority (OHA) is the public housing agency for the City of Oakland. HUD lists it as PHA CA003 with a program type of Combined, so it runs both Housing Choice Vouchers (Section 8) and public housing, which OHA now calls Affordable (Public) Housing. OHA is a Moving to Work agency. Its main office is at 1619 Harrison Street, and its Eligibility Department, which handles applicants, is at 1540 Webster Street. The short version today: every waiting list OHA runs is closed, OHA publishes no reopening date, and if you already sent a pre-application, OHA will contact you by email, not by phone. This page covers OHA's exact words, how its lottery works, the preferences in its 2026 Administrative Plan, and the other housing authorities in Alameda County, especially the Housing Authority of the County of Alameda (HACA), which people often mistake for OHA.

Waiting-List Status

Waiting lists open and close frequently, so treat the status below as a starting point and confirm on the official site before you rely on it.

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8): Closed — Closed (checked November 3, 2026). OHA's Waitlists page lists "Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8)" with the status "Closed". OHA gives no closing date and no reopening date. OHA says that when the list is open, "OHA announces publicly that it will accept Pre-Applications" and then uses a lottery to put them in order, so applying early in an opening does not put you ahead.
  • Affordable (Public) Housing: Closed — The same table lists "OHA Affordable (Public) Housing" as "Closed" (checked November 3, 2026). These are site-based lists for OHA's own buildings: when one opens, OHA gives each pre-application a random lottery number, and names are drawn from that site's list as apartments come open.
  • Project-Based Voucher (OAHPI PBV): Closed — Listed as "OAHPI Project-Based Voucher (PBV)": "Closed" (checked November 3, 2026).
  • Mainstream Voucher Program: Closed — Listed as "OHA Mainstream Voucher Program": "Closed" (checked November 3, 2026). OHA also posts openings at other agencies as a courtesy; that row, "Other (Non-OHA) Agencies", showed "Closed" too.

Programs OHA Runs

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8), including Moving to Work vouchers, VASH vouchers for veterans, Mainstream vouchers for non-elderly people with disabilities, and Foster Youth to Independence vouchers
  • Project-Based Vouchers, where the subsidy stays with the apartment (OHA lists its PBV waiting list as OAHPI Project-Based Voucher)
  • Affordable (Public) Housing in apartment buildings OHA owns and operates in Oakland
  • Shelter Plus Care for unhoused people with disabilities, run by Alameda County Housing and Community Development with OHA and the other county housing authorities
  • Family Unification and the Section 8 Homeownership Program

How to Apply

Apply directly through the authority's official application page: myohaportal.oakha.org/. If you have already applied, do not call to check: OHA says "Status is not provided by telephone" and that it will email you about your pre-application status, so keep your email address current and report a new address through the OHA Applicant Portal.

New to the process? Our step-by-step guide to applying for Section 8 walks through the whole thing, and how to find your PHA helps you locate the right authority.

Other Authorities Serving This Area

  • Housing Authority of the County of Alameda (HACA) is a separate agency, HUD code CA067, at 22941 Atherton Street, Hayward, CA 94541, (510) 538-8876. HACA says that because the cities of Alameda, Berkeley, Livermore and Oakland "each have their own Housing Authorities," it "does not provide rental assistance to those cities." It serves Albany, Dublin, Emeryville, Fremont, Hayward, Newark, Pleasanton, San Leandro, Union City and unincorporated areas such as Castro Valley, San Lorenzo, Ashland and Cherryland. Its Applying for Housing page reads "HACA WAITLIST IS NOW CLOSED" (checked November 3, 2026); it stopped taking pre-applications on May 6, 2024, and says it will advertise any opening at haca.net and at (510) 538-8876, extension 1.
  • Other city housing authorities in Alameda County, per HUD's California PHA list: Berkeley Housing Authority (CA058), (510) 488-5470; City of Alameda Housing Authority (CA062), (510) 747-4300; and Housing Authority of the City of Livermore (CA074), (925) 447-3600. We did not check their waiting lists for this page, so call or check their sites. OHA's own How to Apply page also links Bay Area authorities in San Francisco, Marin and Contra Costa County.
  • OHA is not an emergency housing agency. OHA says it "does not offer emergency housing or operate homeless shelters." For shelter and urgent help, dial 2-1-1 (Eden I&R) or the shelter hotline OHA lists, 1 (800) 774-3583. If you are behind on rent, see our Oakland emergency rent help guide: the City-funded Keep People Housed program, free and low-cost eviction lawyers, the Rent Adjustment Program's counselors and California's eviction deadlines. Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Oakland Section 8 waiting list open?

No. As of November 3, 2026, OHA's Waitlists page shows "Closed" for the Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8) list, and also for OHA Affordable (Public) Housing, the OAHPI Project-Based Voucher list and the OHA Mainstream Voucher Program. OHA does not publish a reopening date. When a list opens, OHA announces it publicly and takes pre-applications, so check oakha.org and treat any date you see elsewhere as unconfirmed unless OHA's own site shows it.

I already applied to OHA. How do I check my status?

By email, not by phone. OHA's Waitlists page says: "Status is not provided by telephone. OHA will communicate with Applicants via email about their pre-application status." It says your pre-application goes through a lottery for a place on one or more lists. Keep your email address current. To report a new address, use OHA's Applicant Portal at myohaportal.oakha.org, or mail a written request to the Oakland Housing Authority, Eligibility Department, 1540 Webster Street, Oakland, CA 94612.

How does OHA pick people from the waiting list?

By lottery, then by preference. OHA says it "accepts pre-applications when we anticipate housing becoming available within a few months," gives each pre-application a random lottery number, and builds the waiting list from the lottery. Its 2026 Administrative Plan says voucher families "will be selected from the waiting list in the order of their assigned lottery number and according to OHA preferences." Before an offer, OHA verifies income, assets and household members and runs a criminal background check on adults.

What preferences does OHA use for Section 8?

OHA's 2026 Administrative Plan lists local preferences for the voucher list, including a residency preference for applicants who live or work in Oakland, or have household members enrolled in school there; a family preference for households of two or more people and for single applicants who are elderly or have a disability; a veteran and active military preference; a homeless preference; and referrals from the Alameda County Continuum of Care Coordinated Entry system. Bring proof of residency, work or school to your interview, because OHA checks it then.

Is OHA the same as the Alameda County Housing Authority?

No. The Housing Authority of the County of Alameda (HACA) is a separate agency in Hayward, and it does not serve Oakland. HACA covers cities such as Hayward, Fremont, San Leandro, Union City, Dublin and Pleasanton, plus unincorporated areas like Castro Valley and San Lorenzo. Its list is closed too: HACA stopped taking pre-applications on May 6, 2024. The cities of Berkeley, Alameda and Livermore each have their own housing authority as well.

What number should I call at OHA?

(510) 874-1500 is OHA's main number. It is in the footer of every OHA page and on its Waitlists page, and HUD's directory lists the same number for CA003. For voucher (Leased Housing) questions, OHA lists (510) 587-2100 and lhcustomerservice@oakha.org on its Contact and Voucher Based Housing pages and its portal. OHA's Customer Service Line, on its Contact page, is (510) 874-1653. TTY: (510) 587-7119. Remember that OHA does not give waiting-list status by phone.
