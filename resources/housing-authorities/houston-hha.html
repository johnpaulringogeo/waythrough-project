Housing Alliance HTX (Houston Housing Authority)

Serves the City of Houston, Texas · Last checked November 2026

Waythrough is an independent resource, not Housing Alliance HTX. This is a summary of public information. To apply, or to check the current waiting-list status, use the official links below.

Quick Facts

Serves: the City of Houston, Texas
Phone: (713) 260-0500
Official site: www.alliancehtx.org/
Apply: Housing Alliance HTX application page
Status last checked: November 2026

Housing Alliance HTX (the Houston Housing Authority, or HHA, which began operating under the name Housing Alliance HTX in 2025) is the public housing authority (PHA) that administers Section 8 Housing Choice Vouchers, public housing, and project-based vouchers for the City of Houston. This page summarizes HHA's current waiting-list status, contact information, and how to apply, and links to the official HHA site for anything time-sensitive.

Waiting-List Status

Waiting lists open and close often. The status below is what we found the last time we checked; confirm it on the official site before you count on it.

  • Housing Choice Voucher (tenant-based Section 8): Closed. In June 2026 HHA said "The Housing Choice Voucher (HCV) waitlist remains closed, and no new HCV applications will be accepted," and its news page announced no reopening as of November 3, 2026. If you are already on it, make sure you completed the required re-registration, because people who did not re-register can lose their place.
  • Public Housing: Closed. A site-based public-housing lottery for eight named properties accepted applications from June 22 to July 6, 2026, and closed at 12:01 a.m. on July 7 after about 56,000 people applied; HHA said it expected to add about 10,000 of them to the waitlists and to notify applicants by email through the RentCafe portal.
  • Project-Based Vouchers (PBV): Varies by property. PBV lists are tied to specific developments; check the official site for current openings.

Programs Housing Alliance HTX Runs

  • Housing Choice Voucher (tenant-based Section 8)
  • Public Housing
  • Project-Based Vouchers (PBV)
  • HUD-VASH (for veterans)
  • Mainstream vouchers (non-elderly people with disabilities)
  • Family Self-Sufficiency (FSS) and homeownership programs

How to Apply

Apply on the authority's own application page: www.alliancehtx.org/rent-cafe-access. If you already applied, call (713) 260-0500 to check your place on the list, and give them your new phone number or address every time it changes. If they can't reach you when your name comes up, they can drop you from the list.

If this is your first time applying, our step-by-step guide to applying for Section 8 covers each stage, and how to find your PHA shows which authority covers where you live.

Other Authorities Serving This Area

  • Harris County Housing Authority (HCHA) is a separate authority serving unincorporated Harris County outside the Houston city limits, with its own waiting lists (hchatexas.org).
  • The Texas Department of Housing and Community Affairs (TDHCA) administers Section 8 in parts of Texas that do not have a local PHA.
  • Housing Alliance HTX is not an emergency rent fund. If you are behind on rent, Catholic Charities' Harris County rent help says "The application is currently closed" (checked November 3, 2026), and Harris County's Community Resilience Rental Assistance Program is reached through 2-1-1. Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Houston (HHA) Section 8 waiting list open?

No. In June 2026 HHA said its tenant-based Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8) waiting list remains closed and no new applications will be accepted, and no reopening had been announced as of November 3, 2026. Its site-based public-housing lottery for eight properties closed on July 7, 2026. Always confirm the current status on the official HHA site before relying on it.

Is the Houston Housing Authority the same as Housing Alliance HTX?

Yes. In 2025 the Houston Housing Authority began operating under the name Housing Alliance HTX. It is the same public housing authority. Its official website is now alliancehtx.org; the old housingforhouston.com address no longer loads.

Who do I contact if I live outside the Houston city limits?

The Harris County Housing Authority (HCHA) serves unincorporated Harris County outside the city and runs its own separate waiting lists at hchatexas.org. It is a different agency from the Houston Housing Authority.

How do I check my HHA waiting-list status?

Use the Rent Café portal (the Check Waitlist Status link on alliancehtx.org) rather than calling the office. Keep your contact information current so HHA can reach you when your name comes up.
