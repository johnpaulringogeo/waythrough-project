Housing Authority

Denver Housing Authority (DHA)

Serves the City and County of Denver, Colorado · Last checked October 15, 2026

Waythrough is an independent resource, not DHA. We summarize public information to help you get oriented. For applications and the most current waiting-list status, always use the official links below.

The Denver Housing Authority, formally the Housing Authority of the City and County of Denver, is a quasi-municipal corporation that has provided affordable housing in Denver since 1938 and now serves over 31,100 people through more than 13,000 units and Housing Choice Vouchers. The single most important thing to understand about applying here is that DHA does not run a conventional Section 8 waiting list at all. It runs a lottery, once a year, and if you miss the registration window you wait a year for the next one. The 2027 lottery has already run: DHA's lottery page reads "The lottery closed at 11:59 p.m. on Friday, September 18, 2026." Just as important, DHA says "Because of limited federal funding, DHA did not select names from the Housing Choice Voucher lottery in 2025 or 2026" and "Based on current funding, DHA may not be able to select names from the 2027 lottery" (checked October 15, 2026). In plain terms, nobody was drawn from the last two lotteries, and a 2027 lottery number may never be called. Keep your registration and your mailing address current, but do not count on the lottery alone. This page explains how the lottery works, what DHA says about the next one, the other doors open to Denver renters in the meantime, and who to call.

Programs DHA Runs

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8), awarded through an annual lottery
  • Subsidized and public housing at DHA properties
  • Project-Based Vouchers
  • Mixed-income and affordable developments
  • Homebuyer education
  • Youth and family services, including the Youth Employment Academy

How to Apply

Apply directly through the authority's official application page: www.denverhousing.org/housing-choice-vouchers-section8/. If you have already applied, call (720) 932-3023 to verify your position and keep your contact information current — if the authority cannot reach you when your name comes up, you can be dropped from the list.

New to the process? Our step-by-step guide to applying for Section 8 walks through the whole thing, and how to find your PHA helps you locate the right authority.

Other Authorities Serving This Area

  • The lottery is a registration, not an application, and it does not guarantee anything. DHA is explicit: "Lottery registration does not guarantee that your number will be drawn. The amount of lottery numbers drawn depends on available funding." After registration closes, "throughout the following year, as funds are available, DHA will randomly select lottery numbers via a computerized random selection process," and selected households are notified by mail within 120 days of a drawing. There are no scheduled drawing dates: DHA says "lottery numbers are drawn as funds are available, so, there are no specific dates."
  • Register once, and get the Social Security number right. DHA warns that duplicate entries disqualify every entry from that household, and that you should "be sure to enter your Social Security number correctly, you will need your Social Security to check your lottery status later." Registration takes under ten minutes and asks for name, Social Security number, date of birth, family size, income range and mailing address. Results are checked back on DHA's site using that same Social Security number, and DHA tells registrants to "print your confirmation page or make note of your lottery number."
  • Results lag by months, and funding decides everything. DHA's lottery results page still covers "the lottery held in September 2025 only," and states that "Information for the lottery held in September 2026 is expected to be available in January 2027" (checked October 15, 2026). DHA's main voucher page still carries an older line about the September 2024 lottery; the results page is the current one. So if you registered in September, do not read the silence through the autumn as a rejection. But be realistic about what January can bring: DHA says it "did not select names from the Housing Choice Voucher lottery in 2025 or 2026" and "may not be able to select names from the 2027 lottery." Whether anyone is drawn depends on federal funding, not on how long you have waited.
  • Do not wait on the lottery alone: the other doors. The Colorado Division of Housing runs state voucher programs through local partners, but on its program list (doh.colorado.gov/housing-voucher-programs) its general Housing Choice Voucher program reads "Program Status: Pause" (checked October 15, 2026). The state programs listed as open work by referral: VASH for homeless veterans, through Colorado VA Medical Centers; Community Access Team Vouchers for people with disabilities leaving or at risk of nursing homes and other institutions; Section 811 for people with significant, long-term disabilities; and Project Based Assistance. Permanent Supportive Housing for people experiencing homelessness with a disability "Varies by CoC" and is reached through Coordinated Entry. If rent is the emergency, Denver's Temporary Rental and Utility Assistance (TRUA) program says it "anticipates reopening for new applications at 10 a.m. on Tuesday, Oct. 20 and closing at 9:59 a.m. on Wednesday, Oct. 21," and after that opens on the third Tuesday of each month, followed by a random selection. You need a current Demand for Rent from your landlord, a Denver County Court summons or other proof of an active eviction case, and households that received TRUA in 2025 cannot receive it in 2026. The state's Colorado Emergency Rental Assistance opens its next Monthly Random Selection from Wednesday, November 4, 2026 at 10:00 a.m. to Friday, November 6, 2026 at 5:00 p.m. The CARE Center, (303) 838-1200 by call or text, Monday to Friday 8:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m., helps with both. For shelter, food and everything else, dial 2-1-1, which the Division of Housing says connects "people with emergency shelter, food, rental assistance, healthcare, and more." And DHA's own properties with site-based waiting lists, listed above, are a door the lottery does not close.
  • DHA has two offices and a separate walk-in location for vouchers. The central office is at 1035 Osage St., open Monday through Friday 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. The HCV and Section 8 walk-in office is at 1401 Mariposa Street, open Tuesday through Friday 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Mail goes to PO Box 40305, Santa Fe Drive Station, Denver, CO 80204. Portability questions have their own line at (720) 932-3220 and mailbox DHAPorts@denverhousing.org.
  • The Housing Authority of the City of Aurora is a separate agency next door. It serves Aurora, runs its own vouchers and affordable communities, and states that it "currently serves 2,000 households through voucher programs and 1,620 residents that reside in our affordable rental communities." Its main line is (720) 251-2100 and its office is at 2280 S Xanadu Way. A Denver lottery registration does not put you on Aurora's lists.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get on the Denver Section 8 waiting list?

You do not, because there is not one. DHA awards Housing Choice Vouchers through an annual online lottery instead of an open waiting list. You register during a short window once a year, and DHA then draws numbers at random through the following year as funding allows. The window for the 2027 lottery was September 17 and 18, 2026, and it is closed: DHA says "The lottery closed at 11:59 p.m. on Friday, September 18, 2026" (checked October 15, 2026). DHA's entry form says registration for the next one, the 2028 lottery, takes place "on the 3rd Thursday and Friday of September 2027." DHA announces each year's dates "in all local papers under the public notice section, the DHA phone system as well as our website," so watch denverhousing.org to confirm. DHA also warns that entries "do not carry over from year to year; persons must re-register each year," so a registration from a past year does not count. Be realistic about the odds: DHA says "Because of limited federal funding, DHA did not select names from the Housing Choice Voucher lottery in 2025 or 2026," and that it "may not be able to select names from the 2027 lottery."

When is the next Denver housing lottery?

DHA's entry form points to September 2027. It reads "The 2028 Lottery is Currently Closed" and says registration takes place "on the 3rd Thursday and Friday of September 2027," which would be September 16 and 17, 2027 (checked October 15, 2026). DHA says the dates "change yearly" and are announced "in all local papers under the public notice section, the DHA phone system as well as our website," so confirm them when that notice runs. The most recent lottery, for 2027, ran for 48 hours on September 17 and 18, 2026, and DHA's lottery page says "The lottery closed at 11:59 p.m. on Friday, September 18, 2026." When the next one opens, DHA says entries "MUST be submitted on-line (through the internet) using ANY computer, tablet, or smart phone that has Internet access," and that "Only one computer entry can be submitted per household." Have this ready before you start: "Name, social security number, date of birth, family size, income range and mailing address." DHA says the process "takes less than 10 minutes" and tells you to "Be sure to enter your Social Security number correctly" and to "print your confirmation page or make note of your lottery number." You must be "18 years of age or older" and "A U.S. citizen or eligible immigrant." Never pay anyone to enter: DHA says "NEVER pay money to apply for housing!"

Does winning the lottery mean I get a voucher?

No, and DHA says so directly: "Lottery registration does not guarantee that your number will be drawn. The amount of lottery numbers drawn depends on available funding." Right now DHA is not drawing at all: it says it "did not select names from the Housing Choice Voucher lottery in 2025 or 2026" and "may not be able to select names from the 2027 lottery" (checked October 15, 2026). If drawings resume, being drawn means DHA contacts you to begin eligibility screening. Households selected are notified by mail within 120 days of a drawing, and drawings happen through the year as funds allow rather than on set dates. Keep your mailing address current with DHA, because a letter you never receive is the most common way a drawn number goes to waste. If you move, DHA asks you to send a letter to "Denver Housing Authority, Attn: Housing Choice Voucher Lottery, 1401 Mariposa St., Denver, CO 80204" with your new contact information, your lottery number if you know it, and the last 4 digits of your Social Security number.

I registered. How do I check my lottery status?

Through DHA's lottery results page, using the Social Security number you entered on the application. Expect a wait: checked October 15, 2026, that page still covers "the lottery held in September 2025 only," and DHA says "information for the lottery held in September 2026 is expected to be available in January 2027." DHA's main voucher page still shows an older note about the September 2024 lottery; go by the results page. Also note DHA's warning that duplicate entries disqualify all entries from a household, so if you think someone in your household also registered, call (720) 932-3023 rather than registering again.

Is there anything open in Denver besides the lottery?

Not through DHA's central lists, but DHA's own properties are worth calling. DHA's wait-list page read "There are no currently open lottery or pre-application lists" when we checked on October 15, 2026, and its availability page lists Section 8 Housing Choice Vouchers as "Apply when lottery is open." DHA's lottery page points elsewhere: "For other housing options, explore DHA properties with site-based waiting lists and apply online." On the availability page, Shoshone and Studebaker Lofts read "Available to All," Pancratia Hall Lofts takes applicants who meet income and household size requirements, several properties say to contact the office for status, and Benedict Park Place says applications "can be picked up at 305 Park Ave West, Denver, Co 80205." Call the number listed for the property, or the Occupancy department at (720) 932-3020, to ask what is actually accepting applications.

What should I do while I wait for the Denver lottery?

Apply for everything else you qualify for, because DHA says it "may not be able to select names from the 2027 lottery." First, DHA properties with site-based waiting lists: call the numbers on DHA's availability page or Occupancy at (720) 932-3020. Second, the Colorado Division of Housing: its general state Housing Choice Voucher program reads "Program Status: Pause" (checked October 15, 2026), but VASH for homeless veterans, Community Access Team Vouchers, Section 811 and Project Based Assistance are listed as open by referral, and Permanent Supportive Housing is reached through Coordinated Entry. Third, if you are behind on rent, Denver's Temporary Rental and Utility Assistance (TRUA) program expects to take applications from 10 a.m. on Tuesday, October 20 to 9:59 a.m. on Wednesday, October 21, and then on the third Tuesday of each month, and you need a current Demand for Rent or proof of an active eviction case. The state's Colorado Emergency Rental Assistance opens its next Monthly Random Selection from November 4, 2026 at 10:00 a.m. to November 6, 2026 at 5:00 p.m. The CARE Center at (303) 838-1200 helps with both. Fourth, dial 2-1-1 for shelter, food and other help. Finally, keep your DHA mailing address current, and watch for the 2028 lottery, which DHA's entry form says registers on the 3rd Thursday and Friday of September 2027.

What number should I call at DHA?

It depends on what you need, and calling the wrong one costs you a day. Housing Choice Voucher questions go to (720) 932-3023 or (720) 932-3168, or section8@denverhousing.org. Housing applications and the subsidized lists go to Occupancy at (720) 932-3020 or frontdoor@denverhousing.org. Portability, meaning moving a voucher in or out of Denver, is (720) 932-3220. Housing Management is (720) 932-3100, and Resident and Community Connections is (720) 932-3053. All of these were on DHA's contact page when we checked on October 15, 2026.
