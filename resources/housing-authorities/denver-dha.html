Housing Authority

Denver Housing Authority (DHA)

Serves the City and County of Denver, Colorado · Last checked August 25, 2026

Waythrough is an independent resource, not DHA. We summarize public information to help you get oriented. For applications and the most current waiting-list status, always use the official links below.

The Denver Housing Authority, formally the Housing Authority of the City and County of Denver, is a quasi-municipal corporation that has provided affordable housing in Denver since 1938 and now serves over 31,100 people through more than 13,000 units and Housing Choice Vouchers. The single most important thing to understand about applying here is that DHA does not run a conventional Section 8 waiting list at all. It runs a lottery, once a year, and if you miss the registration window you wait a year for the next one. As of August 25, 2026 that window has not opened yet: DHA's entry page states "The 2027 Lottery is Currently Closed" and says registration takes place on "the 3rd Thursday and Friday of September 2026," which is only a few weeks away. This page explains how the lottery works, what else is open, and who to call.

Waiting-List Status

Waiting lists open and close frequently, so treat the status below as a starting point and confirm on the official site before you rely on it.

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8): Closed now, annual lottery registration expected in September 2026 — There is no ongoing waiting list to join. DHA states: "Once annually the Denver Housing Authority holds an online lottery process on our website for interested applicants, 18 years of age and older." As of August 25, 2026 its entry page reads "The 2027 Lottery is Currently Closed" and states that registration takes place on "the 3rd Thursday and Friday of September 2026." On the calendar that points to September 17 and 18, 2026, but DHA publishes the rule rather than the dates, and it also says "the dates of the lottery change yearly; however, these dates are announced in all local papers under the public notice section, the DHA phone system as well as our website." Confirm on denverhousing.org before you rely on those two days.
  • Subsidized and public housing waiting lists: Closed — Closed (checked August 25, 2026). DHA's wait-list page reads: "There are no currently open lottery or pre-application lists." When they do open, DHA says "a public notice will run in four local newspapers in English and Spanish. (Denver Post, El Semanario, Denver Weekly and La Voz)." DHA describes three subsidized waiting lists organized by bedroom size and says "you may submit interest cards for all three waiting lists," and that reaching the top of a list typically takes 6 to 12 months once you are on it.
  • Individual DHA properties: Varies by property — DHA's availability page lists properties with their own status, several of which read "contact office for status" rather than open or closed, and at least one, Benedict Park Place, where "applications can be picked up at 305 Park Ave West, Denver, Co 80205." Because this is property by property and changes without announcement, call the Occupancy department at (720) 932-3020 to ask what is actually taking applications this week rather than working from the page alone.

Programs DHA Runs

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8), awarded through an annual lottery
  • Subsidized and public housing at DHA properties
  • Project-Based Vouchers
  • Mixed-income and affordable developments
  • Homebuyer education
  • Youth and family services, including the Youth Employment Academy

How to Apply

Apply directly through the authority's official application page: www.denverhousing.org/housing-choice-vouchers-section8/. If you have already applied, call (720) 932-3023 to verify your position and keep your contact information current — if the authority cannot reach you when your name comes up, you can be dropped from the list.

Other Authorities Serving This Area

  • The lottery is a registration, not an application, and it does not guarantee anything. DHA is explicit: "Lottery registration does not guarantee that your number will be drawn. The amount of lottery numbers drawn depends on available funding." After registration closes, "throughout the following year, as funds are available, DHA will randomly select lottery numbers via a computerized random selection process," and selected households are notified by mail within 120 days of a drawing. There are no scheduled drawing dates: DHA says "lottery numbers are drawn as funds are available, so, there are no specific dates."
  • Register once, and get the Social Security number right. DHA warns that duplicate entries disqualify every entry from that household, and that you should "be sure to enter your Social Security number correctly, you will need your Social Security to check your lottery status later." Registration takes under ten minutes and asks for name, Social Security number, date of birth, family size, income range and mailing address. Results are checked back on DHA's site using that same Social Security number.
  • Results lag by months, and that is normal. DHA's lottery results page currently covers "the lottery held in September 2025 only," and states that "information for the lottery held in September 2026 is expected to be available in January 2027." So if you register this September, do not read the silence through the autumn as a rejection.
  • DHA has two offices and a separate walk-in location for vouchers. The central office is at 1035 Osage St., open Monday through Friday 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. The HCV and Section 8 walk-in office is at 1401 Mariposa Street, open Tuesday through Friday 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Mail goes to PO Box 40305, Santa Fe Drive Station, Denver, CO 80204. Portability questions have their own line at (720) 932-3220 and mailbox DHAPorts@denverhousing.org.
  • The Housing Authority of the City of Aurora is a separate agency next door. It serves Aurora, runs its own vouchers and affordable communities, and states that it "currently serves 2,000 households through voucher programs and 1,620 residents that reside in our affordable rental communities." Its main line is (720) 251-2100 and its office is at 2280 S Xanadu Way. A Denver lottery registration does not put you on Aurora's lists.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get on the Denver Section 8 waiting list?

You do not, because there is not one. DHA awards Housing Choice Vouchers through an annual online lottery instead of an open waiting list. You register during a short window once a year, and DHA then draws numbers at random through the following year as funding allows. As of August 25, 2026 the entry page reads "The 2027 Lottery is Currently Closed" and says registration takes place on "the 3rd Thursday and Friday of September 2026," which points to September 17 and 18. Check denverhousing.org in early September to confirm the exact days.

When is the next Denver housing lottery?

DHA states that the 2027 lottery registration takes place on "the 3rd Thursday and Friday of September 2026." That is September 17 and 18, 2026 on the calendar, though DHA publishes the rule rather than the two dates, and adds that "the dates of the lottery change yearly; however, these dates are announced in all local papers under the public notice section, the DHA phone system as well as our website." If you want one reminder to set this year, set it for the first week of September and check DHA's site then. The window is two days, and the next one after that is a year later.

Does winning the lottery mean I get a voucher?

No, and DHA says so directly: "Lottery registration does not guarantee that your number will be drawn. The amount of lottery numbers drawn depends on available funding." Being drawn means DHA contacts you to begin eligibility screening. Households selected are notified by mail within 120 days of a drawing, and drawings happen through the year as funds allow rather than on set dates. Keep your mailing address current with DHA, because a letter you never receive is the most common way a drawn number goes to waste.

I registered. How do I check my lottery status?

Through DHA's lottery results page, using the Social Security number you entered on the application. Expect a wait: as of August 25, 2026 the page covers "the lottery held in September 2025 only," and DHA says "information for the lottery held in September 2026 is expected to be available in January 2027." Also note DHA's warning that duplicate entries disqualify all entries from a household, so if you think someone in your household also registered, call (720) 932-3023 rather than registering again.

Is there anything open in Denver besides the lottery?

Not much right now, but it is worth checking rather than assuming. DHA's wait-list page reads "there are no currently open lottery or pre-application lists" as of August 25, 2026. Its property availability page is more mixed: several DHA properties say to contact the office for status, and Benedict Park Place says applications "can be picked up at 305 Park Ave West, Denver, Co 80205." The Occupancy department at (720) 932-3020 is the right number to ask what is actually accepting applications.

What number should I call at DHA?

It depends on what you need, and calling the wrong one costs you a day. Housing Choice Voucher questions go to (720) 932-3023 or (720) 932-3168, or section8@denverhousing.org. Housing applications and the subsidized lists go to Occupancy at (720) 932-3020 or frontdoor@denverhousing.org. Portability, meaning moving a voucher in or out of Denver, is (720) 932-3220. Housing Management is (720) 932-3100, and Resident and Community Connections is (720) 932-3053.
