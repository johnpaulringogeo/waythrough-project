Affordable Housing in Houston, TX

Waythrough Project · Updated November 2026

This page collects the specific programs, agencies, phone numbers, and rules that apply in Houston and Harris County — not generic Section 8 advice. Housing Alliance HTX's Housing Choice Voucher waitlist is closed, and its 2026 public housing lottery closed on July 7, so the realistic short-term path is one-time help through 211, Harris County, and the neighborhood ministries below. Texas eviction law also changed on January 1, 2026 (SB 38): the notice to vacate is 3 days, and a summary disposition motion gives you only 4 days to respond, so Lone Star Legal Aid speed-dial matters. Section 8 in Houston: HHA and HCHA Status

Section 8 Housing Choice Vouchers in the Houston area are administered by two separate authorities. Current status (checked November 3, 2026):

  • Housing Alliance HTX (formerly the Houston Housing Authority, HHA) — serves the City of Houston. HCV waitlist is closed: in June 2026 HHA said "The Housing Choice Voucher (HCV) waitlist remains closed, and no new HCV applications will be accepted," and no reopening has been announced. HHA periodically re-registers existing waitlist members; if you're already on the list, watch your email and mail. Failure to re-register removes you from the list
  • Harris County Housing Authority (HCHA) — serves unincorporated Harris County and contracting municipalities outside Houston city limits. HCHA's waiting-list page announces no open list, and says it has no anticipated date for contacting applicants on its 2016 waiting list. (713) 578-2100 · hchatexas.org
  • Public Housing waitlist: separate from HCV. HHA's public housing lottery for eight properties closed on July 7, 2026, after about 56,000 people applied; applicants are notified through their RentCafe account
  • Specialty referrals still accepted: HUD-VASH (veterans), Family Unification Program (FUP), Mainstream vouchers for non-elderly people with disabilities
  • Other Texas authorities you can apply to simultaneously: Pasadena Housing Authority, Pasadena Second Ward HA, Galveston Housing Authority, the Texas Department of Housing and Community Affairs statewide voucher program
  • Contact: Housing Alliance HTX (713) 260-0500 · alliancehtx.org

Texas Rent Relief (TRR) has ended

The Texas Department of Housing and Community Affairs says the Texas Rent Relief Program and the Texas Eviction Diversion Program "launched in February 2021 and closed in the summer of 2023." Tenant Rights in Texas (and Recent Changes)

Texas has one of the more landlord-friendly legal frameworks in the country. SB 38, which took effect on January 1, 2026, made evictions even faster. Houston tenants are covered by the Texas Property Code Chapters 24, 92, and 301:

  • Texas SB 38 sped up evictions (effective January 1, 2026): if the landlord files a motion for summary disposition, you have until the 4th day after you are served to file a written response, or the judge can rule without a trial (Texas Property Code § 24.005106). Contact Lone Star Legal Aid immediately
  • 3-day notice to vacate for nonpayment (Texas Property Code § 24.005) — among the shortest in the country. Once you get one, act immediately. See how to avoid eviction
  • No source-of-income protection — cities preempted: Texas SB 267 (2015) prohibits cities from passing local source-of-income ordinances. Houston cannot enact one. A landlord can lawfully decline to accept a voucher
  • Security deposit return: 30 days after move-out with itemized deductions (TX § 92.103). Texas does not cap deposits
  • Warranty of habitability and right to repair — TX Property Code Chapter 92 requires diligent effort to repair conditions materially affecting health or safety after written notice. Don't withhold rent without legal advice
  • Notice to end month-to-month: one full rental period (usually 30 days), from either side
  • Retaliatory eviction is illegal (TX § 92.331) — within the prior 6 months of a tenant's good-faith complaint or organizing
  • Self-help eviction is illegal (TX § 92.0081) — landlords cannot change locks, shut off utilities, or remove belongings. They must go through Justice of the Peace court
  • Justice of the Peace court venue: Harris County has 16 JP precincts that hear evictions. The trial must be held between the 10th and 21st day after the petition is filed (§ 24.0051(g)). After a loss you have 5 days to appeal, and the writ of possession cannot issue before the 6th day after the judgment (§ 24.0061(b))
  • Fair housing: federal Fair Housing Act and TX Property Code Chapter 301 cover race, color, religion, sex, national origin, familial status, and disability

For free legal help: Lone Star Legal Aid — call or text "APPLY" to its eviction line, 361-35EVICT (361-353-8428), or call the main office at (713) 652-0077 or (800) 733-8394. For state-level details, see our Texas housing resources.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Houston HHA Section 8 waitlist open in 2026?

No. The Housing Alliance HTX (formerly Houston Housing Authority, HHA) Housing Choice Voucher waitlist is closed: in June 2026 HHA said the HCV waitlist remains closed and no new HCV applications will be accepted, and no reopening had been announced as of November 3, 2026. Its public housing lottery for eight properties closed on July 7, 2026. HHA periodically re-registers existing waitlist members — failure to re-register removes you from the list, so watch your email and mail. The Harris County Housing Authority (HCHA) runs separate lists and announces no open list. Specialty referrals for HUD-VASH (veterans) and FUP continue. Call HHA at (713) 260-0500.

What rental assistance is available in Houston right now?

Less than before, and the biggest programs are closed. Catholic Charities' Harris County rental assistance says its application is currently closed (checked November 3, 2026). Dial 211 and ask about Harris County's Community Resilience Rental Assistance Program. West Houston Assistance Ministries helps renters in nine west Houston ZIP codes by phone appointment on Mondays at 10 a.m. at (713) 780-2727, ext. 200, and Memorial Assistance Ministries takes people in person at 1625 Blalock Rd. ((713) 468-4516). BakerRipley's electric and gas bill help is closed and expected to reopen Monday, November 9 (checked November 4, 2026); if that date has passed, check BakerRipley's Utility Assistance page. Its hotline is (713) 590-2327.

Where can I get emergency shelter tonight in Houston?

Star of Hope Mission ((713) 222-2220) is Houston's largest shelter system with men's, women's, and family centers. Salvation Army Family Residence ((713) 650-6530), SEARCH Homeless Services ((713) 739-7752, day shelter), Magnificat Houses ((713) 655-4000), and The Beacon ((713) 236-0643, day services) round out the system. Covenant House Texas ((713) 523-7900) serves youth 18-24. The Houston Area Women's Center ((713) 528-7273) has a 24-hour DV crisis line. Call 211 to route through the Coalition for the Homeless Coordinated Access.

Can a Houston landlord refuse Section 8?

Yes. Texas has no statewide source-of-income protection, and Texas SB 267 (2015) prohibits cities like Houston from passing local ordinances requiring landlords to accept vouchers. Finding a landlord who already participates is part of the work — HHA can provide a list of participating landlords.

What is Texas SB 38 and how does it affect Houston tenants?

Texas SB 38 took effect on January 1, 2026, and sped up the eviction process. The landlord still gives a 3-day written notice to vacate (Texas Property Code § 24.005) unless the lease says otherwise. If the landlord files a motion for summary disposition, you must file a written response by the 4th day after you are served, or the judge can rule without a trial. Harris County's 16 Justice of the Peace courts hear evictions, and the trial must be held between the 10th and 21st day after filing. If you're served with eviction papers in Houston, call or text Lone Star Legal Aid's eviction line at 361-353-8428 immediately — its lawyers work in all 16 Harris County JP courts.
