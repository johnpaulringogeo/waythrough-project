Affordable Housing in Tulsa, OK

Waythrough Project · Updated November 2026

This page collects the specific programs, agencies, phone numbers, and rules that apply in Tulsa and Tulsa County — not generic Section 8 advice. THA publishes no open or closed status, and the status system it points applicants to showed no open waiting lists on September 9, 2026, so the realistic short-term path is one-time help through Restore Hope, MHAOK, and the named shelters and Continuum-of-Care intake at Housing Solutions Tulsa. Tulsa is one of the fastest-eviction cities in the country — Oklahoma's 5-day pay-or-quit notice plus FED trial within 5–10 days means same-day legal aid contact is critical if you're served.

Section 8 in Tulsa: THA Status

Section 8 Housing Choice Vouchers in Tulsa are administered by the Tulsa Housing Authority (THA). Current status (checked September 9, 2026):

  • THA does not publish an open or closed status — neither its Apply page nor its Section 8 page says either. What it does publish is where to check, on its Application Instructions page: "Please do not call THA to check your status. You may check the status of your application at www.waitlistcheck.com."
  • As of September 9, 2026, the status system THA directs applicants to showed no open waiting lists. Both THA listings on waitlistcheck.com read "There are no open waiting lists at this time." Because THA itself designates that system, this is its operator portal rather than an outside source — but THA does not say "closed" in its own voice, so neither do we
  • If you already applied: THA contacts selected households directly. Estimated wait once on the list: 12 to 36 months. Watch your email and mail, and check waitlistcheck.com rather than calling
  • River West Section 8 waitlist: THA also closed the River West project-based waitlist in 2024 due to backlog. Some other project-based developments maintain separate waitlists — check tulsahousing.org for current openings
  • Specialty referrals still accepted: HUD-VASH (veterans, through the Eastern Oklahoma VA Health Care System), Family Unification Program (FUP), Mainstream vouchers for non-elderly people with disabilities
  • Public Housing: THA manages public housing communities across Tulsa with separate waitlists — check tulsahousing.org/applyforhousing
  • Apply to neighboring authorities too: Sand Springs Housing Authority, Sapulpa Housing Authority, Broken Arrow has its own programs, and the Oklahoma Housing Finance Agency (OHFA) runs a statewide voucher program for areas without a local PHA
  • Contact: THA (918) 582-0021 · tulsahousing.org

The pandemic ERA dollars have ended

The federal Emergency Rental Assistance Program (ERA1/ERA2) money that flowed through the City of Tulsa, Tulsa County, and Oklahoma during 2021-2023 has been fully spent. Don't waste time on closed pandemic-era portals. Current paths are Restore Hope, the Social Services Hub at Iron Gate, and 211.

Tenant Rights in Oklahoma (Applies in Tulsa)

Oklahoma has one of the more landlord-friendly legal frameworks in the country. Tulsa tenants are covered by the Oklahoma Residential Landlord and Tenant Act (ORLTA), 41 O.S. § 101 et seq.:

  • 5-day notice to pay rent or quit (41 O.S. § 131(B)) — among the shortest in the country. If you do not pay within 5 days of the landlord's written demand, the landlord can end the lease
  • 15-day notice for non-payment lease violations (41 O.S. § 132) — with a 10-day cure period for most curable violations. Repeat violations can shorten the timeline
  • Eviction process: Forcible Entry and Detainer (FED) cases are filed in Tulsa County District Court. The summons sets the trial 5 to 10 days after it is issued, and you must be served at least 3 days before it (12 O.S. §§ 1148.4, 1148.5). A motion for a new trial must be filed within 3 days, and after a judgment the landlord or officer must give 48 hours' notice before you are removed (12 O.S. §§ 1148.10, 1148.10A). Restore Hope says all eviction hearings are currently held at the Family Center for Juvenile Justice
  • Security deposit: Oklahoma does not cap the deposit amount. The landlord must return the deposit (or written itemized deductions) within 45 days of move-out and a written demand from the tenant (41 O.S. § 115)
  • No source-of-income protection: Oklahoma does not include source of income as a protected class under state fair housing law, and Tulsa does not have a local source-of-income ordinance. A landlord can lawfully decline to accept a Section 8 voucher. Finding a landlord who already participates is part of the work — THA can provide a list of participating landlords
  • No rent control: Oklahoma preempts cities from enacting rent control
  • Warranty of habitability (41 O.S. § 118) — landlords must comply with applicable building and housing codes affecting health and safety. After written notice and reasonable time, tenants may pursue remedies
  • Self-help eviction is illegal (41 O.S. § 123) — landlords cannot lock out, shut off utilities, or remove belongings without a court order and the sheriff. You can go to court to get back in, and recover up to twice the average monthly rent or twice your actual damages, whichever is more
  • Retaliatory eviction: Oklahoma has limited statutory anti-retaliation protection but it is weaker than many states. Document any complaints in writing and with dated photos
  • Fair housing: federal Fair Housing Act and Oklahoma Civil Rights Commission Act cover race, color, religion, sex, national origin, familial status, and disability (but NOT source of income)

For free legal help: Legal Aid Services of Oklahoma — Tulsa Office at 918-584-3338, or statewide intake at 1-888-534-5243 (Monday to Thursday, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m.). Its Right to Counsel program gives free help with no income limit in ZIP codes 74105, 74133, 74135, 74136 and 74145: (918) 984-4636. Tulsa County Bar Association Lawyer Referral & Legal Navigator (not free) at 918-587-6014. Oklahoma Legal Aid's oklaw.org has free self-help forms and guides. If You're Facing an Eviction Tomorrow

  1. Do not skip court. Oklahoma eviction hearings are set 5 to 10 days after the summons is issued. If you do not show up, the landlord usually wins by default
  2. Bring proof of any payments — receipts, money order stubs, bank records, screenshots of Zelle or Cash App transfers
  3. Call Legal Aid Services of Oklahoma the same day at 1-888-534-5243 (or (918) 984-4636 in the Right to Counsel ZIP codes) — they may be able to negotiate with the landlord before the case is even filed
  4. Apply for emergency rental assistance immediately — call Restore Hope at 918-922-8282, or go to the Social Services Hub at Iron Gate (Tuesdays and Thursdays, 1 to 4 p.m.) if you already have a court date
  5. If conditions are the issue (no heat, no hot water, pests, mold), document with dated photos and a written notice; the warranty of habitability is one of the few tenant defenses in Oklahoma

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Tulsa Section 8 waitlist open in 2026?

The Tulsa Housing Authority (THA) does not publish an open or closed status — neither its Apply page nor its Section 8 page says either way. What THA does publish is where to look: "Please do not call THA to check your status. You may check the status of your application at www.waitlistcheck.com." As of September 9, 2026, both THA listings on that system read "There are no open waiting lists at this time." Since THA designates waitlistcheck itself, that is its operator portal, not an outside source — but THA never says "closed" in its own voice, so we report the snapshot and its date rather than putting a word in THA's mouth. A caution if you find THA's waitlist-closure page: it announces a closure "on September 18" with no year printed. That is September 18, 2024 — the page's own metadata dates it to September 2024, THA's newsroom carries no waitlist item since, and September 18 fell on a Wednesday in 2024, as the page says, but on a Friday in 2026. It is not an upcoming deadline. Estimated wait once on a list is 12 to 36 months. Call THA at (918) 582-0021. Specialty referrals for HUD-VASH (veterans) and FUP continue. The Oklahoma Housing Finance Agency (OHFA) runs a statewide program, though it publishes no county coverage map — check with OHFA directly rather than assuming it covers the Tulsa suburbs.

What rental assistance is available in Tulsa right now?

Restore Hope Ministries is the main door for people who live in Tulsa County: call its rent intake line at 918-922-8282, Monday through Thursday, 9 a.m. to noon. It says the line closes from November 23 to December 6 and from December 21 to January 3 (checked November 5, 2026). If you already have an eviction case, go to the Social Services Hub at Iron Gate, 501 W. Archer St., on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 1 to 4 p.m. Mental Health Association Oklahoma offers mediation, not rent money. For heating bills, Oklahoma Human Services expects LIHEAP winter enrollment to open on December 15, 2026; call (405) 522-5050. Dial 211 for other help.

Where can I get emergency shelter tonight in Tulsa?

The Tulsa Day Center at 415 W. Archer St. ((918) 583-5588) is the central day-services hub for adults. John 3:16 Mission ((918) 587-1186) is the largest faith-based men's shelter with New Beginnings for women and children. Salvation Army Center of Hope ((918) 582-7201) serves individuals and families. MHAOK's Denver House drop-in center supports adults experiencing mental illness. Call 211 to access Housing Solutions Tulsa Coordinated Entry.

Can a Tulsa landlord refuse Section 8?

Yes. Oklahoma does not include source of income as a protected class under state fair housing law, and Tulsa has not enacted a local source-of-income ordinance. A landlord can legally refuse to accept a Housing Choice Voucher in Tulsa. THA can provide a list of landlords who already participate in the voucher program.

How fast can a Tulsa eviction move?

Very fast. Under 41 O.S. § 131(B), the landlord can end your lease if you do not pay within 5 days of a written demand for the rent. After the case is filed in Tulsa County District Court, the hearing is set 5 to 10 days after the summons is issued, and you must get the summons at least 3 days before. After a judgment, the landlord or officer must give 48 hours' notice before you are removed. Call Legal Aid Services of Oklahoma at 1-888-534-5243 right away, or (918) 984-4636 if you live in ZIP code 74105, 74133, 74135, 74136 or 74145.
