Affordable Housing in Wichita, KS

Waythrough Project · Updated November 2026

This page lists the programs, agencies, phone numbers and rules that apply in Wichita and Sedgwick County. Three things to know first: the Wichita Housing Authority (WHA) is taking Section 8 pre-applications online right now, and its portal shows the voucher list open since July 2020; the Center of Hope pays emergency rent and utility bills for people facing eviction or a shutoff, by phone intake on weekday mornings; and Kansas law lets a landlord end your lease 3 days after a written notice for unpaid rent, so call the day you get one. Emergency Help Tonight in Wichita

If you need a safe place to sleep tonight or could lose your home in the next few days, start with these:

  • United Way of the Plains 211: dial 2-1-1, text your ZIP code to 898-211, or search 211Kansas.org. Calls are answered Monday through Friday, 7 a.m. to 7 p.m.; after hours, a recording points you to resources. It is also how Wichita's homeless service providers take referrals.
  • Wichita Family Crisis Center: emergency shelter for people fleeing domestic or sexual violence is reached through its 24-hour hotline, 316-267-SAFE (316-267-7233). wichitafamilycrisiscenter.org
  • Already homeless: the City's Rapid Re-Housing program pays rent and utilities so people who are homeless can move into permanent housing, but only by referral from agencies such as the Wichita Police Homeless Outreach Team, United Methodist Open Door, The Salvation Army, Catholic Charities or HumanKind Ministries. Call 211 to be connected.
  • 911 if you are in danger right now.

For a full walkthrough of finding shelter the first night, see our emergency housing tonight guide.

Section 8 in Wichita: WHA Status and How to Apply

In Wichita, Section 8 Housing Choice Vouchers, project-based vouchers and public housing are run by the Wichita Housing Authority (WHA), part of the City's Housing & Community Services department. WHA says it "serves Sedgwick, Harvey (With the exception of the City of Newton), and Butler Counties." Current status (checked November 25, 2026):

  • Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8) list: open. The City's How to Apply page says: "Preliminary applications are being accepted online." WHA's applicant portal lists the Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8) waiting list under "Open Waiting List(s)" with a start date of July 22, 2020 and no end date.
  • Project-based vouchers for 5 or 6 bedrooms: open. WHA's notice says the list for families who qualify for a 5- or 6-bedroom voucher opened May 15, 2025 "and will remain open indefinitely." The homes are in South City, Northeast and Southwest Wichita. People already on the regular list can add this one by logging in and updating their application.
  • Public housing: WHA's portal shows no public housing list among its open lists, and the City is selling its 352 single-family public housing homes as part of a HUD-approved repositioning. Ask at 316-462-3700 before you count on public housing.
  • How to apply: online only, at wichita.myhousing.com (English or Spanish). Have Social Security cards, names and birth dates for everyone, and everyone's income ready, and write down your confirmation number. WHA says to submit only one application. If you need help, call 316-462-3721 at least 5 business days ahead; the same number arranges an interpreter.
  • Keeping your place: WHA notifies you by mail when your name comes up. If you do not answer or the letter comes back, your name is removed. Report a change of address, phone, income or family size within 10 business days, in writing.
  • WHA's office: 455 N. Main, 10th Floor, Wichita, KS 67202. Phone 316-462-3700, Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.; email wha@wichita.gov. The City says only people with appointments are seen by Housing staff, and there is a document drop box near the west entrance of City Hall.

Emergency Rental Assistance in Wichita (Named Programs)

Start here: our guide to emergency rent help in Wichita walks through each door: the Center of Hope, Kansas Legal Services, the court's Eviction Resolution Program, 211, LIEAP and Kansas's eviction deadlines.

Wichita has no City rent fund you apply to directly. These are the doors that are open:

  • Center of Hope: "The Center of Hope provides emergency rent and utility assistance to those in danger of being evicted from their homes or having their utilities shut off," for people in the Wichita area and the counties around it. Call 316-267-0222; it takes calls "from 9am to 12 noon Monday through Friday" and says to keep calling if the line is busy. It has Spanish-speaking staff. Have your photo ID, Social Security cards, eviction notice and lease, utility bills, rent receipts and 30 days of income for everyone ready for the phone interview (checked November 25, 2026). centerofhopeinc.org
  • Eviction Resolution Program (Sedgwick County District Court): it explains the court process and connects tenants and landlords to help, including mediation. Fill out the tenant intake form on the court's page or call 316-660-5659. dc18.org
  • Free legal help: Kansas Legal Services, 316-267-3975 or 1-800-723-6953. Its attorneys also give free advice at the Sedgwick County Courthouse, 525 N. Main, courtroom 5-5, on Wednesdays from 8:30 a.m. to 4 p.m., same-day and first come, first served.
  • Utility help: Kansas LIEAP opens November 30, 2026 and runs to March 31, 2027, through the DCF Self-Service Portal. The Center of Hope's Project DESERVE helps Evergy customers. Details are in our Wichita rent help guide

The pandemic rent money has ended

The pandemic-era emergency rental assistance is gone. The Kansas Housing Resources Corporation no longer lists it among its renter programs; its county directory now sends Sedgwick County renters looking for homelessness prevention help to United Way 211. The City of Wichita's old Housing Stability page now returns "page not found." If a flyer or website sends you to either one, call 211 instead.

Tenant Rights in Wichita & Kansas

Wichita follows the Kansas Residential Landlord and Tenant Act (K.S.A. 58-2540 to 58-2573) and the state's eviction statutes (K.S.A. 61-3801 to 61-3809). Here is what matters most:

  • Notice for unpaid rent: the landlord can end the lease if you do not pay within three days after a written notice, counted as "three consecutive 24-hour periods," with two more days if the notice was mailed (K.S.A. 58-2564(b)). For other lease violations, the notice gives you 14 days to fix the problem and ends the lease no sooner than 30 days after you receive it (K.S.A. 58-2564(a)). A month-to-month tenancy takes 30 days' written notice (K.S.A. 58-2570)
  • Court comes fast: the summons sets your court date "not less than three nor more than 14 days" after it is issued (K.S.A. 61-3805). Go. If you miss it, the landlord wins by default. If there is a trial, it must be held within 14 days of that first date, and the court must consider mediation (K.S.A. 61-3806, 61-3807)
  • Only 7 days to appeal the move-out order: "notice of appeal shall be filed within seven days after entry of judgment" (K.S.A. 61-3902), and an appeal does not stop the eviction unless you post a bond (K.S.A. 61-3904). The officer serving the writ of restitution must carry it out within 14 days of getting it (K.S.A. 61-3808)
  • Lockouts and shutoffs are illegal: if a landlord locks you out or cuts electric, gas, water or another essential service, you can get back in or end the lease and recover up to one and a half months' rent or your actual damages, whichever is more (K.S.A. 58-2563)
  • Retaliation: a landlord may not raise rent or cut services because you complained to a code agency or the landlord, or joined a tenant union (K.S.A. 58-2572). Wichita added its own retaliatory eviction ordinance in January 2024; complaints go to the City Clerk at RetaliatoryEvictions@wichita.gov
  • Security deposits: no more than one month's rent for an unfurnished home. The landlord must return what is owed within 14 days after figuring deductions and no later than 30 days after you move out and ask for it, or owe you one and a half times the amount wrongly kept (K.S.A. 58-2550)
  • Vouchers: neither Kansas law nor Wichita's Non-Discrimination Ordinance lists source of income as protected, and Senate Bill 391, passed over the Governor's veto in 2026, bars Kansas cities and counties from making landlords accept vouchers. So a Wichita landlord can refuse a voucher. Refusals because of race, disability, family status or another protected trait are still illegal; Wichita's ordinance also covers sexual orientation, gender identity, age and veteran status

For state-level details, see our Kansas housing resources. If you face discrimination, see how to file a housing discrimination complaint. An old eviction on your record? Kansas lets you ask the court to expunge a dismissed case, or a judgment after three years if you owe nothing on it (K.S.A. 61-3809).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Wichita Section 8 waitlist open right now?

Yes. On November 25, 2026, the City of Wichita said preliminary applications are being accepted online, and the Wichita Housing Authority portal listed the Housing Choice Voucher (Section 8) waiting list as open, with a start date of July 22, 2020. Apply once at wichita.myhousing.com. The project-based voucher list for families who need 5 or 6 bedrooms is also open. Call 316-462-3700 with questions.

Is there emergency rent help in Wichita?

Yes, but not from the City directly. The Center of Hope pays emergency rent and utility bills for people in danger of eviction or a shutoff in the Wichita area. Call 316-267-0222 between 9 a.m. and noon, Monday through Friday, and have your ID, Social Security cards, eviction notice, lease, bills and 30 days of income ready. Dial 211 for other agencies.

How much notice does a Wichita landlord have to give?

For unpaid rent, three days. Kansas law lets a landlord end the lease if you do not pay within three days after a written notice, counted as three full 24-hour periods, plus two days if it was mailed. For other lease violations you get 14 days to fix the problem, and a month-to-month tenancy takes 30 days' written notice to end.

Can I get a free lawyer for an eviction in Wichita?

You can ask. Call Kansas Legal Services at 316-267-3975 or 1-800-723-6953, or apply online at kansaslegalservices.org. Its attorneys also give free advice at the Sedgwick County Courthouse, 525 N. Main, courtroom 5-5, on Wednesdays from 8:30 a.m. to 4 p.m., first come, first served. Wichita has no right-to-counsel law, so call as soon as you get a notice.

How long do I have after an eviction judgment in Wichita?

Very little. Kansas law gives you seven days after the judgment to file a notice of appeal on the move-out order, and an appeal does not stop the eviction unless you post a bond. The officer must carry out the writ of restitution within 14 days of receiving it, and Kansas Legal Services says you may get as little as 24 hours to move. Get legal help before the court date.
