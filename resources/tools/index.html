Free Section 8 & Housing Tools

These tools are free and need no account. Use them to find out where to apply, what you may qualify for, and roughly what you would pay. The housing authority that runs your program always makes the final call, so check the numbers with them before you decide anything.

Section 8 Waiting List Tracker

Find Section 8 waiting lists that are open now, by state, with each housing authority's contact information.

Eligibility Screener

Answer a few questions about your income, household and situation to see which housing programs you may qualify for.

Know Your Numbers

Enter your income and household size to estimate your Section 8 rent and see where you fall against local income limits.

Fair Market Rent & Income Limits Lookup

Look up HUD's Fair Market Rents and income limits for your area — the numbers housing programs use to set rents and decide who qualifies.

Compare Housing Programs

See Section 8, public housing, tax-credit (LIHTC) apartments and other programs side by side: who qualifies, how rent is set, and how long the wait is.

Benefits Interaction Calculator

See how getting one benefit can change your eligibility for, and the amount you get from, your other programs.

Monthly Budget Calculator

Add up your income and bills to see where your money goes each month, including what you spend on housing.

Housing Letter Generator

Write a clear letter to ask for repairs, request a reasonable accommodation, get your deposit back, or appeal a decision.

Not sure which tool to start with? Where to Start asks a few questions and points you to the right one. To apply, you will need your local housing authority — find yours in our housing authority directory or read how to find your PHA.
