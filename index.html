About This Project

Waythrough Project was started by Matt, who spent years with the VA's HUD-VASH program helping veterans find housing. He kept meeting people who were doing everything right and still getting stuck, because what they needed to know was spread across dozens of agency websites and hard to read. He built this site to put it in one place.

Everything here is free. No ads, no courses, no paid version. If something here helps you take your next step, it did its job.
